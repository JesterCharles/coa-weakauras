Retail feel

Closest to Protection Warrior with a Guardian Druid flavor. Like a Prot Warrior, you fight in melee with Rage that fuels both defensive cooldowns and hard control: Mountain Hammer's stun ~ Shockwave, Bash's auto-attack stun ~ concussive procs. King of the Mountain's Strength-into-parry/dodge/haste is your main avoidance lever, with Stone Edge layering extra parry on top. Mountain Fury's frontal cone that hits several enemies and drags them toward you is the standout mechanical fingerprint. Nothing in retail does exactly this, but it plays like a self-contained gather-and-cleave tank moment. Earthen Avatar (damage up, damage taken down, attack speed up) is a Guardian-Druid-style Berserk/Barkskin cooldown. What it still lacks natively is an interrupt and a poison/disease cleanse, the latter only via the class kit's Grove Guardian. Protective Roar's missing-health group heal and Boon of the Lion's periodic fear/charm/sleep dispel are real utility from its own kit. It stays a self-sufficient melee off-tank-bruiser rather than a healer or full support.

Playstyle

A dual-wield / one-hand melee brawler who fuels almost everything off Rage, with Mana as a smaller secondary pool for a couple of utilities. Nearly every spec-defining tool lives in melee range: Rock Barrier, Quake, Mountain Fury, Mountain Hammer, Seismic Smash. Rage comes from more than swings. Quake and the Seismic family refund rage per target hit, avoiding attacks grants rage and Mountain Mover stacks (Spiritbound gives 5 Rage and shaves Seismic cooldowns on an avoid), and Terrasmash flings a rage-generating Geode off your off-hand, so you're rarely rage-starved. You spend it on the signature combo: Mountain Fury, a frontal cone that hits several enemies, pushes and pulls them and scales off your Stamina, followed by Quake, a cheap 2-charge AoE weapon+Nature hit on an 8-sec recharge, to cluster and blend a pack. Mountain Hammer is a thrown, stunning hammer that Hammer Time! makes cleave, and the passive Bash auto-attack stun adds hard control. Earth's Rage stacks build from Primal Rush and Quake, feeding Call of the Mountain at 5 stacks. Earthen Avatar is your big cooldown: more damage dealt, less damage taken, faster attack speed, and it feeds off Seismic and Quake damage via Earthmaker to come up sooner. Rock Barrier is your reactive armor/charge shield.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   Mountain Fury cones and clusters, then Quake cleaves them for cheap on two charges and an 8-second recharge. That pair swings teamfights and 1vX.
*   Mountain Hammer and Bash are hard stuns, King of the Mountain scales parry and dodge off Strength alongside Unyielding Form and Mountain Mover, and Earthen Avatar works on offense or defense while your own Seismic damage cools it down.
*   Rock Barrier's charge-based armor shield, Boon of the Turtle's armor and damage reduction, and heavy avoidance compound into a hard floor against physical focus fire.

Weaknesses

*   No interrupt anywhere, and no poison or disease cleanse without taking Grove Guardian's zone from the class kit. Native support is threat and body-blocking, plus Protective Roar's missing-health group heal and Boon of the Lion's periodic fear, charm and sleep dispel. Nothing answers a real interrupt check.
*   Rock Barrier is an armor shield and Boon of the Turtle is armor and damage reduction. Nothing in the kit reduces magic damage, so caster and DoT comps walk a cleaner path through your defenses.
*   No sustained self-heal and no gap-closer in the spec line. Past Protective Roar's group heal, recovery and mobility are class-kit purchases: Earthmother's Pendant, Wildheart, Primal Rush, Boulder Dash, all spending Ability Essence a tank wants elsewhere. Quake and Mountain Fury need the enemy already close.

Stat priority

Hit (to the special-attack and dual-wield white hit caps)›Expertise (until your attacks stop being dodged/parried)›Strength›Armor Penetration›Critical Strike›Haste

Rotations

Solo / questing

1) Open with Mountain Hammer (ranged stun opener), or close and swing to bank rage before contact. 2) On 2+ mobs, open with Mountain Fury to hit and cluster the pack in its cone. 3) Spend rage on Quake on cooldown (2 charges/8 sec) for AoE weapon+Nature cleave and more rage back per target hit. 4) Weave Seismic Smash and the Seismic family for line damage and extra rage, and lean on Bash's auto-attack stun and Mountain Hammer for control. 5) Keep Boon of the Turtle active for durable grinding. 6) There is no self-heal in this kit — rely on mitigation (Rock Barrier, Boon of the Turtle, avoidance) and out-of-combat regen between packs rather than a heal spell. 7) Rock Barrier reactively when you take a spike of physical damage. 8) Earthen Avatar as the big offensive/defensive cooldown on a dangerous pull, refreshed faster by your Seismic/Quake damage via Earthmaker.

Dungeon

1) Establish threat with your Seismic/Geode damage (Fury of the Earthmother boosts Geode threat) and hold aggro with sustained cleave. 2) Mountain Fury on trash pulls to hit and cluster adds in front of you; it functions as pseudo off-tank. 3) Keep Quake between attacks for AoE threat/damage and rage back per target. 4) Seismic Smash and the Seismic family for line damage and Aftershock/Thane's Rage generation. 5) Mountain Hammer to stun a dangerous caster add. This spec has no interrupt, so the stun is your lockout — Bash's auto-stun is a bonus. 6) Rock Barrier proactively before a known burst-damage phase, or reactively when you're taking hits. 7) Boon of the Turtle when the group is eating sustained physical damage. 8) Earthen Avatar as your emergency mitigation/damage cooldown under heavy focus, cooled faster by your Seismic damage. Note: no cleanse or ally heal. A dedicated healer must cover poison, disease and healing.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Dual resource. Rage is the primary engine, funding Mountain Fury, Rock Barrier, Mountain Hammer and the Seismic family. It's well-fed: Quake, the Seismic abilities, avoiding attacks via Spiritbound's 5 Rage per avoid, and Terrasmash's off-hand Geode all mint rage on top of swings. You rarely go rage-dry in a sustained fight. Mana is a small secondary pool for the spec's handful of mana-costed tools, and it matters far less here than in the other Primalist specs because Mountain King has no mana-hungry cleanse or dispel kit. The toolset is overwhelmingly rage-based. What gates you in a long fight is rage availability for Mountain Fury and Rock Barrier.**

Difficulty**Reactive play is enough to get by: auto-attack, drop Mountain Fury on a cluster, press Rock Barrier, Boon of the Turtle or Earthen Avatar when things get dicey. Higher up, Mountain Fury bundles the right targets before Quake, Seismic damage cools Earthen Avatar through Earthmaker so you can hold it for the exact CC-chain or burst window instead of panic-using it, Mountain Hammer's stun goes on the caster that matters, and avoidance-triggered Mountain Mover and Spiritbound rage is worth banking.**
