Primary stat

Resource

Retail feel

Closest to Mistweaver Monk in Fistweaving mode. The signature mechanic, melee auto-attacks triggering a free heal on nearby allies via Hammer of Life, is functionally identical to Mistweaver's Teachings of the Monastery/Spinning Crane Kick fistweaving loop. Stand in melee, autoattack, and your damage rotation IS your AoE healing rotation. Layer in a secondary combat-generated resource, Rage standing in for Mana/Chi timing windows, that gates your biggest heals: Tears of the Earthmother, Seismic Wave, Earthmother's Binding and the Seismic spenders. Add melee-hybrid passives that turn healing and swings into buffs (Grove Training's Aftershock proc, Battleweaver, Infusion of Neptulon, Nature's Fury's auto-attack mana return) and the comparison holds up mechanically, not just in vibe.

Playstyle

Grovekeeper is a dual-resource melee-healer. Mana fuels the heal and utility kit (Flourishing Growth, Sacred Grove, Spirit Charge, Seismic Wave, cleanses), while Rage fuels the heaviest tools: Tears of the Earthmother, Earthmother's Binding and the Seismic family. Rage is generated by fighting. Melee swings feed Grove Training and Battleweaver, so it stays in melee or mid-range, swinging its weapon between heals rather than sitting back to cast. Hammer of Life turns melee swings into a free splash heal on nearby allies and damage on nearby enemies, so weaving auto-attacks into the rotation is core, not optional. On top of that it layers HoTs and splash-heals: Tears of the Earthmother's rejuvenate, Flourishing Growth's AP/SP buff, Seismic Wave and Spirit Charge as AoE toppers. Under that sits a strong group cooldown in Sacred Grove and per-ally protection from Ring of Life, Earthmother's Binding and the transformation cooldowns Ancient of Lore and Ancient of War.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   Flourishing Growth, Seismic Wave and Sacred Grove layer AoE healing, and Hammer of Life splashes a free heal off every melee swing. You out-heal sustained BG chip damage without ever stopping to cast.
*   Hammer of Life heals you and the group while you swing, and Spirit Charge takes you to whoever is struggling. No cast bar holding you in place.
*   Sacred Grove drops a big instant heal and a healing zone, Ring of Life raises an ally's healing received while cutting your own magic damage taken, and Earthmother's Binding heals while pulling a share of an ally's damage onto you.

Weaknesses

*   No CC-break and no immunity anywhere in the kit. Sacred Grove and Ring of Life stop damage, not stuns, fears, or roots, so a lockdown chain deletes you like any caster healer.
*   Tears of the Earthmother, Seismic Wave, Earthmother's Binding and the Seismic spenders all draw on a Rage pool that only fills from active melee. Walk into a fight without having been swinging and your biggest heals are not there.
*   No hard lockdown, no ranged answer. Spirit Charge only goes to an ally, so a kiting attacker keeps you off the melee that feeds your healing.

Strength (Attack Power)›Haste›Intellect›Critical Strike›Spirit/MP5

Rotations

Solo / questing

Grovekeeper is a Support spec, so its "rotation" is its off-role damage priority, weaving defensive heals as needed. Single-target and AoE are nearly identical. Set up with Boon of the Bear as your Boon (+melee AP/haste and +Rage from auto-attacks) and Primal Instinct as your Instinct (+attack power). Then by priority: 1) Therazane's Rage to top mana and generate Rage; 2) Neptulon's Wrath / Primal Awakening; 3) Primal Convergence (on the GCD) together with Ancient of War (off-GCD) to empower your Boons; 4) Grove Guardian (off-GCD); 5) Primal Rush for Running on Instinct stacks. It heals allies and pets it passes through, so weave it carefully: never out of melee for longer than a GCD while a Seismic spell is up. Use it pre-pull for free Rage and stacks, and mid-fight only when you can clip 3+ allies. 6) Your Seismic spender: Seismic Crash for single-target Rage generation, Seismic Spike at 3+ targets, or Seismic Wave to spend Rage (and, when low on mana in group content, to conserve mana and fish Infusion of Neptulon stacks). 7) Keep Earthquake down under your target(s) whenever Aftershock procs it, or Geode Barrage when you're low on Rage and Earthquake is still ticking. 8) Wildclaw as filler. TL;DR: offensive cooldowns > Seismic ability > spam Wildclaw. Weave Hand of the Earthmother, or another defensive healing cooldown, the moment you or a party member drops low — you can't do damage if the group is dead.

Dungeon

On pull: Flourishing Growth to buff the group's attack/spell power, then start Tears of the Earthmother on the tank as your primary rejuvenate. Sustain: keep Tears of the Earthmother rolling on the tank. Spirit Charge closes to and tops a spike target, and deals splash damage on the way. Drop Seismic Wave when the group is stacked for a big AoE heal that also chips the pack (Ride the Wave adds a movement-speed boost, Nature's Blessing adds a trailing heal). Per-ally protection: Ring of Life on the group for increased healing received, Earthmother's Binding to redirect a share of a low-health ally's incoming damage onto yourself, and Ancient of Lore / Ancient of War as transformation cooldowns to spike group healing or your own throughput. Emergency: Sacred Grove to stabilize a dying pull, a big instant heal plus a healing zone. Utility: cleanse poison and disease off allies, and keep Grovekeeper's Presence up for the party hit buff. For off-role damage between heals, run the Boon of the Bear / Seismic / Wildclaw priority from the solo rotation, dropping back to heals whenever the group needs it. There is no battle-rez here.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Dual resource. Base-mana percentage costs cover the mana-gated half of the kit: Flourishing Growth, Spirit Charge, Sacred Grove, Ring of Life, Earthmother's Binding, cleanses and Grovekeeper's Presence. Rage covers the heavy-heal half, meaning Tears of the Earthmother, Seismic Wave and the Seismic spenders. Rage only regenerates from actively fighting, through melee swings feeding Grove Training and Battleweaver plus Power Hammer boosting rage from damage. A passive stand-and-cast playstyle leaves the rage-gated heals unavailable exactly when you need them. Mana sustain leans on Spirit/MP5 itemization and Nature's Fury's auto-attack mana return.**

Difficulty**Much of the kit is instant AoE healing — Seismic Wave, Spirit Charge, Hammer of Life splash — and forgiving to just press. Mastery is two resource pools at once, plus weaving melee swings for Hammer of Life uptime while staying inside the AoE-heal radii. Sacred Grove's window belongs against a group spike. Ring of Life and Earthmother's Binding go on the right ally before the damage lands, which is a read, not a reaction.**
