Retail feel

Closest to retail Fury Warrior, but with Second-Wind-style self-healing built into the rotation instead of bolted on. Both run a rage/enrage-empowered filler loop and cleave hard in a clump, with Barbaric Whirl, Brutal Swing and Maximum Carnage filling the same niche as Whirlwind and Bladestorm. Both finish low-health targets with an execute (Decapitate ≈ Execute). Two things differ. The resource is Energy, not Rage, so it never runs dry the way Fury does mid-fight. And self-sustain, the Born in Blood HoT plus Sustaining Swings' lifesteal, is core to the kit rather than absent. That makes it a Fury Warrior built to solo and skirmish rather than one built to parse a raid dummy.

Playstyle

Brutality runs on Energy. Excessive Power raises your max Energy and Thirst for Battle refunds a chunk on Smash. The engine is Enrage: Smash, your biggest no-cooldown Energy dump, requires an active one, and the spec carries three. Unbridled Rage is your main burst window (+Physical damage, and it triggers Maximum Carnage's whirl), Onslaught is an Energy battery that floods the bar for execute phases, and Battle Vigor is a +max-health defensive that still counts as an enrage, so it keeps Smash live. Two rules — never overcap Energy, never overlap your enrages. Brutal Swing is your off-global cleave dump between specials, and you cycle the three enrages so a Smash window is always open. Fire Unbridled Rage, then dump Energy into Smash and Ancestral Strike, swapping to Brutal Swing when 2+ targets clump, while banking crits to shorten Carnage's cooldown via Anger Issues. Execute with Decapitate below 35% HP, which converts spare Energy into extra damage. Overkill empowers it further under 20% and Headlopper adds a crit bleed. Self-preservation is baked into the filler: the Born in Blood heal-over-time and Sustaining Swings' chance-on-hit lifesteal, with Defiance (damage taken cut 30-80% the lower your health) as the panic button and Killing Spree to charge back in after a kill blow. It plays naturally dual-wield since Smash and Barbaric Whirl both strike with both weapons, but a two-hander works too. Battering Ram rewards a big 2H, so run whichever your gear favors.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   Born in Blood's heal-over-time, extended by Mounting Fury and applied through Killing Spree, keeps you topped without a healer. Defiance cuts damage taken 30-80% the lower your health drops.
*   Crush is a frontal-cone knockdown and stun. Horde Breaker makes it a guaranteed crit on a Headbutt target and applies Wounded.
*   Cleave is constant: Barbaric Whirl and Maximum Carnage hit everything nearby, and Decapitate finishes low targets with Overkill boosting it under 20%.

Weaknesses

*   No ally-targeted heal, cleanse, or shield. Warband, a class-pool pick, pulses the Born in Blood heal-over-time onto nearby allies; that is a regen buff, not triage. It cannot heal a dungeon.
*   Outside Unbridled Rage you are pressing filler. Maximum Carnage and Savage's crit-damage boost both key off the window, single-target throughput is middling either way, and the spec leans on outside Strength and Armor-Pen buffs to keep pace.
*   No fear, root, or snare break and no immunity. Killing Spree only fires after a kill or a Born in Blood application, so a kite-and-root chain just holds you.

Stat priority

Hit (until your specials stop missing a +3-level target)›Expertise (until your attacks stop being dodged/parried)›Agility›Critical Strike›Armor Penetration›Haste

Rotations

Solo / questing

Open with Unbridled Rage on anything more than a single mob to trigger Maximum Carnage's whirl and light up your empowered window -> keep an Enrage up at all times (Unbridled Rage, then Onslaught, then Battle Vigor) so Smash stays castable -> Smash and Ancestral Strike as your Energy fillers, Smash being near-free thanks to Thirst for Battle's Energy refund -> Brutal Swing when 2+ targets are clumped, and to bleed off Energy before you overcap -> Crush to stun and set up a guaranteed crit into a Headbutt target -> Decapitate to execute once the target is below 35% HP (each point of spare Energy adds damage; Overkill empowers it further under 20%) -> Barbaric Whirl on any pull of 3+ mobs, and Storm of Steel on a tough single target for sustained ramp. The Born in Blood HoT and Sustaining Swings' lifesteal are your in-combat self-heal, with Defiance for emergencies.

Dungeon

There is no group-facing heal or support ability in this kit. Treat "support" as staying alive and clearing trash fast rather than sustaining others. Pop Unbridled Rage before a pull to trigger Maximum Carnage and open your burst window. Run the same Smash/Ancestral Strike/Brutal Swing filler loop; the Born in Blood HoT and Sustaining Swings lifesteal are your only in-combat recovery. Use Barbaric Whirl, Maximum Carnage and Storm of Steel to clear packs quickly. Keep an Enrage rolling (Unbridled Rage -> Onslaught -> Battle Vigor) so Smash never goes dark, and keep Crush ready to stun a dangerous caster or add. Landing Crush on a full clump as the tank gathers — that is the biggest AoE payoff the spec has. This is an AoE/cleave specialist: it tops meters on multi-target pulls and lags on pure single-target. Symbol of the Warspear gives the party a passive attack-power buff, but do not expect it to offset a healer's absence. Bring an actual healer for anything beyond trash.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Energy, with the max pool raised by Excessive Power and Smash refunding a chunk via Thirst for Battle. Energy uptime is rarely the bottleneck, and Efficient Killer and Relentless' crit refunds ease it further. Two rules run the resource. Never overcap Energy: Brutal Swing is your off-global dump between specials. Never overlap your three Enrage effects (Unbridled Rage, Onslaught, Battle Vigor), because Smash is only castable while an enrage is up, so overlapping wastes Smash windows. The real damage driver is your Unbridled Rage window. Casting it triggers Maximum Carnage and empowers your burst with Savage crit damage, so sustained output depends on keeping Unbridled Rage rolling rather than on raw Energy regen. Anger Issues helps by cutting Carnage's cooldown on crit. It does not go dry the way a Rage-based kit can, but your damage sags between Unbridled Rage windows.**

Difficulty**Smash into Ancestral Strike works on autopilot and will carry you through most content. Everything above that is setup: landing Crush stuns, chaining Headbutt into the guaranteed Horde Breaker crit, banking crits so Anger Issues keeps Carnage's cooldown short, holding Decapitate and Killing Spree for execute range. Unbridled Rage wants a clump, not a single target, so Maximum Carnage and your empowered window land together. Underneath all of it you cycle three Enrage effects — Unbridled Rage, Onslaught, Battle Vigor — so Smash never goes uncastable, and dump Brutal Swing before Energy overcaps.**
