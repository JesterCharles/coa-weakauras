Your control suite is Guard Strike (interrupt plus school lockout), Chains of Darkness (pull plus taunt to yank a target off an ally) and Witchblood Tonic's curse cleanse.

Retail feel

The kit's only caster answer is Guard Strike's interrupt and school lockout. There's no AMS-style magic absorb, no self-peel snare or root, and no gap closer. It plays like a sticky avoidance tank rather than an anti-caster bruiser.

Playstyle

Auto-attack into your two-handed weapon to power the kit. Pommel Smash strikes feed Shadowstorm, a chance to trigger a Shadowstorm burst. Noctis Blade is your on-demand self-heal, an active strike on a short cooldown whose damage and self-heal both scale with your parry rating. Control tools are Guard Strike (interrupt + lock that spell school), Chains of Darkness (AoE pull + taunt), and Desecrate as your avoidance-gated counterstrike. Drop a Witching Idol to cleanse fear, sleep and charm off the party.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   Gaze of the Black Knight shields you, then diverts a share of the damage your party members take. Rare group damage-redirection, and it protects a focused teammate directly.
*   Noctis Blade heals for a share of the damage it deals, both scaling with parry rating, and Pommel Smash procs passive AoE Shadowstorm. Prolonged melee brawls do not kill you.
*   Noctis Blade and Guard Strike cost little and March of the Black King refunds Rage. You rarely go dry and hold threat indefinitely.

Weaknesses

*   No stun, root, snare, or fear anywhere in the kit. Control is Guard Strike's interrupt and school-lockout plus the Chains of Darkness pull and taunt, so you can grab attention and briefly shut a caster's school, never pin a kiting target in place.
*   Noctis Blade is a melee strike, so the self-heal only lands while you are on the target. Against ranged and caster pressure you cannot stick to it does nothing, and that is exactly the damage most likely to kill you.
*   Gaze of the Black Knight is one long-cooldown panic button, so you get roughly one real group-save per fight. Misjudge it, or have it down, and you and your ally have no group mitigation at all.

Stat priority

Hit (melee special-attack cap (until your specials stop missing a +3-level target), keeps Slash/Pommel Smash/Noctis Blade connecting)›Expertise (until your attacks stop being dodged/parried)›Agility (AP, crit, and passive dodge)›Parry Rating (scales Noctis Blade's damage AND its 150%-of-damage self-heal - it's an active strike, not an avoidance proc - and Witchmail/Enchanted Armor double armor-from-items, so parry doubles as offense, sustain and mitigation)›Armor Penetration›Critical Strike›Haste

Rotations

Solo / questing

Auto-attack to build Pommel Smash and let Shadowstorm proc off it. Use Noctis Blade on cooldown; that's your heal, and its damage and healing scale with your parry rating. Fire Desecrate after avoiding an attack, and weave Guard Strike to interrupt casters. Chains of Darkness is available to pull adds but is low-value in a straight solo grind. Keep a Witching Idol down if fear or charm mobs are around.

Dungeon

Hold threat with your Shadow-Brand builders and Noctis Blade. Use Chains of Darkness (AoE pull + taunt) to gather a pack, and Guard Strike to interrupt caster adds. The Witching Idol periodically dispels fear, sleep and charm for the group. Gaze of the Black Knight is your big cooldown to soak a spike for a focused teammate.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Mana- and Rage-hybrid. Core buttons are cheap: Noctis Blade and Guard Strike run on cooldown rather than a big resource dump, and March of the Black King generates Rage over its duration. Under normal play you rarely run dry. Proc and avoidance timing limits your throughput, not a resource bar you have to babysit.**

Difficulty**Auto-attack, press Noctis Blade on cooldown for the heal, drop Gaze roughly on cooldown when things get dicey. That's most of it. The skill left over is timing — whether a Gaze cooldown is worth spending and on whom, Guard Strike interrupts sequenced, Chains of Darkness gathering at the right moment. There's no deep combo or priority system underneath, because the core loop is a handful of buttons.**
