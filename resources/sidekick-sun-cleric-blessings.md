Retail feel

Closest to Holy Paladin, with a strong side-order of Discipline Priest. Like Holy Paladin it's a mana healer built around single-ally externals. Bless tags one ally to unlock an exclusive spell family, the Blessing line of Purity, Retribution, Triumph and Absolution, the way a Beacon target unlocks Holy Paladin's toolkit. That makes you a dedicated bodyguard for whoever you pick. The Blessing-of-Absolution burst that expels Holy damage off your Blessed ally, plus the crit-heal payoff chains (Cultivate Divinity feeding your next Illumination, Hope shaving cooldowns off crit heals, The Redeemer banking a follow-up heal), give it Discipline-Priest-flavored proactive throughput. The Vow/Dawn Solar Power swing is a mild Eclipse-style polarity layered on top of all that.

Playstyle

A mana-based healer with a Solar Power sub-resource. Mana funds the bulk of your heals (Illumination, Shine, Daybreak, Radiant Cascade, Bless, Mercy), while Solar Power builds through a Vow and swings you into a Dawn state that lets you fulfill your Vow and upgrade your output via Fulfillment and Sunlight. One single-ally bodyguard mechanic defines the kit. Bless tags one ally, never yourself, and unlocks the Blessing family on them: Blessing of Purity (full damage immunity, at the cost of their own damage and healing), Blessing of Retribution (deflect the next harmful spell on them), Blessing of Triumph (a haste/damage enrage that also self-heals them under 35%), and Blessing of Absolution (the Blessed ally unleashes a frontal burst of Holy damage). Moment to moment you alternate your two workhorse heals, Illumination and Shine, weaving Daybreak and a rolling Bless on your priority ally, spreading Radiant Cascade's bouncing beam, running Mercy to abolish disease, toggling Holy Form for cheaper healing, and watching for a Dawn window to dump cooldowns.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   Bless makes any nearby ally temporarily unkillable, and Blessing of Purity is full damage immunity. It works on whoever happens to be there rather than a set premade partner, and it is the strongest skirmish-swinging button in the kit.
*   Illumination and Shine do the work, Daybreak covers instants, Radiant Cascade bounces and Heat Wave splashes. Cultivate Divinity, Hope and The Redeemer pay you back for landing effective heals.
*   Blessing of Purity is immunity, Blessing of Retribution deflects spells, Blessing of Triumph enrages with a safety net, and Mercy removes disease. You are the answer to a focused kill target.

Weaknesses

*   Bless cannot target you. The moment you are the one focused, or you are fighting alone, Purity, Retribution and Triumph are dead buttons and your defense is instant heals, Holy Form and kiting.
*   Illumination and Shine are interruptible and pushback-vulnerable unless Dawn is active. A kick chain or a melee train takes a large share of your throughput at the worst possible time.
*   No interrupt, no fear, no snare. Solar Invocation: Revelation roots one enemy on the shared Invocation cooldown, the class kit's Glare adds a short AoE stun, and Sanctify dispels magic, poison and disease. You can peel a caster off a teammate, never lock out a school mid-cast.

Rotations

Solo / questing

Blessings is a healer with almost no offense of its own, so solo grinding is slow and defensive. Keep a Vow active so Solar Power builds and you can reach Dawn. Sustain with Shine (bigger, instant if Dawn is active) or Illumination below ~70% HP, weave Daybreak as an instant top-off, and run Mercy if something's applying disease. Radiant Cascade's bounce is your only real spread-heal, and Heat Wave splashes some of your Illumination/Shine onto nearby allies. Run Holy Form on threat-safe trash for cheaper, better self-healing, and drop it only if you specifically need the threat reduction. Expect to lean on Radiant Cascade and Blessing of Absolution bursts, off a Blessed ally when you have one, for what little damage you have.

Dungeon

Pre-pull: place Bless on the tank, or whoever eats the pull, to unlock the Blessing line and start a rolling heal on them. Sustain: alternate Illumination/Shine as primary heals, and keep Daybreak and a rolling Bless on the tank. Run Mercy to abolish incoming disease. Keep Radiant Cascade for spread-healing bounces (Everglow adds extra jumps), and let Heat Wave splash Illumination/Shine onto the stacked group. Emergency: Blessing of Purity to no-sell an incoming mechanic on your tagged ally, Blessing of Retribution to deflect a dangerous single-target cast, Blessing of Triumph to enrage-and-safety-net a low ally, and a fast Daybreak/Radiant Cascade when the party dips. Filler: run Holy Form during low-damage phases for cheaper healing, and time your Dawn window (and its Fulfillment/Sunlight payoff) before a heavy-damage stretch.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Two resources. Mana, regenerated by Spirit/MP5 with Ancient Rites adding a passive top-up, funds nearly every heal and utility spell. Solar Power builds through a Vow plus heal procs, swinging you into a Dawn state that lets you fulfill your Vow and upgrade specific spells: an instant Shine, plus the Sunlight and Fulfillment payoffs. There's no Rage or melee resource in this spec. It plays like a standard mana-limited healer, so mana management is your real constraint. Run dry and you're down to whatever instants and Blessing externals you have left until you can regen.**

Difficulty**Ignore the Bless bodyguard layer entirely and you still function — spam Illumination and Shine, weave Daybreak, bounce Radiant Cascade. Everything past that is layered on top of a normal healing rotation: Mana and the Vow/Dawn Solar Power swing tracked, your Blessed ally chosen and re-tagged as the fight moves, Blessing of Purity, Retribution, Triumph and Absolution each on the right target, Mercy run proactively against disease, and the Cultivate Divinity, Hope and The Redeemer crit-heal payoffs stacked over the top.**
