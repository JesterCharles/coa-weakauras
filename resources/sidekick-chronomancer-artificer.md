Retail feel

Closest to a builder/spender ranged caster: a stack-driven Arcane Mage crossed with the Essence loop of an Evoker. You cycle wand casts to build Discovery and bank Echo Fragments, then spend those fragments into empowered Continuum/Distortion windows for burst rather than casting big spells on demand. There's no teammate amplification and no pet here. Despite the temporal-support flavor, Artificer is pure Ranged DPS. The payoff is stacking one stat, Spirit, into wand and Arcane damage and managing the fragment economy well.

Playstyle

Artificer's Wand is your filler and main Echo Fragment generator. Wand of Time and Discordance also bank fragments, and you spend fragments on Distortion spells (Shatter Echo, plus an empowered Decomposition) and on fueling and extending Continuum spells. Continuum spells (Singularity Core, Flux Emitter, Paradox Cannon, Aether Compression, only one active at a time) empower or extend your wand attacks for a window that grows with each Echo Fragment consumed. Wand of Time extends whichever one is up through Time Is A Circle. Discordance's Clocked In makes your next Artificer's Wand instant, banks a bonus fragment, and fires Crystal Cannon's extra wand strikes. You self-buff through passives rather than a toggled stance. Artificer's Empowerment raises your Spirit and Wand Expert converts a share of that Spirit into ranged attack power, so almost the entire kit scales off one stat. Control and utility are situational: Collapse sets up a delayed stun, Stasis incapacitates, Clasp of Infinity roots, Waves of Time knocks back for peels, and Unstable Chronoglass soaks a caster's opening spells, with a Threads of Eternity damage-over-time riding your Discordance. Self-sustain is minor. Reverse Wound is a direct heal you can cast while moving under Aether Compression.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   Artificer's Wand and auto-wand attacks keep damage flowing while you kite. No cast bar.
*   Artificer's Empowerment raises Spirit and Wand Expert converts part of it into ranged attack power. Nearly the whole kit runs off one stat, which makes gearing trivial.
*   Bank Echo Fragments into an empowered Singularity Core, dump them on Shatter Echo, and the sustained spike holds up once the loop is spun. Expert testers keep pace with uncapped-AoE specs while only cleaving. Single-target is the identity here.

Weaknesses

*   Reverse Wound is the only real self-heal and there is no interrupt. The offensive control is there (Collapse stun, Stasis incapacitate, roots, knockbacks) but none of it is a spell-lock or a peel, so a melee train that sticks kills you before fragments matter.
*   No uncapped AoE. Flux Emitter caps at 3/4 targets and Arc Collision is a capped, undertuned timestorm, so dense add packs are your worst content.
*   Burst waits on the Echo Fragment loop. You cannot spike a fresh target on demand, only chip with the wand until fragments are banked.
*   Mobility costs damage directly. Aether Compression is your only cast-on-the-move enabler and it is a Continuum, so turning it on drops Singularity Core, which costs banked Echo Fragments to put back. Every kiting window has a DPS bill, and it is a capstone pick.
*   Group utility is thin. No pet, situational control, a Threads of Eternity DoT, and Hasten to hand one ally haste and movement speed. You contribute your damage and little else.

Rotations

Solo / questing

Fill with Artificer's Wand to bank Echo Fragments and keep your damage Continuum empowered, Singularity Core on a tough target. Press Wand of Time and Discordance on cooldown. Neither hits hard per cast, but Wand of Time extends whichever Continuum is up (Time Is A Circle), so you can hold Singularity with a cheap 2–3-fragment cast instead of a full 5. Discordance's Clocked In makes your next Artificer's Wand instant, banks a bonus fragment, and fires Crystal Cannon. Spend banked fragments on Shatter Echo, your main burst spender. Keep Decomposition ticking on anything that will live its full duration, but don't sink fragments into it. Bank ahead with Paradox Cannon between pulls. Against undead or demons, Wand of Time is a guaranteed crit. Drop Reverse Wound to top off between pulls rather than mid-burst.

Dungeon

On a single boss, keep Singularity Core and Decomposition up. Press Wand of Time and Discordance on cooldown to hold the Continuum cheaply and bank fragments, filling with Artificer's Wand and spending on Shatter Echo. Open with Past Mistakes + Hasten. On a pack, swap your Continuum to Flux Emitter for its 3–4-target cleave. There is no uncapped AoE here, and Arc Collision is a capped, undertuned timestorm rather than a pack nuke — don't build around it. Wand of Time shines in undead dungeons (Strat/Scholo), where it always crits. Use Unstable Chronoglass to soak an add's dangerous casts, and let a Threads of Eternity DoT ride your Discordance. Self-cover with Reverse Wound during quieter beats. Lean on positioning rather than expecting to top yourself mid-burst.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Mana matters early, then stops. Before you pick up Luck, sustained casting can run Artificer dry, which is a common early-leveling complaint. Luck (base Chronomancer, down-and-right of the HoT) is the fix. Each point keeps 40% of your mana regeneration ticking in combat, 80% at two points, and the spec stacks Spirit, so that in-combat regen takes mana off the table once it's talented. At 60, Temporal Restoration, Time Out and Spirit remove the problem entirely. Geared Artificers never run low even while throwing dungeon dispels. If mana is still short there, it's the gear, not the spec. What you manage is Echo Fragments. Artificer's Wand, Wand of Time and Discordance generate them. You spend them on your Distortion spells (Shatter Echo, an empowered Decomposition) and Continuum spells, and to extend their empower windows. That loop is the spec, not mana. Your wand casts also build a stacking Discovery buff on the side.**

Difficulty**Artificer's Wand as filler with Wand of Time and Discordance on cooldown is a solid baseline for almost no effort. Running it well is another thing: Echo Fragment banking, keeping your Continuum (Singularity Core, or Flux Emitter on packs) alive with cheap Wand of Time extensions instead of full-cost recasts, timing Shatter Echo spends and Decomposition windows, banking ahead with Paradox Cannon between pulls, and layering control on a read — all while kiting to keep wand uptime. That is a lot of systems running at once under pressure.**
