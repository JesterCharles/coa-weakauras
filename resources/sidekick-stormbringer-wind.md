Retail feel

Closest to Augmentation Evoker. Its identity isn't throughput healing or raw DPS. It stacks group buffs onto allies the way Ebon Might, Prescience and Blessing of the Bronze do. That's Tailwind's group haste and damage, Aerodynamics' attack and spell power buff, Clear Skies' group hit%, Tempest's Call's group haste, and Surge of Might's damage aura, plus an absorb in Kiss of the Clouds. Its own damage comes from a semi-autonomous extension of itself, the Air Elemental, rather than personal burst. Unlike a real healer it has no ally HP heal at all. Its sustain is a group absorb plus deep CC, mirroring Augmentation's buff-and-mitigate-rather-than-heal kit. Like Augmentation, its PvP fantasy is attaching to a teammate and making the fight lopsided rather than being the wall.

Load leveling build at level **60**

Playstyle

You run with a permanent Air Elemental out (Summon: Air Elemental) that deals damage and stacks Invigoration off your Aeroblast casts. Gale and other damage/utility spells generate Static (3 charges, 10-sec recharge) while your bigger group tools (Aerodynamics, Kiss of the Clouds, Tailwind, Updraft, Aeroblast, Unshackle) all deplete it. So you juggle two resources: mana, a real cost at a chunk of base per cast, and Static, a build-then-spend meter closer to combo points than to mana. Moment to moment you're pre-buffing with Tailwind/Clear Skies/Aerodynamics, shielding the group with Kiss of the Clouds, and layering peels (Raging Zephyr's repeated pull, Cloudburst, Windsurf's attack-intercept, Wind Gate/Evacuate to yank an ally to safety) rather than tanking damage yourself. Note there is no ally HP heal in the kit, only shields and control. Survival leans on keeping the elemental alive. Losing it costs you your damage, and re-summoning is a slow, expensive cast, mitigated only when Lexicon of Servitude is banked to make the next summon instant and free.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   Raging Zephyr pulls in repeatedly, Cloudburst knocks back, Windsurf intercepts an attack, and Wind Gate and Evacuate yank an ally out. You scatter several attackers and pull a teammate out of the fire.
*   Tailwind, Aerodynamics, Clear Skies, Tempest's Call and Kiss of the Clouds stack into one haste, power, hit and shield package. A coin-flip clash goes your team's way.
*   The Air Elemental keeps damaging while you are kiting or CC'd. Kiss of the Clouds' group absorb and deep peels stand in for the healing this kit does not have.

Weaknesses

*   No ally heal anywhere in the kit. Kiss of the Clouds' absorb and your peels are the whole answer to a focus train, and neither puts health back on a bar.
*   Stacking Aerodynamics, Tailwind and Tempest's Call, shielding with Kiss of the Clouds, and casting CC in one fight burns base mana fast.
*   Everything hangs on the Air Elemental staying up. Its death or dispel takes your damage with it, and the re-summon is a slow cast that gets punished in PvP unless Lexicon of Servitude is banked.

Stat priority

Spell Hit Rating (to cap)›Spell Power›Intellect›Haste Rating›Critical Strike Rating›Spirit/MP5

Rotations

Solo / questing

Open with Summon: Air Elemental and let it engage first. Use Gale on cooldown (3 charges) to build Static; it's your closest thing to a filler nuke and cleaves up to 2 extra enemies. Against a single mob, dump Static into Aeroblast for the two-part hit. Against a pull, drop Updraft under the group for ground AoE tick damage. Raging Zephyr pulls a dangerous add off you, Cloudburst shoves a pack off you if you're swarmed, and Windsurf intercepts a hard incoming hit while you keep casting. Command your elemental with Flurry or Skyfall for extra burst on your target, and pop Unshackle to transform it into a stronger form for a damage window (extended as you keep depleting Static via Wrath of Al'Akir). There is no self-heal here — if a pull goes bad, disengage and shield with Kiss of the Clouds rather than trying to heal through it. Keep the pet alive, since re-summoning is slow unless Lexicon of Servitude is banked.

Dungeon

Pre-pull: Tailwind (group haste/damage), Aerodynamics (group attack/spell power), Clear Skies (group hit%), and Tempest's Call (group haste burst) before a hard pull. Then Kiss of the Clouds right before the pull lands so the shield eats the opening damage. Sustain: there is no ally HP heal, so the group absorb from Kiss of the Clouds, re-applied on cooldown, is your damage smoothing. Emergency: Wind Gate/Evacuate pulls a lagging or badly positioned ally back to the group, and Windsurf intercepts a spike aimed at you. Control: Raging Zephyr and Cloudburst on adds as needed. Damage: keep the Air Elemental engaged and buffed with Invigoration stacks, command it via Flurry/Skyfall, and fill downtime with Gale-into-Updraft/Aeroblast once buffs and shields are up. Treat this spec as a buff-shield-and-control support whose only 'sustain' is the Kiss of the Clouds absorb, not as a group's healer.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Dual resource. Mana pays real costs per cast and can run dry in extended fights or under heavy buff and shield spam. Static is the combo-style secondary, built mainly by Gale (3 charges) and by the elemental's damage via Storm Bond, then spent by your bigger group tools: Updraft, Flurry, Tailwind, Aerodynamics, Kiss of the Clouds, Skyfall, Unshackle, Aeroblast. The two feed each other. Master Airbender lets Gale casts shave the cooldowns of your Static-depleting abilities, and Wrath of Al'Akir lets each Static spend extend Unshackle. You'll go dry on mana well before you're starved for Static, so mana is the real ceiling on how long you can keep buffing, shielding and controlling. Nothing drains it on healing, since the kit has no ally HP heal.**

Difficulty**More to track than a single-resource caster deals with — two resources, pet upkeep, buff and shield timing on top of control — and it shows early. Peel is sequenced, not improvised: pull into knockback into intercept. Tailwind, Aerodynamics, Tempest's Call and Kiss of the Clouds want to land exactly when your team commits. Unshackle uptime comes from chaining Static spends through Wrath of Al'Akir. None of it happens if the Air Elemental is dead or out of range.**
