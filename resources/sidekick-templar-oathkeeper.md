The layers are the point: the delayed Stagger from Keeping the Oath, the Magic-damage reduction Battlepriest gives while its health sits high or dips low, the magic resistances from Light's Ward, the Staffguard absorb each Sacred Swing generates, the Physical damage reduction Untainted passively procs off its own healing, and the healing from Benediction and Libram of Grace.

Retail feel

Closest to a Brewmaster Monk fused with Mistweaver fistweaving. The Keeping the Oath passive is a direct Stagger clone, delaying a % of damage and taking it over time, exactly like Brewmaster's core defensive gimmick. Tome of Light's shield plus the Untainted/Staffguard absorbs are Brewmaster-style active mitigation layered on top. The damage-into-healing engine plays like Mistweaver's Fistweaving loop. You build Oaths from melee swings and dodges/parries, then dump a finisher, Benediction, that heals off Stamina and dodge/parry rating, gated behind combo-point-style banking rather than mana. Uther's Legacy even makes Condemn heal you off the damage it deals, so the spec both tanks hits and turns those same hits into sustain.

Playstyle

Oathkeeper runs an Oath loop on top of Energy. Its builders each bank an Oath: Sacred Swing (usable off an avoided attack), Righteous Lunge (whose Oath makes your abilities damage and heal for more), Holy Cleave (whose Oath makes your abilities strike additional nearby enemies, so spamming it scales your whole kit toward multi-target), and Reckoning. When you've banked enough you dump an Oath Breaker (Chastise, Reckoning, Benediction) that consumes them, except the spec passive Keeping the Oath stops Oath Breakers from consuming your Oaths and delays 40% of incoming direct damage over time, a Stagger clone. You're built to stand in melee and out-heal and out-mitigate rather than avoid damage. So you auto-attack, use Sacred Swing off your dodges and parries to keep Oaths flowing and grant a Staffguard absorb, and pick each Oath Breaker on the fly: Reckoning or Chastise to burst something down, Benediction when you or an ally need the heal. Tome of Light's full-health shield, Barrier of Light, the Untainted and Staffguard absorbs, and the Absolution taunt round it out into a self-sufficient brawler that peels for others while barely needing outside healing.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   Keeping the Oath staggers and delays damage, Battlepriest and Light's Ward cut magic damage, and the Untainted and Staffguard absorbs sit on top of Benediction and Condemn self-heals. In an extended skirmish you do not die.
*   Tome of Light shields for 100% of max health and Staffguard and Untainted absorb repeatedly, which answers burst windows. Disciplined Mind's dispel and Barrier of Light's healing boost extend that to the party. Testament of Hope summons mirror copies that fight at your side and cut your damage taken.
*   Sacred Swing and Reckoning deliver real AoE cleave, Absolution's AoE taunt peels attackers off a partner, and Barrier of Light redirects ally threat onto you. Messy BG scrums are where this is strongest.

Weaknesses

*   No interrupt in the spec line. Covering it costs a class-kit pick on Divine Force, a stun-leap whose spell-lock only works on non-player casts. No dispel-all and no magic-immunity button, so once Tome of Light and the absorbs are spent you tank the next chain raw.
*   Sacred Swing requires a recent dodge or parry, so against pure ranged attackers or while CC-locked your Oath generation stalls and takes your damage and your healing with it.
*   Every burst-heal answer is cooldown-bound. Benediction wants banked Oaths to hit hard, Temple Guardian's charge-heal sits on a charge timer, and Testament of Faith and the class kit's Libram of Tenacity each live on their own cooldown. Back-to-back heal checks outpace what you have available.

Stat priority

Hit Rating (until your specials stop missing a +3-level target)›Expertise Rating (until your attacks stop being dodged/parried)›Agility›Stamina›Critical Strike›Haste›Spirit/MP5

Rotations

Solo / questing

Open by generating Oaths: Righteous Lunge (via Faith Guard), or Sacred Swing once a mob's swing gives you a dodge or parry to open it. Spend into Reckoning as your default Oath Breaker while leveling. It deals AoE Holy damage and restores Energy per enemy struck, keeping you both offensive and resource-solvent through a pull, and Sacred Resistance bumps your Armor when you cast it. Use Sacred Swing on cooldown whenever it's up, after a dodge or parry, for cheap AoE damage and a Staffguard absorb. Temple Guardian is your gap-closer and opener. It charges to an ally, healing you both. Let Untainted, Condemn's self-heal (via Uther's Legacy), the Oath Breaker heals, and the Stagger passive handle sustain. You shouldn't need to sit and drink much between pulls.

Dungeon

Pull with Absolution to taunt and establish threat; Temple Guardian's charge is a gap-closer, not a taunt. Then maintain the Oath loop continuously through auto-attacks, Sacred Swing off your dodges and parries, and your Oath builders on cleave packs. Benediction is your baseline reactive heal. It scales with Stamina and consumes banked Oaths for extra healing, Improved Benediction also armors the target off your Agility/Stamina, and Disciplined Mind lets it dispel a poison or disease, so leaning on it is both healing and mitigation. Bank toward Benediction by default for tank and party sustain. Switch to Reckoning/Chastise only when a pull needs to die faster than it needs healing; under Faith Guard your Chastise unleashes a line of Holy damage while a Libram is up. Use Absolution to peel adds off the actual healer or a squishy DPS, and Barrier of Light to pull allies' threat onto yourself. Pop Tome of Light's full-health shield as your defensive cooldown when a burst AoE phase hits, and keep a Libram active before an incoming sustained-damage phase. Casting Oath Breakers and Libram use feed each other's cooldowns via Lorescribe and Eternally Devoted.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Energy, Rogue and Monk style. It regenerates passively and doesn't go dry like mana, but a second layer gates ability access: your Oath count. You bank Oaths from builders before you can lean on an Oath Breaker. Those builders are Sacred Swing, which needs a recent dodge or parry, plus Righteous Lunge and Reckoning. Keeping the Oath stops Oath Breakers consuming those Oaths at all. So you never run out of resource, but your generation rate depends partly on landing dodges and parries to open Sacred Swing, which dries up against non-melee attackers or while crowd-controlled. Reckoning also refunds Energy per enemy struck, keeping the pool healthy on packs.**

Difficulty**Auto/dodge into Oath builders into Oath Breaker is a priority system anyone picks up in a pull or two. Mastery is defensive sequencing — Tome of Light's shield, Barrier of Light, the Staffguard and Untainted absorbs used in order rather than panic-dumped together — plus knowing mid-fight whether an Oath Breaker should be Benediction for the heal or Reckoning/Chastise for the damage. Sacred Swing's dodge/parry gate has to be forced open when you aren't naturally getting hit, and the Absolution taunt has to peel for a partner without spending your own survival cooldowns early.**
