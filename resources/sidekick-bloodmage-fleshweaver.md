Retail feel

Closest to Discipline Priest, with a dash of Blood DK bolted on. The core identity echoes the Atonement pattern: build a resource by attacking, spend it on shields and instant heals, and carry a signature ability that damages the enemy while healing your group. That signature is Heartbreak, which deals Shadow damage to a target and heals allies near it. Blood Veil is your Power Word: Shield/Rapture analog, with Aortic Aegis extending it to the target's party. Fleshcraft, which heals an ally for a big share of their max HP, is your Pain Suppression/Guardian Spirit-tier stop-the-bleeding button. Layer in paying your own health to cast for Rage plus Sanguine Essence amplifying your output, and you get Blood DK self-cost texture stapled onto the Disc chassis. No single retail healer runs an Atonement-style AoE loop off a Rage-from-health-cost economy like this.

Playstyle

Fleshweaver runs on Rage, not mana. You build it in caster form by paying your own health to cast; Blood Tap and your other health-cost spells each seed a stack of Pooled Vitality. That Rage goes into a mostly-instant healing kit: Sanguine Mend for single-target, Hemoglobe and Crimson Tide for zone AoE, Dark Liturgy for a heal that links you to allies through Blood Bond, Heartbreak as an attack-them-heal-nearby-allies nova, and Blood Veil / Fleshcraft as your shield and panic button. Sanguine Essence is a transformation that doesn't lock out your caster spells. It amplifies the kit you already have rather than handing you a melee bar, so you keep casting straight through it. Pooled Vitality closes the loop: health-cost spells stack it, and your big Rage-cost heals consume 10 stacks to fire empowered. Once the engine is spinning it rarely stalls. The fragile window is the first few seconds of a surprise fight, before you have any Rage banked.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   Fleshcraft is a large percent-max-HP instant heal and Blood Veil a Spirit-scaled instant absorb, with Vampyr's Kiss refunding Rage behind them. Chip damage does not kill this.
*   Heartbreak, Crimson Tide and Darkfallen Lament damage and heal a stacked pack at once. Clustered BG brawls turn into attrition wins.
*   The kit is mostly instants, and Sanguine Essence amplifies healing and damage without locking you out of your caster spells. You heal and damage on the move.

Weaknesses

*   No stun, root, fear, or snare. Arterial Bind's AoE silence is all you have, and it only stops casters standing in the pool, not a melee train on your ally.
*   The spec cleanses nothing off you. Purify Blood only removes a disease from an ally, Hemal Excision only siphons curses, and there is no speed or disengage button. Class-kit picks soften it, but a stun chain that has landed still gets ridden out.
*   Cold start is Rage-gated. Blood Veil, Fleshcraft and Crimson Tide all cost Rage you build first through Blood Tap and health-cost casts, so an ambush at 0 Rage leaves you no shield and no panic button.

Stat priority

Spell Power (every heal — Hemoglobe, Sanguine Mend, Dark Liturgy, Crimson Tide — scales directly off SP)›Spirit (Teachings of Lana'thel converts Spirit straight into spell hit and spell crit rating, on top of Hemoglobe/Aortic Aegis's own Spi coefficients)›Stamina (pays for the health-cost casts — Gorge, Suture, Vampyr's Kiss, Dark Liturgy — and co-scales Hemoglobe)›Haste (more frequent Hemoglobe pulses/Crimson Tide windows in a dot-transmute healing style)›Critical Strike›Intellect (no mana is ever spent, so Int's only value is incidental)

Rotations

Solo / questing

Pull with Heartbreak on a single target. Keep weaving your health-cost casts, Blood Tap in particular, as your Rage and Pooled Vitality builders. On tougher targets, drop into Sanguine Essence so it amplifies your throughput while you keep casting. Pop Gorge before a hard pull to bank 10 stacks of Pooled Vitality for an empowered heal. Use Blood Veil proactively when a pull is about to hit, and Vampyr's Kiss to refund a chunk of Rage when you're spending faster than you build. Sanguine Mend keeps you up. Save Fleshcraft for the 'about to die' moments on elites or rares.

Dungeon

Pre-pull: put up your party buffs, Blood-Cursed Armor for group armor and Bloodthorns on whoever takes the hits. Add a Blood Veil pre-shield on the tank if a hard pull is incoming. On the pull: drop Hemoglobe for its pulsing AoE heal and Pooled Vitality generation, then use Heartbreak/Crimson Tide to chip trash while topping the group. Sustain: Sanguine Mend as your primary tank/spot heal. Dark Liturgy links a hurt member through Blood Bond, so Darkfallen Lament keeps them topped. Crimson Tide and Hemoglobe when the group is clustered. Emergency: Blood Veil on the tank before a big hit, Fleshcraft as the true 'oh crap' button on a near-death tank or yourself. Utility as needed: Arterial Bind to silence a dangerous caster pack, Hemal Excision to siphon a curse.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Pure Rage economy, with no mana anywhere in the kit. Rage comes from health-cost casting (Blood Tap and your other health-cost spells) and from Vampyr's Kiss, which instantly refunds a chunk of your max Rage. You spend it on heals: Sanguine Mend, Blood Veil, Crimson Tide, Fleshcraft, Heartbreak. Going fully dry in a sustained fight is hard. Pooled Vitality stacks from health-cost spells and Gorge make your big Rage-cost heals fire empowered by consuming 10 stacks, and Vampyr's Kiss is an on-demand refill. The real risk is the opening seconds of a surprise engagement, when the Rage bar starts at zero and none of the safety-net loops have spun up yet.**

Difficulty**You track Rage the way a Warrior does, and the priority order is close to linear once you know which tools want Sanguine Essence up. The depth: banking Pooled Vitality stacks for the right spell — Blood Veil or Fleshcraft — and putting ground effects where they belong, Crimson Tide and Hemoglobe on the group, Arterial Bind's silence pool on a caster. Heartbreak has to hit the right target while its heal reaches the most allies. And Vampyr's Kiss refills Rage, so knowing when to fire it beats casting into an empty bar.**
