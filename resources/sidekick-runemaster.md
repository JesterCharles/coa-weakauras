Runemasters are the master arcanists of Azeroth, with powers over all forms of magic. The Runemaster specialises in teleportation, and their immense knowledge of the magical arts makes them effective mage hunters.

🌱 We recommend leveling as

GlyphicPvE APvP B

Rune/glyph combo caster with a Familiar pet and Frost Glyph slows for kiting safety; kills fast with a warding defensive.

**Solo tip** Keep your flame glyph up, build essence then spend combos; frost-glyph slows let you kite melee mobs.
