Retail feel

Closest to a retail Enhancement Shaman or Retribution Paladin reskinned in Shadow. It's a Strength-based melee striker with a build-and-spend secondary resource. Voidforged Edge and Netherstrike generate Insanity, Obliteration dumps it with a crit-refund loop via Twilight's Call, and Shadow of the Void is a Maelstrom-Weapon-style proc window that escalates your Shadow damage the longer you stay in melee above 40 Insanity. The Void Strength passive converts Strength into Shadow spell damage and spell hit. Add Voidborne's stun-immunity burst and you get a Strength melee DPS whose weapon strikes carry a Shadow-magic tail, not a healer or a caster.

Playstyle

Godblade is a melee-weapon-gated Shadow bruiser. You stand in a target's face building Insanity off Voidforged Edge (charges, +20) and Netherstrike (+10), then dump it into Obliteration for a big Weapon+Shadow smash that drains Insanity. Crits refund 20 via Twilight's Call. Netherstrike also purges a magic buff and, via Voidseeker, resets to become an any-health execute. Above 40 Insanity your melee passively triggers Shadow of the Void, a stacking Shadow-damage-and-crit buff, so once you're in the pocket your damage snowballs. Rift sets up a guaranteed Obliteration crit and drops a rift. Voidborne is your burst-and-anti-stun cooldown: more damage, more crit, stun immunity. Tentacle summon lines (Tentacle of N'Zoth's silence, Eldritch Obelisk) and pulls round it out. The priority is simple. Keep Voidforged Edge and Netherstrike feeding Insanity, spend on Obliteration, ride Shadow of the Void's stacks.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   Hold above 40 Insanity and Shadow of the Void keeps stacking. Rift into a guaranteed-crit Obliteration, with the crit refund behind it, is a repeatable kill window.
*   Voidborne is burst and survival in one button: damage, crit, and stun immunity, so you push through a peel with the ramp intact.
*   Empire's Grasp pulls and snares a kiter, Tentacle of N'Zoth silences a caster you cannot reach. Netherstrike strips a defensive buff off the kill target on the way in.

Weaknesses

*   Voidforged Edge, Netherstrike and Obliteration all need a melee weapon and melee range. A kiter who plays around Empire's Grasp starves the entire kit and stalls the Insanity ramp.
*   Outside Voidborne you are thin. Nothing defensive in the spec tree, and the self-healing is piecemeal: Lost in the Void's heal-back on the class-tree Embrace the Void, and the ally healing Lead the Covenant echoes back. Hallucination sheds movement-impairing effects and nothing else. A stun chain leaves you no answers.
*   Drop below 40 Insanity and Shadow of the Void falls off entirely. An interrupted approach or a forced kite resets a large chunk of your damage, not just a GCD.

Stat priority

Hit (melee special-attack cap (until your specials stop missing a +3-level target))›Expertise (until your attacks stop being dodged/parried)›Strength›Armor Penetration›Critical Strike›Haste

Rotations

Solo / questing

Open at range with Eye of N'zoth to start the pull, then close to melee. Keep Voidforged Edge on cooldown (charges, +20 Insanity, your main generator) and weave Netherstrike (+10, purges a buff; Voidseeker can reset it into an any-health execute). Once you've built the Insanity, set up with Rift for a guaranteed crit, then dump Obliteration for the big Weapon+Shadow smash. Crits refund 20 Insanity via Twilight's Call. Stay above 40 Insanity so Shadow of the Void keeps proccing off your melee. Pop Voidborne for burst on tougher single targets, and summon a Tentacle of N'Zoth or drop Eldritch Obelisk on drawn-out fights for extra Shadow damage.

Dungeon

Pre-pull: buff the group and get into position. Pull with Eye of N'zoth or a Voidforged Edge opener to start Insanity. Then stay in melee, weaving Voidforged Edge (Abyssal Cleave makes Eye of N'zoth strike an extra enemy for pack cleave) and Netherstrike while building toward Obliteration for the AoE burst. Obliteration's Weapon damage is boosted by Warrior of the Old Gods. Keep Shadow of the Void stacked by staying above 40 Insanity, set up big hits with Rift, and blow Voidborne on the pull that needs the burst. Use Empire's Grasp to yank a caster off a squishy ally, Tentacle of N'Zoth to silence a dangerous add, and Eldritch Obelisk for a damage/speed zone on a stack. Voidborne's stun immunity also lets you keep swinging through a mechanic that would otherwise lock you out.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Hybrid Mana and Insanity, but Insanity is what you manage. Mana funds your occasional casts (Eye of N'zoth and the like, costing a % of base mana), and End Times cuts those costs, so a melee-uptime player rarely goes dry. Insanity comes from Voidforged Edge (20) and Netherstrike (10), and drains through Obliteration. Obliteration crits refund 20 via Twilight's Call, and Netherstrike crits stack its crit-damage via Who Needs Sanity? Staying above 40 Insanity keeps Shadow of the Void proccing off every melee attack, so hovering high is rewarded. Hitting 100 risks a class-wide overload that only an ally's cleanse clears. Spend into Obliteration rather than idle-capping.**

Difficulty**Deeper than it looks. The base loop — Voidforged Edge and Netherstrike on cooldown, Obliteration when the Insanity is there, above 40 for Shadow of the Void — works on autopilot.
