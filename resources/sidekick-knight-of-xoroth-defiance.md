Retail feel

Closest to Blood Death Knight, with a strong secondary nod to Vengeance Demon Hunter. The core identifying loop sits close to Death Strike's math. Build a stacking resource off your own weapon-swing abilities (Shieldgore → Demonfire), then dump it into a self-heal scaled by Stamina and defensive stats (Suffuse). The layered block and absorb mechanics (Brimstone Buckler, Demonic Bulwark, Black Shield fed by Demon's Blood) mirror Blood's Bone Shield/Icebound Fortitude defensive stacking. The fire and demon theming, Demonfire-as-Soul-Fragment-analog, and pet-summon flavor (Hellfire Imp/Abyssal) also read as Vengeance DH's Fracture→Soul Cleave loop. Either way it's the retail archetype of tanky melee that heals itself off its own damage rotation and is hard to burst down.

Playstyle

Defiance runs on Rage plus a stacking secondary resource, Demonfire, thrown off almost automatically by its own attack buttons. Shieldgore generates 2 Demonfire and requires a shield; Call: Hellfire Imp is a free rage battery that also summons a body. You spend Demonfire on Suffuse, an instant self-heal that scales off Stamina and defensive stats, so you top yourself off constantly as a side effect of pressing your rotation. On top of that self-heal you layer stacking block and absorb mechanics. Brimstone Buckler summons a Hellfire Imp off successful blocks, Forgefiend's Bulwark enrages you off block/parry/dodge, Demonic Bulwark converts some blocks into critical blocks for double value, and Curse of Xoroth feeds Black Shield, a stacking absorb pumped up by your Demon's Blood via Hellwrath and refilled again by Sacrificial Circle's Soul Furnace shield. Each active Hellfire Imp also chips both damage schools down (Imp Guards for physical, Imp Aura for magic, Hellsmelted Armor for extra block), so more imps out means more mitigation. Group utility comes with it: Chains of Xoroth slows everything in an area then pulls it in at the end, Call: Hellfire Abyssal drops a summoned abyssal that keeps swinging for you, and Legion's Presence pulses an ally damage-buff aura. A chunk of your job is timing those to swing a fight, not just surviving it.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   Suffuse scales off Stamina and defense and Shieldgore's Demonfire generation pays for it. Sacrificial Circle burns your active imps as a backup emergency heal and leaves a Soul Furnace absorb behind. Sustained pressure does not out-damage that.
*   Brimstone Buckler summons imps off blocks, Demonic Bulwark adds critical block, and Curse of Xoroth feeds a Black Shield absorb that Demon's Blood pumps through Hellwrath. Imp Aura, Imp Guards and Hellsmelted Armor cover magic as well as melee.
*   Chains of Xoroth slows an area then pulls at the end, Call: Hellfire Abyssal leaves something attacking, and Legion's Presence buffs ally damage. Demolisher's Flesh Hook and Chainwhip taunt on top. You change the fight around you, not just your own health bar.

Weaknesses

*   No external healing anywhere. Suffuse, Sacrificial Circle and Forgefiend's Bulwark's Enrage all target the Knight and nobody else, so a duo partner lives on your peels, never on your heals.
*   No anti-CC or CC-break in the ability list. A stunlock, fear, or sap chain turns off Shieldgore and Suffuse at once, since nothing casts while controlled.
*   Shieldgore and Brimstone Buckler both key off a shield, locking you into 1H and shield. The identity is block and mitigation first, so against a two-hand physical bruiser the damage gap is real. You swing fights through survivability, CC and utility, not damage races.

Stat priority

Defense rating (to crit-immunity)›Stamina›Dodge›Parry›Block›Strength›Armor

Rotations

Solo / questing

Open by engaging with Flesh Hook (pulls the enemy to you) or Chains of Xoroth, then apply Curse of Xoroth for the DoT, which also primes your next Shieldgore's Black Shield. Settle into the loop from there: Shieldgore on cooldown (main threat/damage + 2 Demonfire), Call: Hellfire Imp on cooldown for the free rage and an extra body soaking hits, Hellfire Bellows against packs (transformed Flames of Xoroth), and Implosion on pulls for the AoE burst + imp generation + rage. Dump Demonfire into Suffuse liberally to stay topped while leveling; self-sustain beats banking for burst when soloing. Fall back on Sacrificial Circle only if you need an emergency top-up by sacrificing active imps (it also grants a Soul Furnace absorb shield). Keep imps up against elites and tough mobs. Each one is passive block and damage reduction via Imp Guards/Imp Aura/Hellsmelted Armor.

Dungeon

Pull: open with Flesh Hook or Chains of Xoroth to gather the pack onto you. On a big pull, pop Call: Hellfire Abyssal for the summoned abyssal that keeps attacking. Shieldgore establishes threat immediately, and Demolisher lets Flesh Hook/Chainwhip taunt loose adds. Sustain: keep Shieldgore on cooldown to keep Demonfire flowing, and spend most stacks into Suffuse to hold your own HP up. This spec cannot heal the party, so plan pulls assuming the group's actual healer - if any - covers everyone else. Keep Call: Hellfire Imp on cooldown to stack imps for their per-imp mitigation and threat. Emergency: Curse of Xoroth → Black Shield absorb (buffed by your Demon's Blood via Hellwrath) and Brimstone Buckler's block-summons for incoming spike damage. Sacrificial Circle is the last-ditch self-heal, sacrificing active imps and leaving a Soul Furnace shield behind.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Rage is the primary resource, generated normally through combat and topped up by Call: Hellfire Imp. It doesn't go dry in a real fight. Shieldgore, Implosion and Hellfire Bellows are all cheap relative to how fast Rage comes in, and A Gift from Hell and Black Skull Shield cut Shieldgore's cost further. Demonfire is the secondary, a stacking currency generated by Shieldgore and Implosion and spent on Suffuse, a self-heal, and Hellfire Bellows. Your own core rotation buttons feed it, so it effectively never runs out either, and self-sustain stays nearly unconditional as long as you're swinging. Demon's Blood is a tertiary and more situational resource, stacked passively and extended by Hellwrath, that pumps up the absorb amount of Black Shield.**

Difficulty**Staying alive is the easy part: Shieldgore on cooldown, Demonfire dumped into Suffuse, imps and Black Shield up when you're focused. The difference higher up is deliberate spending. Bank Demonfire for a timed Hellfire Bellows or Implosion instead of auto-feeding Suffuse. Chains of Xoroth yanks an enemy healer or a low-HP target somewhere terrible, which beats the defensive use. Prime Curse of Xoroth so your next Shieldgore drops a full Black Shield right before a predictable enemy cooldown lands. Stack imps ahead of a burst window for the per-imp mitigation, and use Demolisher's Flesh Hook and Chainwhip to peel on a plan rather than a reflex.**
