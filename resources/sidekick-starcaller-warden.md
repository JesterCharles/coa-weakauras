Retail feel

Closest to Havoc Demon Hunter, a hyper-mobile melee DPS built around glaives, burst mobility and a transform cooldown. Sentinel Glaive is the classic thrown-glaive poke. Shooting Star plays the Fel Rush role as a speed burst. Avatar of Vengeance is the Metamorphosis moment, a transform that teleports you to your target and empowers you. Halt fills the Disrupt slot as the interrupt. All of it wraps in the Maiev-style Warden fantasy (Maiev's Discipline, Vigil of the Watchers). Secondary flavor comparison: the melee-weaving-with-instant-procs feel, meaning Aspect of the Warden's extra-attack chance plus Starsunder/Astral Blade as burst nukes fed by a mana pool, plays like old mana-based Enhancement Shaman.

Playstyle

Warden weaves two resources at once. Energy fuels the fillers, Celestial Strike in melee and Sentinel Glaive as a thrown ranged option, while a separate mana pool pays flat costs for utility spells and gets drained directly as a damage-scaling resource by Starsunder and Astral Blade, each of which eats a chunk of max mana. You spend big pieces of your mana bar to hit hard, then claw it back through Celestial Strike, Aspect of the Warden's proc layer, and Rally the Sentinels' mana-returning Sentinel Glaive. Open with Starsunder to build Scattered Stars, dump Energy into Celestial Strike, burn Astral Blade on cooldown (Infused Blades can reset and refund it, Stellar Amplification can make it free), and pop Shadowsong's Mandate to chain crits into Warden's Blade procs. Outside straight damage your toolkit value is Halt (a teleport that silences everything around you where you land), Bonds of Justice (a tether that grips a runner back to you), and Avatar of Vengeance (teleport to your target with an Intellect buff). You're a self-sufficient hybrid, not anyone's healer. Sentinel Glaive's ranged throw and Shooting Star's unslowable sprint make it decent at closing on or peeling off a single target while still doing damage.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   Sentinel Glaive throws at range, Shooting Star is an unslowable speed burst, Halt teleports and silences, and Bonds of Justice tethers and grips. You fight while repositioning, and runners do not get away.
*   Shadowsong's Mandate opens a crit window into Warden's Blade procs, Aspect of the Warden adds a bonus-attack chance, and mana-scaled Starsunder and Astral Blade land the damage with Infused Blades and Stellar Amplification refunding. You burst someone down with no outside support.
*   Halt's AoE-silence teleport, Vigil of the Watchers' raid-wide crit and Bonds of Justice give a group real value. You heal nobody.

Weaknesses

*   The spec kit has no heal. Touch of Moonlight is one mana-costly cast bought with a class pick, and it will not sustain you or a partner through real damage. Control and mobility are the whole survival plan.
*   The only defensive in the spec is the incidental unslowable window on Shooting Star. Vial of Moonwell Water is a trinket-style break usable while stunned or feared, at the price of a class pick, and there is no cleanse of any kind. Way of the Goddess only shortens harmful magic.
*   Starsunder and Astral Blade each drain a large chunk of max mana. If Celestial Strike, Aspect of the Warden procs and Rally the Sentinels do not refill it, your burst is locked out exactly when you need it.

Stat priority

Hit (melee special-attack cap (until your specials stop missing a +3-level target))›Expertise (until your attacks stop being dodged/parried, keeps Aspect of the Warden swings and Astral Blade/Sentinel Glaive landing)›Agility (Attack Power, crit)›Critical Strike (Shadowsong's Mandate proc engine, Scattered Stars crit application)›Spell Power (Astral Blade's dominant SP term)›Haste (more swings for Aspect of the Warden procs and faster Scattered Stars stacking)

Rotations

Solo / questing

1) Pre-pull: settle on Aspect of the Warden for grinding. Its bonus-attack proc is your baseline, and Aspect Mastery/Scattering Blades push its trigger rate. 2) Open with Sentinel Glaive while closing distance (thrown ranged damage; Rally the Sentinels makes it return mana and apply a Scattered Star). 3) Starsunder on entering melee (drains max mana, builds Scattered Stars). 4) Celestial Strike as your Energy dump on cooldown; it also returns mana. 5) Astral Blade on cooldown as a burst nuke, which Infused Blades can reset and refund. 6) Umbral Blade once you have 2-3 Scattered Stars stacked for the big consume payoff (AoE arcane damage). 7) Shadowsong's Mandate on cooldown during burst windows to chain Warden's Blade procs off crits. 8) Use Shooting Star to close gaps on runners or to disengage and kite when overextended, Bonds of Justice to grip a fleeing mob back, and Fan of Knives to spread Scattered Stars on 2-3 mob packs.

Dungeon

Priority is utility and damage, not healing. 1) Halt teleports you into a caster cluster and silences dangerous casts; it locks out everyone around where you land. 2) Bonds of Justice to yank a loose add off the healer or squishies. 3) Everything else, run the solo single-target rotation (Starsunder → Celestial Strike filler → Astral Blade/Umbral Blade on cooldown) on the kill target for damage contribution. Sentinel Glaive pokes a runner, Shooting Star intercepts a leashing or kiting mob, and Fan of Knives handles AoE Scattered-Star spread on packs. The spec has no heal, no cleanse, and no battle-rez, so it cannot cover any healer or dispel duty. It contributes damage, an AoE-silence teleport, control, and a raid-wide crit buff via Vigil of the Watchers.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Dual resource. An Energy pool fuels the moment-to-moment fillers, Celestial Strike and Sentinel Glaive. A separate mana pool pays flat percentage costs for utility spells like Halt, Shooting Star and Bonds of Justice, and gets drained directly as a damage-scaling cost by Starsunder and Astral Blade, each a large chunk of max mana. Mana comes back through Celestial Strike, Aspect of the Warden's proc layer, Rally the Sentinels' mana-returning Sentinel Glaive, and Umbral Blade's Scattered Star consumption, with Infused Blades occasionally refunding Astral Blade's full cost outright. Burn burst abilities back-to-back without weaving in those fillers and you will run dry.**

Difficulty**Two resource pools plus keeping Aspect of the Warden and its proc layer active is more bookkeeping than a single-resource melee spec carries. Sequence Shadowsong's Mandate into your Warden's Blade proc windows, stack Scattered Stars into a well-timed Umbral Blade, and read Halt, Shooting Star and Bonds of Justice as gap-closers or disengages depending on the fight. Mismanage the mana-drain abilities and you're stranded with no burst and no heal to fall back on, at the worst possible moment.**
