Retail feel

Closest to Preservation Evoker, mechanically more than thematically. The real parallel is structural. Cleansing Flames is a Living Flame / Dream Breath in miniature, one cast that heals allies or damages enemies depending who's in front of you. Phoenix Dive is a dash-shield like Rescue/Zephyr repositioning, and Inferno Barrier's absorb echoes Renewing Blaze/Obsidian Scales-style mitigation. The one thing retail Preservation doesn't have that this spec does is a hard cheat-death. That's Spirit of the Phoenix, closer to old Priest Spirit of Redemption territory, layered on top of the Evoker-shaped hybrid kit.

Playstyle

Flameweaving heals and fights off the same button bar. Kindle and Cinderheart are your cast-time heals, Ember Touch is an instant Ember-spender burst heal that goes right through a kick, and Cleansing Flames damages enemies and heals allies caught in the same frontal spread. DPSing and healing a group skirmish are often literally the same cast. On the damage side you weave Flare Bolt, which builds Flamecasting stacks toward haste and crit, with Lava Shard, feeding a Heat/Ember economy that funds your Ember-spender heals. The cooldown layer is defensive and Phoenix-flavored rather than a CC lockdown kit: Inferno Barrier's absorb, Phoenix Dive's dash-and-shield, and the Phoenix Egg / Roaring Pyre / Kael's Command healing zones, backed by passive death insurance in Spirit of the Phoenix. Positioning is the skill test. Cleansing Flames only pays off if you've lined it through allies and enemies correctly, and your Phoenix zones only heal the people standing in them.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   Spirit of the Phoenix is a cheat-death once per 10 minutes, sitting behind Inferno Barrier absorbs and Shielded by Flames shielding on crits. Finishing you off is one of the hardest jobs in the game, and that is the backbone of 1vX.
*   Cleansing Flames heals your side and hits theirs in one frontal cast. No coordination required.
*   Burn Impurities cleanses magic and disease, Neltharion's Resolve wards the party against a fear, and Warmth passively heals your lowest ally. Roaring Pyre, Phoenix Egg and Kael's Command blanket the group in passive healing. None of it needs a partner to set up.

Weaknesses

*   Kindle, Cinderheart and Cleansing Flames are all cast-time and fold to an interrupt chain. A rogue on you shuts them off before your absorbs can bail you out. Only Ember Touch, an instant Ember-spender, keeps flowing through a kick.
*   Flame Step is instant, dashes 20 yards and frees you from stuns and bonds, but it is one 20-second cooldown and your only escape. Caught with it down under a stunlock you are on Inferno Barrier absorbs and the once-per-10-minute Spirit of the Phoenix. Everything else is peel.
*   Inferno Barrier, Phoenix Dive and the Roaring Pyre and Kael's Command healing zones are all cooldown-gated, and once spent sustain falls back to cast-time heal spam. Phoenix Egg is the exception, with no cooldown beyond the one-active-Phoenix cap, so it stays up as passive healing.

Stat priority

Spirit›Intellect›Critical Strike›Spell Power›Haste

Rotations

Solo / questing

Open at range with Flare Bolt as the main filler; it builds Flamecasting stacks toward haste and crit. Weave Lava Shard on cooldown for the hard hit, and Cleansing Flames when a mob is lined up in front of you so the same cast damages it and heals you. On multi-mob pulls, drop a Phoenix Egg or Roaring Pyre so its zone tops you off passively while you burn the pack. Self-heal with Kindle/Cinderheart (and Ember Touch when you have an Ember to spend) between pulls rather than sitting to drink; they keep you moving. Use your control to manage a bad pull: Cindergrip roots a runner, Gaze of Ysera sleeps an extra mob, and Circle of Fire / Pulverizespout lock down a clump standing in your Path of Flames. When one goes wrong, lean on absorbs (Inferno Barrier) and the cheat-death.

Dungeon

Pre-pull: nothing to hand out but your own buffs. Flamecasting stacks build as you cast, and Emberheart can give the group a movement-speed burst before a pull. Sustain the tank with Kindle/Cinderheart as your mana-efficient heals, banking Embers for bigger Ember Touch top-offs. Cast Burn Impurities proactively on any poison/disease/magic-heavy pull; it clears a harmful magic and a disease effect. Drop Roaring Pyre or Phoenix Egg / Kael's Command under the group on sustained AoE pulls for passive raid-wide healing while your hands are free for damage. When a pull goes loud, lay down Cleansing Flames through the pack to hybrid-heal the party and hit adds at once, and reserve Inferno Barrier for the tank's next big spike. Neltharion's Resolve pre-wards the party against a known fear. Spirit of the Phoenix is your once-per-fight insurance against a lethal spike.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Mana, layered on a Heat/Ember economy. Damage and heal casts generate Heat that fuels Embers, which pay for Ember spenders like Ember Touch. Ashen Priest smooths the mana curve: a share of your mana regeneration continues in combat, and your critical heals turn into Replenishment for the group. Spammable Kindle keeps you mana-positive at baseline. Stack cooldown-heavy heals back-to-back without letting a regen window catch up and you'll bottom out.**

Difficulty**Spamming Kindle and Flare Bolt gets you competent healing and damage output. The demanding part is a positioning-and-resource puzzle: Flamecasting proc stacks tracked, the Heat/Ember economy fed so your Ember Touch top-offs are available, Cleansing Flames aimed so the frontal spread threads allies for the heal and enemies for the damage at once, and your Phoenix zones — Roaring Pyre, Phoenix Egg, Kael's Command — dropped where the group will stand rather than where you wish they would.**
