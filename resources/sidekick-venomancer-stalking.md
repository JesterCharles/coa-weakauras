Retail feel

Closest to retail Feral Druid, in a Cat duelist/brawler build. Both are shapeshift-gated melee specs running a builder/finisher economy that rarely runs dry: Energy plus Brood Marks here, Energy plus Combo Points there. Both lean on bleed and DoT finishers for damage (Facemelter/Rotfang/Withering Venom vs Rip/Rake). Both have a hard execute at low target HP (Widowmaker's healing-reduction hit vs Ferocious Bite). Both open from stealth, with Skulk mirroring Prowl. The gap is defensive. Feral has real defensive tools (Bear Form armor, Survival Instincts) and anti-CC. Stalking has essentially none: no stun-break, no root or snare break, no immunity. It is far more vulnerable to being locked down than a Feral.

Playstyle

You shapeshift into Spider Form and fight on Energy plus Brood Marks, a combo-point-like resource. Venom Fang applies a Brood Mark, and the finishers (Facemelter, Rotfang, Widowmaker) consume Marks for scaling damage. Run Blight Venom as your active venom and let Vulnerability (an auto-attack chance while Blight Venom is up) and Vile Fangs (periodic damage) auto-generate application and Marks while you weave Venom Fang and Nerubian Sting into the Mark-consuming finishers. Widowmaker is a healing-reduction execute that ramps below 35% HP via Black Widow. Skulk (stealth) makes Venom Fang free and hard-hitting for openers, and Bugging rewards striking from Skulk with bonus damage. Every 3rd Venom Fang becomes Widow's Kiss for extra damage and self-haste. Energy relief comes from Blistering Fangs and Death Spray refunds rather than positional tricks, and the Serpent Lord augment can give some Spider Form casts a short cast time and extra range if you want a soft ranged option. Self-sustain is thin. You are pressure and execute, not a leech-tank.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   Withering Venom, Rotfang and Facemelter grind a target down, and Widowmaker's healing-reduction hit ramps under 35% through Black Widow to seal the kill.
*   Vulnerability auto-applies Blight Venom, Vile Fangs and Skulk feed Marks and free casts, and Blistering Fangs and Death Spray refund Energy on spenders. The economy rarely runs dry.
*   Spindlebind roots a chaser or a kill target on two charges, and the Serpent Lord augment can give a few Spider Form casts a short cast time and extra range.

Weaknesses

*   No stun-break, no root or snare break, no fear or slow immunity. One well-timed hard-CC chain takes you out of the fight entirely.
*   Fully melee-committed, and the Serpent Lord augment adds only a little range. A kiter outside Spindlebind range largely ignores you.
*   Web Wrap, Impale and Arachnophobia are one-shot answers on real cooldowns, and Nullifying Toxin locks only one spell school. Past Envenomed Weapons' party weapon-poison there is no group buff and no cleanse of any kind. Two full-health opponents cannot be kept shut down at once.

Stat priority

Spell Hit (until your spells stop missing a +3-level target — the core bites Nerubian Sting, Widowmaker, Facemelter, Withering Venom are Shadow/Nature/Plague school and must land)›Spell Power (direct +SP coefficients on those bites)›Intellect (Prowler converts Intellect into spell damage AND hit rating; Deadly Venom/Precision Bites convert it into crit rating)›Haste (Skitter Smack/Acidfang stack melee haste, speeding rage generation and proc uptime)›Critical Strike (Boding Dread/Glory To Shadra Shadow/Nature spell crit)›Hit/Expertise (keeps auto-attack procs like Drenched Fangs landing)›Armor Penetration

Rotations

Solo / questing

Pre-pull: set Blight Venom as your active venom and, if available, open from Skulk for a free, hard-hitting Venom Fang. Build Brood Marks with Venom Fang. Weave Nerubian Sting on cooldown for its Shadow DoT and its stacking damage-amp on the target (Nature Against Nature buffs your Widowmaker/Venom Fang per stack). Spend high-Mark finishers, Facemelter (big Nature DoT) and Rotfang, for damage, letting Withering Venom ramp its DoT alongside. Keep Vulnerability applying Blight Venom passively off auto-attacks. Once the target drops under 35%, Widowmaker for the healing-reduction execute; it consumes Marks for scaling damage and hits harder below the threshold via Black Widow. On adds, use Spindlebind to root one while you finish the other, and pop Noxious Empowerment for an Energy-regen/damage burst window.

Dungeon

Pre-pull: nothing spec-unique to hand out. Stalking is a pure DPS spec with no group buff. On pull: use Spindlebind to root an add the tank is not holding yet, and Brood Lord's webs to slow spenders' targets if adds are spreading. Run your normal Energy/Brood-Mark rotation (Venom Fang to Nerubian Sting to Facemelter/Rotfang on Marks to Widowmaker on execute-range adds) for damage, and use Contagion's periodic-damage bursts on stacked trash for extra AoE. Hold Noxious Empowerment for burst windows on a boss or a big pack. Note this spec has no battle-rez, interrupt, or group cleanse of its own.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Energy plus Brood Marks power the melee rotation while in Spider Form. Venom Fang applies Marks and your finishers consume them: Facemelter, Rotfang, Widowmaker. Energy regenerates in combat off baseline regen, Bile Eruption's Venom procs, Noxious Empowerment's regen boost, Blistering Fangs' and Death Spray's refunds, and Skulk making Venom Fang free. The damage rotation rarely goes dry mid-fight. A separate mana pool fuels the utility and mobility layer, and that side can run dry if you spam it heavily in a long fight without downtime to regen.**

Difficulty**Vulnerability applies Blight Venom passively, so builders into finishers on cooldown already produces solid DPS with almost nothing tracked. The version that takes multitasking: Brood Marks banked for a bigger Facemelter or Rotfang payout, Nerubian Sting's damage-amp stacks and Widow's Kiss's haste kept rolling, Spindlebind and Brood Lord webs used as fight-swinging tools rather than panic buttons, and Widowmaker's execute timed.**
