Retail feel

Closest to Augmentation Evoker. Like Aug, its identity is built around empowering allies' output: the Horns' haste and crit auras, Battle Screech's attack/spell-power empower, Battle Prowess's party hit, Command Aura's shared bonus damage, Defense of the Ancients' armor, and Frenzy's haste. It still does meaningful personal ranged damage on its own Advantage builder/finisher loop, with only minor incidental healing rather than a real HPS rotation. That is exactly Aug's Ebon Might/Prescience-buffs-plus-chip-damage-plus-a-little-healing profile, not anything resembling a Discipline/Holy/Resto kit.

Load leveling build at level **60**

Playstyle

Core loop is a Focus-and-Advantage rotation off a Bow or Crossbow. Quick Shot builds Advantage and is the hook for Forest Fighter's and the Falconstrike transformation, where every 5th Quick Shot becomes Falconstrike. Then dump the bank into a finisher: Skullpiercer for the hardest hit (scales per Advantage stack), Emerald Arrow for Nature burst (also scales per stack), Whipvine Arrow when you need Nature damage plus a root on the move, or Woodland Arrow when you want the Horn cooldown refund. Layered on top: Toxic Dart and Quel'dorei Poison for DoT pressure, Falconstrike/Falcon's Call/Falcon Diving to summon War Falcons and Dragonhawks (temporary pet-like adds that also feed Wingman's stacking damage reduction), and Thalassian Brand to mark a priority target, which grants your allies bonus ranged attack power against it and ticks Nature damage. On a shared cooldown you drop a Horn, Horn of War for crit or Horn of Alacrity for haste, to buff the party. Woodland Arrow and the passive Blow the Horns! chip that cooldown back down, so a skilled Ranger keeps a Horn up far more than the raw cooldown suggests. Self-sustain is passive and proc-based: Dream Flowers spawn off crits and heal and speed whoever runs over them, Phoenix Plumes ties a Focus-and-Falcon proc to your finishers, and Survival Potion is your panic button.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   Falcon's Call slows a group and brings Falcon adds, Whipvine Arrow roots, and Freedom cleanses roots and snares off the group. Enough to peel a train off a partner by yourself.
*   Phoenix Plumes returns Focus and a Falcon off finishers, Dream Flowers heals and speeds you on crits, and Wingman cuts damage taken per Falcon. No external healer needed.
*   Backstep, Bushcraft Glider and Falcon Guide's speed proc make you hard to pin down.

Weaknesses

*   No CC-break, no immunity. You answer CC by landing yours first and kiting, which does nothing about a stunlock opener.
*   Skullpiercer, Emerald Arrow and the Whipvine and Woodland Arrow finishers scale with the Advantage stacks you spend, so damage is backloaded and thin in the opening seconds.
*   Dream Flowers and Phoenix Plumes only fire off crits and finisher hits. Against a burst opener the healing is not there on turn one, which is when you need it.

Stat priority

Hit Rating (to land proc/utility triggers)›Agility›Critical Strike›Haste›Armor Penetration

Rotations

Solo / questing

Quick Shot to build Advantage. Dump into Skullpiercer (hits hardest at full stacks) or Emerald Arrow (Nature burst that also scales per stack) as your default finisher. Open tougher fights with Toxic Dart and Quel'dorei Poison for DoT pressure, and Thalassian Brand on elites (ally-RAP mark + Nature DoT). Swap to Whipvine Arrow as your finisher when you need to keep moving; it also roots. On 2-3 mob pulls, open with Falcon's Call (AoE slow + Falcon summons), or let Falcon Diving spawn Falcons on kills to cleave the group before finishing normally. Sustain with Dream Flowers and Phoenix Plumes procs rather than sitting to drink. Backstep repositions you on a hit-and-run kill.

Dungeon

Pre-pull, drop the Horn suited to the pack: Horn of War for crit on a burst pull, Horn of Alacrity for haste to speed the group up. Layer Battle Screech (attack/spell power), Battle Prowess (party hit), Defense of the Ancients (armor), or Frenzy (haste) as the pull demands. Then chain Woodland Arrow during the pull to shave the Horn cooldown back down (Blow the Horns! shortens it too) so a buff is active on nearly every pull. Keep running your normal Quick Shot-to-finisher rotation for personal DPS throughout. Drink Survival Potion yourself when eating poison or disease damage. Whipvine Arrow's root (or Freedom on the group) peels a breakaway add off the healer, and Falcon's Call slows and pins a dangerous caster or add. Let Dream Flowers and Falcon/Dragonhawk adds provide passive off-heal and threat-free extra damage.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Focus is primary and regenerates passively. Cunning boosts that, Horn of Alacrity adds self-Focus-per-second while active, and Phoenix Plumes restores Focus on a 5-Advantage finisher. You spend it on shots, finishers and utility like Whipvine Arrow, Falcon's Call and Backstep. Advantage is the combo-point-style secondary, built primarily by Quick Shot and exhausted by finishers for scaling bonus damage and effect: Skullpiercer, Whipvine Arrow, Woodland Arrow, Emerald Arrow. Extended Horn and utility spam can run Focus dry, but Cunning, Horn of Alacrity and Phoenix Plumes largely keep it topped up in normal play. Advantage is the more common bottleneck to burst timing, since finishers scale with stacks spent.**

Difficulty**Build Advantage, dump a finisher. Simple, forgiving, and nowhere near the whole spec. The strong version runs a Horn-cooldown-reduction chain — Woodland Arrow plus the Blow the Horns! passive and the Falconstrike-every-5th-Quick-Shot cadence — until the party buff is near-permanent. Wingman stacks off multiple Falcons and Dragonhawks. And Survival Potion and your defensive Falcon layer are the two things average players spend early and strong players bank for the burst window when it comes.**
