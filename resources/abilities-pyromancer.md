# Pyromancer — ability inventory

Specs: **Incineration** damage, **Flameweaving** healing, **Draconic** damage. (`resources/spec-roles.md`, observed in game.)

This class heals. A healing spec earns the target band — your HoTs and absorbs
on your CURRENT target, glowing when one needs a refresh — and its main row is a
healing rotation. Raid frames stay out; VuhDo/Grid own that job on 3.3.5a.

What renders, where, and as what. Generated by
`tools/mkabilities.py pyromancer`; **hand edits are preserved** on refresh.

Seeded from the class skillbook (`coa-pyromancer-skills.json`), not the cooldown audit —
an ability with no cooldown has no audit row, which is how Reverse Wound,
Arc Collision and Waves of Time were missed. The audit only enriches now.

Role priority: main > offensive > defensive > utility. An ability that could be
two things takes the first that applies.

A Notes cell starting `seed:` is a row the seeder wrote and nobody has
reviewed. Delete the prefix once you have placed the ability.

Role and ID both take per-spec overrides, `<default>; <spec>:<value>`:
`defensive; time:offensive` renders one ability in two different rows, and
`520188; time:801280` covers the case where one NAME is two different
spells — the specs that have no override use the default.
