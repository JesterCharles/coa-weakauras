Retail feel

Closest to Elemental Shaman. The Static/Conductive dual-currency system is a more granular reskin of Ele's Maelstrom mechanic: build a stacking resource off filler casts (Lightning Bolt/Lava Burst ≈ Brine), then dump it into a hard finisher (Earth Shock/Earthquake ≈ Torrential Wrath/Deluge). Stormflow fills the Lava Beam channel role, and Stormcloud plus Torrential Wrath play like an Icefury/Ascendance burst window. The utility half is where it diverges. Mystic Thunder (interrupt-into-silence), Eye of the Storm (mark, tick, then mass-root) and Drown (a healing-reduction debuff that unleashes a burst at expiry) are Enhancement/Discipline-style tools grafted onto a nuker. Net effect: Elemental Shaman's rotation and burst windows, with a control-and-debuff utility layer bolted on and a self-heal leech in Blessing of Lei Shen. There's no healer-grade throughput and no ally heal underneath.

Playstyle

You run two parallel currencies. Static is a combo-style resource generated by the baseline builder Brine (Shock transformed via Tempest Sovereign) and spent by the big spells: Deluge and Stormfury each drain 50, Stormflow costs 20, and Thunder Ward drains it entirely. Separately, the passive Conductive makes Brine grant a stack of Conductive. Your finisher, Torrential Wrath (Call Lightning transformed via Tempest Sovereign), consumes the whole Conductive stack at once to trigger Conduction, a proc that scales up per stack consumed. The loop: spam Brine to build both currencies, drop into the Stormflow channel on cooldown (during which Brine casts instantly and hits harder via Unbridled Flow, and Blessing of Lei Shen heals you for its damage), then dump a fully-stacked Torrential Wrath for the Conduction payoff. Packs get cleared with Deluge, which scales with Conductive consumed, and the Stormfury zone. It carries no ally heal. Blessing of Lei Shen turns your Stormflow channel into a self-sustain leech, and survivability otherwise leans on Thunder Ward, a full physical and spell immunity, rather than any repeatable healing.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   Deluge, the Stormfury zone and Eye of the Storm hold a chokepoint and shred a clumped group. BG teamfights swing on it.
*   Thunder Ward is full physical and spell immunity. It eats an entire opening burst.
*   Mystic Thunder interrupts into a silence, Drown cuts healing and detonates late, and Eye of the Storm mass roots. Stormflow's self-leech runs underneath all of it.

Weaknesses

*   No ally heal at all, and Blessing of Lei Shen's leech on Stormflow is modest. Survival is Thunder Ward, so sustained pressure or a second attacker arriving after it drops kills you.
*   No dedicated escape, no root-break. A spec that stands still to channel Stormflow and bank Conductive is easy to peel and kite.
*   Torrential Wrath, Deluge and Stormflow all have to line up with a full Conductive and Static bank. Between those windows you are easy to kite or all-in.

Rotations

Solo / questing

Open with Brine spam to build Static and stack Conductive. Once Conductive is capped, spend it through Torrential Wrath for the Conduction burst. Use it on cooldown once banked. Disciple of Thorim cuts its cast time. Weave in the Stormflow channel whenever it's off cooldown for your main single-target damage. Keep instant-casting Brine during the channel for the bonus damage, and note Blessing of Lei Shen heals you for the channel's damage. Use Drown on tougher single mobs to cut their healing and set up the burst it unleashes at expiry. Against packs, swap to Deluge and/or the Stormfury zone to consume Conductive across the group instead of single-target Torrential Wrath. Save Thunder Ward for a moment you'd otherwise take a big hit, and lean on the Stormflow leech rather than any ally heal to stay topped between pulls.

Dungeon

Be honest about the gap: this kit has no ally heal at all, only a self-leech via Blessing of Lei Shen — it cannot fill a dedicated healer slot. Its dungeon rotation is damage and control. Pre-pull, use Eye of the Storm to cluster and root adds for the group. During the fight, run Mystic Thunder on cooldown to interrupt-and-silence a dangerous caster. Drop the Stormfury zone and Deluge on trash to burn packs and consume Conductive. Use Stormcloud into a full-Conductive Torrential Wrath as your priority-target execute. Your passive group contribution is Conductor In Charge, whose aura raises party/raid damage. There is no active group-haste cooldown, so don't plan a burst window around one. Thunder Ward is self-only. Treat this spec in dungeons as a damage-dealer with an interrupt and a debuff-control kit riding shotgun, not as the group's healer.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Hybrid dual-currency. Base mana gates every cast, and two combo-style meters sit under it. Static is built by Brine, spent in chunks by Stormflow at 20 and Deluge/Stormfury at 50, and fully drained by Thunder Ward. Conductive builds one stack per Brine and is consumed wholesale by Torrential Wrath for the Conduction payoff. It goes dry on extended fights. The kit has no meaningful mana-return, so without downtime to regen, sustained encounters drain the mana bar even while Static and Conductive stay banked. The only health return is the Blessing of Lei Shen leech on your Stormflow channel, and that isn't enough to sustain through real pressure.**

Difficulty**Brine generator spam into the finisher at full still does reasonable damage. Two independent resources sit underneath it: Static thresholds decide which spender you can afford, Conductive stack count decides what Conduction is worth. Stormcloud and Drown want to land right before Torrential Wrath. Instant Brine weaves into the Stormflow channel without clipping it, and cashes its leech on the way. Thunder Ward pressed a second late is Thunder Ward wasted.**
