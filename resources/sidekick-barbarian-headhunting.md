Retail feel

Closest to retail Survival Hunter with a Feral Druid's survivability bolted on. Like Survival, it's a melee/ranged hybrid built around thrown weapons and bleeds: Throw Weapon, Barbed Spear, Javelin Toss and Impaling Spear ≈ the Wildfire-Bomb/Raptor-adjacent kit, and Berserker Rush's charge-root-then-leap-back ≈ Harpoon. It runs on a rogue-style resource, Energy, rather than mana. The self-healing goes heavier than Survival ever gets. The Born in Blood HoT off your own crits (Furious Berserker, Vengeance For Zul'jin), made Agility-scaling and crit-capable by Brutal Form, reads much more like Feral Druid's heal-through-damage-dealt survivability model layered on the Hunter-style kit.

Playstyle

You run on Energy and weave two damage streams: ranged thrown weapons (Throw Weapon, Barbed Spear, Javelin Toss via Sen'jin's Guidance, plus the Impaling Spear line) and melee Berserker Axe swings while Enraged. Puncture Wounds turns Spear critical strikes into stacking bleeds. Spear Thrower and You Want Axe? let Throw Weapon reset your Spears and refund Berserker Axe charges, so damage ramps the longer you stay on a target. Axe Twirling is your burst button, an Enrage that makes Throw Weapon, Gutspiller and Berserker Axe strike additional nearby enemies. Survival is self-generated rather than granted. Furious Berserker off Barbed Spear crits and Vengeance For Zul'jin off Berserker Rush hand you the Born in Blood heal-over-time, which Brutal Form lets scale with Agility and critically heal, and Bloody Fighter/Savage make it stack and hit harder.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   Barbed Spear crits and Berserker Rush self-generate the Born in Blood HoT through Furious Berserker and Vengeance For Zul'jin. Brutal Form makes it scale with Agility and crit. You rarely need a healer.
*   Puncture Wounds stacks bleeds off Spear crits and Gutspiller feeds more on top. It is a damage floor that keeps ticking while you both move.
*   Berserker Rush root-charges in and leaps back out; Spite bursts and flings you a long way. You pick the range, which pure melee cannot. Manhunter hands the party a passive crit buff.

Weaknesses

*   Nothing breaks or shortens a stun, root, or snare. A hard-CC chain lands in full.
*   Savage Revelry's health-and-Energy restore is your only quick button, since Ancestral Roar is enrage-gated and costs a class-pool pick. Everything else is the Born in Blood HoT, so burst that catches you before it is rolling finds a normal HP pool.
*   Armor ignore only happens inside windows. Berserker Axe and Hunting for Sport bypass armor while Enraged or during Unbridled Rage, so your penetration comes and goes with rotation state.

Stat priority

Hit (until your specials stop missing a +3-level target)›Expertise (until your attacks stop being dodged/parried)›Agility›Critical Strike›Armor Penetration›Haste

Rotations

Solo / questing

Open at range with Javelin Toss/Throw Weapon to get Puncture Wounds bleeding off your Spear crits. Then close to melee and weave Berserker Axe while Enraged. Berserker Rush closes distance (root + leap-back), and Impaling Spear is a frontal-line bleed via Spear Shards. Pop Axe Twirling on cooldown against tougher single targets or packs; it makes Throw Weapon/Gutspiller/Berserker Axe cleave. Fire Unbridled Rage to grant Energy via Raging Spirit and enable your armor-ignore window (Hunting for Sport). Priority: maintain Puncture Wounds uptime (Spear crits) > Berserker Rush on cooldown for mobility + a Born in Blood grant > Axe Twirling on multi-mob pulls or tough singles > Barbed Spear to feed Furious Berserker's Born in Blood > Berserker during Enrage burst windows > filler Throw Weapon/Berserker Axe.

Dungeon

Pull: Berserker Rush (root opener + gap closer) or an Impaling Spear line into the pull, tagging with Axe Twirling for group-wide bleed spread if multiple adds. Sustain: keep Barbed Spear crits and Berserker Rush feeding your Born in Blood HoT so you self-heal through trash and movement phases. That rides under your normal Puncture Wounds/Throw Weapon/Berserker Axe damage rotation. Manhunter passively raises the whole party's crit chance, so position to keep it up. Emergency: Unbridled Rage (via Raging Spirit) bridges an Energy gap and opens the armor-ignore window. For recovery, lean on the Born in Blood HoT and Brutal Form's Agility-scaled crit heals. There is no instant self-heal button, so keep the HoT rolling proactively.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Energy, rogue-style. It self-regulates rather than acting as a hard limiter. Axe Twirling opens a cleave and Enrage burst window. Your Power Tosser-boosted thrown attacks, Throw Weapon and Barbed Spear, keep pressure up between swings. You Want Axe? discounts your next casts after a Spear while Enraged, and Raging Spirit hands you Energy on Unbridled Rage while removing Spirit-spell cost. Skip those generation and discount tools and a long uninterrupted fight can get tight, but the layered regen means you essentially never go fully dry.**

Difficulty**The melee/ranged weave feeds Born in Blood, and that covers most of the sustain without thought. Sequencing is where it gets interesting. Bank Axe Twirling for the burst window instead of spending it reflexively. Berserker Rush's root and leap-back does double duty as engage and exit. Keep Puncture Wounds bleeds and the Born in Blood HoT rolling ahead of the damage rather than after it, and line up You Want Axe? and Unbridled Rage so your Berserker Axe charges and armor-ignore land inside burst.**
