Retail feel

Closest to Restoration Shaman. The stationary ground-zone Beacons map almost one-to-one onto a totem kit. Restorative Beacon ≈ Healing Rain, Battery Recharge Station ≈ Mana Tide/Mana Spring, the ZIGGI-6K zone ≈ a healing totem, and Alarm Beacon ≈ Tremor Totem, since via Cybernetic Revivification it dispels fear, charm and sleep. Repair Shot plus Nanobot Reconstruction is a direct-heal-plus-HoT pairing very much like Riptide, and My Greatest Invention! reads as a scaled-down Healing Tide Totem-style AoE cooldown. It diverges from Shaman by bolting on a splash-heal augment layer (Stim Augmentation spreading your direct heals) and a battery/recharge economy (Battery Recharge Station, Overcharge) that Restoration Shaman doesn't have.

Playstyle

Invention Tinker heals by blending a direct 2-sec-cast heal with a long-range instant. Repair Shot is the cast one, and its formula scales off both healing power and a small attack-power term. Zap! scales with Repair Shot modifiers and is guaranteed to crit allies wearing Nanobots. On top of that you drop ground-based Beacon zones, Restorative Beacon and the ZIGGI-6K deployable, which heal a raid-and-party area much like a Shaman's totems. Nanobot abilities are your maintenance layer: Nanobot Reconstruction ticks a HoT, Nanobot Cleanser strips poison and disease, and Nanobot Swarm sends threat-redirecting damage nanobots. Rather than reactive overhealing, it front-loads utility and banks big AoE cooldowns (Med Pack, My Greatest Invention!) for panic windows. It also carries offense and utility on the same GCD economy: an interrupt and school lockout in Build: Noise Box, a mana-and-health refill station in Battery Recharge Station, and a gun-flavored augment in Stim Augmentation that makes your direct heals splash onto extra allies. Overcharge and Beacon Charging supercharge those deployed beacons, so you switch constantly between the direct-heal seat and the deployable-management seat.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   Cybernetic Revivification makes the Alarm Beacon pulse away fear, charm and sleep. Allies keep functioning through an enemy CC chain.
*   Repair Shot, Zap! guaranteed-critting on Nanobot'd allies, the Nanobot Reconstruction HoT, Med Pack and My Greatest Invention! all stack onto one save. The target does not die.
*   Restorative Beacon and the ZIGGI-6K sustain a whole group's health while a self-dropped Battery Recharge Station refills your mana. No downtime.

Weaknesses

*   Repair Shot is a 2-second cast that fires a Round from your gun. Under heavy pushback or focus you are tempo-starved on your core direct heal until Zap! and beacon charges come back up.
*   Nothing reduces physical damage and there is no personal absorb. Melee hits you for full.
*   Beacon charges and their Overcharge and Beacon Charging supercharge windows, Nanobot cooldowns and the Battery Recharge Station mana economy all have to be sequenced correctly under pressure.

Rotations

Solo / questing

Invention is a healer spec, so soloing is slow and attrition-based rather than bursty. Pull with your gun's Scrap-style ranged attack, then lean on your Nanobot layer to survive: Nanobot Reconstruction as a rolling self-HoT, Nanobot Cleanser to clear poison and disease, and Self-Sustaining Technology, which heals you from your pet's and your own damage and healing. Send Nanobot Swarm to redirect threat and chip the target. Top yourself off with Repair Shot or an instant Zap! between exchanges, and drop a Battery Recharge Station to refill mana instead of resting. Let your ZIGGI-6K and any summons do the killing while you stay topped.

Dungeon

Pre-pull: drop the ZIGGI-6K or a Restorative Beacon at the pull spot, and pre-apply Nanobot Reconstruction to the tank. Open sustain with Repair Shot on the tank and Zap! for instant long-range top-offs, guaranteed to crit anyone wearing Nanobots. Spend Overcharge to supercharge your beacons via Beacon Charging as they line up. Drop Restorative Beacon or ZIGGI-6K for AoE zone sustain on a long or AoE-heavy pull. Cleanse debuffs immediately with Nanobot Cleanser, and interrupt casters with Build: Noise Box. Hold Med Pack and My Greatest Invention! as your emergency and AoE cooldowns. Refill mana between pulls at a self-dropped Battery Recharge Station.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Mana-based, with percent-of-base costs throughout. Spirit/MP5 regenerates it, and a self-dropped Battery Recharge Station tops you up on demand. Overcharge, cheapened and shortened by Mana Capacitors, is your active recharge lever for the beacons. J27 Upgrade and Master Builder cut the cost of Nanobots and Build abilities respectively. Without Battery Recharge Station uptime you go mana-dry on a long fight. Managing that station and your Overcharge windows is the economy. There's no separate ammo pool to track.**

Difficulty**Repair Shot plus Nanobot Reconstruction is a serviceable single-target heal loop and not much more. The multitasking is what's demanding: shared beacon charges and their Overcharge and Beacon Charging supercharge windows, the mana economy around Battery Recharge Station, and the burn-or-hold call on Med Pack and My Greatest Invention! — all while you're also the utility caster running Noise Box interrupts, Nanobot Cleanser dispels and Nanobot Swarm threat redirects.**
