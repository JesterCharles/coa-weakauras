Retail feel

Closest to Elemental Shaman crossed with a proc-driven nuker like Fire Mage. The Static build-and-spend loop is a Maelstrom-style resource: build with fillers (Volt, Forked Lightning) and dump into a hard finisher (Arm of Thorim ≈ Earth Shock/Earthquake), with Lightning Cage and Storm Ascendance playing the Ascendance-style burst-window role. Its identity is a pure ranged spell-DPS that punishes with scaling nukes and stuns, not a support. It isn't empty-handed for a group. Body of Lightning is an ally mana battery, Electrifying Aura and Greater Call of the Lightning are party-wide buffs, and Stormcloak is a self-only mitigation barrier, so there is no ally shield or absorb anywhere in this kit. Unlike a healer spec (Resto Druid, Resto Shaman, Holy Paladin, Mistweaver) it has no HoT, direct heal or absorb of any kind. It cannot top someone's HP back up at all. Slot it accordingly: offense first and last, with that utility as garnish rather than a role.

Playstyle

Build-then-spend on a secondary resource called Static. Generators are Volt's DoT ticks (2 Static each), Forked Lightning on-hit (5 Static per enemy struck, on a shared charge/recharge), and Electrocute, your hard-hitting execute-window nuke. You spend Static on Call Lightning, the cheap spammable filler, or Arm of Thorim, which depletes ALL banked Static for a scaling nuke that grows with how much you dump into it and stuns the target. That's a real risk/reward button. Around that you layer Conjure Storm for AoE zone damage, Volt as a standing slow and DoT on one target, and Lightning Cage as a channeled burst window that generates and spends Static in place. Cooldown stacking is where the ceiling lives. Storm Ascendance and its Lord of Lightning bonus form your burst window, Titanstorm shaves Arm of Thorim and Lightning Cage cooldowns whenever you cast Call Lightning or Electrocute, and Critical Circuit refunds 10 Static on crits with your Static spenders. Standing still matters too: Electrical Charge stacks haste every second you hold position, eventually letting your spells stop depleting. Mana is a constraint since almost every spell costs a chunk of base mana, so pacing generator/spender cycles against mana ticks matters as much as the Static math.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   The Static build-and-spend loop feeds Call Lightning filler or the scaling, stunning Arm of Thorim, and Storm Ascendance, Lord of Lightning and Lightning Cage open the burst windows. Real spike damage to swing a skirmish.
*   Volt is a snare and DoT that locks onto one target and needs no follow-up GCDs, good for protecting a ranged partner or running someone down. Ride the Lightning blinks you forward and damages a whole pack in the path.
*   Arm of Thorim stuns as it hits, extendable through High Voltage, so your hardest button also buys a free beat. Conjure Storm covers pack fights and BG chokes.

Weaknesses

*   No healing and no ally support anywhere. Electrifying Aura's passive crit is all a group gets, and you cannot restore health to yourself or an ally. Not dying is mitigation and kiting.
*   Storm Barrier cuts magic damage while Lightning Cage is up, Storm Ascendance adds more, and Tome of Lightning shortens interrupts and silences. Nothing heals and nothing breaks a stun, so once the windows are down and you are focused there is no clawing health back.
*   Nearly every ability costs mana, and Arm of Thorim empties the whole Static meter after wanting a big bank first. Dump it at the wrong moment and the damage is wasted; extended fights run the mana pool dry regardless.

Stat priority

Spell Hit Rating (until your spells stop missing a +3-level target)›Spell Power›Critical Strike Rating›Haste Rating›Intellect›Spirit/MP5

Rotations

Solo / questing

Open at range with Volt (snare + DoT, 2 Static/tick) to start free damage while closing or kiting. Weave Forked Lightning whenever charges are up for cleave and Static (5 per enemy struck). Drop Conjure Storm on any pack of 2+ mobs for the AoE initial hit plus DoT zone. Spend banked Static into Call Lightning, your cheap fast filler spender, as it comes up. Lean on Electrocute to close a target out, especially in its execute window. Save the full Static-dump Arm of Thorim for the toughest single target or an elite, banking as much Static as you can first so the nuke lands big and the stun buys you a free beat. Pop Lightning Cage for a channeled burst window against a tough target, and use Ride the Lightning to blink through a pack or reposition. There is no self-heal, so if a pull goes bad, kite on Volt and disengage rather than trying to trade through it.

Dungeon

Keep Electrifying Aura active; its passive party/raid crit buff is your only group contribution beyond damage. On every pull, apply Volt to your primary target for the slow and DoT, and use it to peel any add breaking toward your healer or ranged. Drop Conjure Storm on trash packs for AoE plus the Wicked Storms damage amp. Run your normal priority: Forked Lightning for Static and cleave, Call Lightning as filler spender, Electrocute in the execute window, Arm of Thorim as your big Static dump with a stun. Save Storm Ascendance/Lord of Lightning and Lightning Cage windows for burst or execute phases. Titanstorm keeps Arm of Thorim and Lightning Cage rolling as you cast Call Lightning and Electrocute, so keep those two flowing to compress your big-hit cooldowns. Treat yourself purely as ranged DPS. Your value to the group is damage and the crit aura, not survivability throughput.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Dual-resource. Mana is the primary gate, charged per spell, so nearly every button drains your own pool and extended fights can run you dry. Static is the secondary, a locally-generated build-and-spend meter. Volt ticks give 2, Forked Lightning gives 5 per enemy on hit, and your Electrocute and Lightning Cage plays feed it too. You spend it cheaply on Call Lightning, or all at once on Arm of Thorim, which depletes everything for a bigger nuke plus a stun. Critical Circuit softens the spend by refunding 10 Static on spender crits, and Electrical Charge, built by standing still, eventually lets spells stop depleting Static entirely. Nothing in this spec returns health.**

Difficulty**The generator-into-Call-Lightning loop and Volt-to-slow are simple enough to execute passably with little practice. Optimizing means calls: bank Static for a scaling Arm of Thorim, or cash out into fast Call Lightning filler. Sync the Storm Ascendance and Lord of Lightning window with a Lightning Cage channel. Keep Titanstorm compressing your big cooldowns via Call Lightning and Electrocute uptime. Hold position for Electrical Charge stacks.**
