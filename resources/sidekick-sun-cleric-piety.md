Retail feel

Closest to Balance Druid, with a Fire-Mage flavor bolted on. The Sunrise/Sunset lock-in from Dawn is straight out of Balance Druid's Solar/Lunar Eclipse: only Holy or only Fire benefits active at once, with Duality of Light handing you a school-specific buff for staying committed, gating your biggest damage modifiers behind one school at a time. On top of that it plays like a Fire-flavored caster. Dawnsear and Sunflare are your primary nukes, Radiant Flame is a channeled DoT-like damage-and-sustain spell, and Rapture is a hard-hitting Fire AoE finisher that pierces immunities, with an execute lean from Burn The Heretics below 35% health. Unlike a melee hybrid there is no weapon strike, no Rage, no interrupt and no battle rez. Its only real heal is the class kit's Solar Prayer, a single instant self-heal usable while stunned. Beyond that one button it self-sustains off City of Light and its own kite tools.

Load leveling build at level **60**

Playstyle

A ranged caster. You open with Dawnsear or Sunflare to start building Solar Power and, via the Piety passives, lock into Dawn's Sunrise (Holy) or Sunset (Fire). Duality of Light then rewards you with Sun Power off Fire damage or Twin Flame off Holy damage while you stay in that school. From there you channel Radiant Flame for sustained damage, and via City of Light to restore your own missing health and mana, fire Horusath Blast the moment it's up for a big single-target hit, and drop Rapture for immunity-piercing Fire burst. There is no melee and no Rage in this spec, and no heal button in the spec line itself. In-spec sustain is City of Light's health and mana return off Radiant Flame. Your one heal is the class kit's Solar Prayer, an instant self-heal usable even while stunned, if you spend the Essence on it. Meanwhile the damage engine keeps Solar Power ticking toward the next Dawn.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   Rapture is a Fire AoE nuke that pierces every immunity and scales up on low-health targets through Burn The Heretics. Horusath Blast hits hard on one target and Radiant Flame grinds. Burst and cleave from safety.
*   City of Light refunds a share of your missing health and mana off Radiant Flame. The damage engine is the healing rotation.
*   The Sunrise and Sunset lock-in through Duality of Light, Day Cycles and Solar Concord pays you for committing to one school. Sun Gate is a root-breaking blink that also gates an ally.

Weaknesses

*   No interrupt and no self-cleanse. Nothing in the kit locks a school, Sun Gate only removes roots, and Sunwell pulse-dispels fear, sleep and charm off a ground aura. A well-timed stun opener locks you down before you can react.
*   Dawnsear, Sunflare and Rapture are interruptible and pushback-vulnerable, and there is no damage-reduction cooldown anywhere. A melee train that sticks takes most of your throughput.
*   No battle rez and no heal in the spec line. Sustain is City of Light's incidental health and mana return off Radiant Flame. The class kit's Solar Prayer, an instant self-heal usable while stunned, is your one stabilizer and it costs an Essence pick. A fallen ally stays down.

Stat priority

Spell Hit (until your spells stop missing a +3-level target vs raid boss)›Spell Power›Critical Strike›Haste›Intellect›MP5/Spirit

Rotations

Solo / questing

Open at range with Sunflare or Dawnsear to build Solar Power and lock in the Dawn state (Sunrise/Sunset) you want. Channel Radiant Flame for sustained single-target damage and, via City of Light, to refund your own missing health and mana. Fire Horusath Blast the moment its cooldown is up for a big single-target hit. Drop Rapture for immunity-piercing Fire burst, which hits harder once the mob is under 35% health (Burn The Heretics). Use Sun Down on a tougher target for the delayed Holyflame detonation. You have no heal spell, so lean on City of Light's health/mana return from Radiant Flame and kite with Sun Gate rather than trying to heal up. Sun Gate also breaks roots and closes distance on a fleeing mob.

Dungeon

Pre-pull: buff yourself and drop Sunwell just before or as the pull starts, so its fear/sleep/charm dispel pulse is live around the group. On the pull: Radiant Flame and Rapture for AoE damage and Solar Power, plus Purgation stacking (via Radiant Flame) to amplify your Holy and Fire damage on the primary target. Sustain: you have no heal to hand out. Your job here is ranged damage, so keep your Sunrise/Sunset cycle turning for throughput and let City of Light keep your own bar topped. Emergency: Sun Gate to blink out of a bad spot or break a root, then reposition to stay at cast range. Note the spec has no interrupt, no curse or magic dispel of its own, and no combat rez. Sunwell only clears fear, sleep and charm off allies — don't rely on it for those group duties.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Two resources. Mana funds the Holy/Fire spells: Dawnsear, Sunflare, Horusath Blast, Rapture, Radiant Flame. There's no direct heal spell for it to pay for, and City of Light refunds some of your health and mana off Radiant Flame damage. Solar Power ticks up from your casts and from Solar Flare procs, with Sun's Hope granting Solar Power per Flare, building toward the Dawn trigger that locks in Sunrise or Sunset. There's no Rage and no melee resource here. Forced off your rotation, you lose Solar Power generation and City of Light's mana return, but a clean channel of Radiant Flame keeps you from being starved for long.**
