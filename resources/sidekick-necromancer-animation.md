Retail feel

Closest to Unholy Death Knight for the mechanical shell: a Runic Power economy, a plague layer (the Zombie Plague its Zombies, Plaguefather and Abominations emit), and a rotating army of pets. It is not a retail healer analog. There is no targeted heal-an-ally cast. The throughput healing (Raise: Ghoul leech, the Sacrifice Undead heal, the class kit's Vampiric Aura) only heals you and your own Undead. Allies get a utility fringe instead: the Corpse Wagon damage aura, Create Bone Reliquary (a clickable heal item allies can use once per minute), Animate: Skeletal Smith gear repairs, and Call of The Scourge, an in-combat battle res. Mechanically it's Unholy DK's pet/plague/RP engine wearing a buff and utility layer instead of throughput healing.

Playstyle

You play less like a spellcaster and more like a one-person raid group. Out of combat you fill your Life Force budget with Raise: Ghoul pets, your semi-permanent army, each occupying 1 Life Force and healing you on every auto-attack. The Summoning passives (Summoning Adept/Expert/Prodigy) and Master Animator grow that budget. In a fight you spend Runic Power, generated from the Crypt Swarm channel, to flood the field with short-duration Animate adds: Bone Construct, Bone Wraith, Plaguefather, Skeletal Archer, Tomb King, each independently ticking Plague/Shadow/Physical damage. Tomb King buffs every Undead attack, Plaguefather smears Zombie Plague, and the Skeletal Archer line gives you ranged pet DPS you can extend with Skeletal Artillery and Summoning Prodigy. You weave Command spells, which drive most of your passives: Forbidden Technique shortens Animate cooldowns, Bone King procs free instant Lichfrost or Blight, and Underking builds toward a free Tomb King. March of the Dead is your AoE plague nuke. Your personal HP comes almost entirely through pets: Raise: Ghoul's on-hit leech, the Sacrifice Undead heal, and Flesh Symbiosis' per-Abomination armor and magic resist plus periodic self-regen.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   There is no heal button here and it does not need one. Raise: Ghoul heals off auto-attacks, Sacrifice Undead converts a destroyed pet into a max-HP heal, and Flesh Symbiosis adds Armor, magic resist and periodic regen per Abomination. Hard to burst cleanly.
*   Permanent Raise: Ghoul pets stack with temporary Animate pets like Bone Construct, Bone Wraith, Skeletal Archers, Tomb King and Plaguefather. Several bodies soaking and dealing damage at once covers for bringing no hard CC.
*   Anti-Magic Shell raises every summon with a magic-absorb bubble and Flesh Symbiosis stacks Armor, magic resistance and a self-heal per Abomination-class pet. Fights you should lose turn into fights you reset.

Weaknesses

*   No teammate heal, and no root, stun, silence, or interrupt to lock a target for your team. The substitutes are indirect: the Corpse Wagon damage aura, Create Bone Reliquary as a once-a-minute click heal, Call of The Scourge as a battle res, and Mass Grave's fear, which only works on Humanoids.
*   The Life Force and pet economy is fragile and management-heavy. Animate pets are short-duration and Raise: Ghoul pets are capped by a pool you grow through the Summoning passives. AoE cleave or CC that kills the pets takes your damage and your self-healing with them.
*   Your damage and your Runic Power both come from the pets, fed by the Crypt Swarm channel and Command spells. Zone them, kite them off, or delete them and you are down to Crypt Swarm and cheap Animate pets until the army is rebuilt.

Stat priority

Spell Hit (until your spells stop missing a +3-level target, for self-cast Crypt Swarm/Ghoulify)›Spell Power›Haste Rating›Intellect›Critical Strike

Rotations

Solo / questing

Pre-pull: keep permanent pets topped. Raise: Ghoul first, since it self-heals you on every auto-attack, then more Ghouls as your Life Force budget allows. Pull with Crypt Swarm, a channel that builds RP while ticking Plague damage. Dump generated RP into Animate: Skeletal Archers for instant ranged bodies, or into Bone Construct / Bone Wraith / Plaguefather / Tomb King to add more damage and buff the pack. Fire March of the Dead as an AoE plague nuke on clumps, and let Bone King's free instant Lichfrost/Blight procs go out whenever they light up. Only Sacrifice Undead between fights if you need the Life Force back or the heal; otherwise leave pets standing to keep tanking trash. Drop a Summoning Ritual circle when you want a Ritual Return recall point for a longer pull chain.

Dungeon

Pull: top off Raise: Ghoul pets to your Life Force budget, then Crypt Swarm on the pull target to start the RP engine. Sustain: rotate RP into Animate: Skeletal Archers and the other Animates on cooldown to keep bodies on the pack and threat-soak stray adds. Keep Tomb King up so every minion attack hits harder. Raise: Ghoul's on-hit leech plus Flesh Symbiosis' periodic regen keep you topped under incoming damage. Emergency: lean on the pet screen and Anti-Magic Shell (every summon raises with a magic-absorb bubble), Flesh Symbiosis' Armor/magic-resist stacking, and the Sacrifice Undead heal if critically low. Corpse Wagon adds a party/raid damage aura for the group. There is no ally-heal, taunt or interrupt to fall back on — the pet screen is your only buffer.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Mana, spent on summoning and Command spells. Raising and animating undead (Abominations, Decaying Colossus) plus your Command, Animate and Raise casts all draw from the same pool, so a big summon phase dips you fast. The spec is built to sustain it. Graverobber and Grave Mastery cut the cost of every Raise, Animate and Command, Summoner discounts Sacrifice Undead, and Bone King and Deadly Bond give your Command casts a chance to make the next Lichfrost or Blight free and instant. Manage mana around your summon windows. With the cost-reduction passives up it rarely runs dry outside heavy back-to-back raising.**

Difficulty**Keep pets topped up, mash Command spells off cooldown, and it functions. Where it gets demanding is Life Force budgeting — how many Raise: Ghouls go to sustain versus which Animate windows you save for an alpha strike. Runic Power spend and Command spells should be feeding the passive engine: Bone King free casts, Forbidden Technique cooldown reduction, Underking working toward a free Tomb King. Pet uptime and March of the Dead placement want attention, and Sacrifice Undead is a decision, not a reflex.**
