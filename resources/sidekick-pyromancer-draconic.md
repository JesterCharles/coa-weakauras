Retail feel

Closest to retail Devastation Evoker: a dragon-themed fire caster built on a builder-spender loop, building Heat and Embers and dumping Embers into empowered payoffs, rather than a healing spec. Both lean on literal draconic Breath-flavored abilities, Breath of Neltharion and Echo of Nozdormu here, and on dragon-flight fantasy mobility, where Dragon Leap and Death From Above's soar-forward answer Evoker's Hover and Deep Breath. It is not a Preservation-style off-healer. The spec's own kit has no dedicated heal spell, and its healing is passive: Ember spenders heal a little via Alexstrasza's Gift, plus a small Lifedrinker leech. The class kit's Ember Touch can be taken as an actual single-target heal, but that's one optional spot cast. It plays as a DPS caster with strong utility, not a hybrid healer.

Playstyle

Flare Bolt is your default nuke and generates 20 Heat, with Firefall, Blaze, Destroyer's Maw and Death From Above as your harder hits and cooldown burst. Casting builds Heat, Heat fuels Embers, and Embers are what you spend. Draconic Invocation instantly generates 5 Embers on demand. The two spenders are Flames of Neltharion for AoE, which splashes several nearby enemies, and Echo of Nozdormu for single target, a chromatic nuke that also cuts all your cooldowns. Both are amplified by the passive Alexstrasza's Gift and by Aspect's Blessing, a cooldown that makes your next spender a guaranteed crit. Meteor (a big 50-Heat AoE nuke) and Eruption (a damage-and-healing buff that also feeds Heat) are your burst windows. Positioning matters because Dragon's Wrath adds bonus Fire damage, and Overwhelming Flames lets Lava Shard spread Blaze to nearby adds. Between pulls you pre-buff with Draconic Aspect and Inner Flame for party crit. Mana is a pressure, but the kit has returns: Ashen Skin keeps your regen flowing in combat and turns your periodic damage into mana, and Essence of Malygos trickles back a share of your max mana. Lean on those and a drink between pulls instead of hard-casting into empty. Dragon Leap, Death From Above's root line and Breath of Neltharion's disarm cone are what keep you from playing like a turret.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   Dragon Leap and Death From Above's root line into a blaze-forward soar move you through CC and terrain that would pin a normal caster. Kiting this is work.
*   Breath of Neltharion cones for real damage and disarms melee and ranged weapons, shutting off autoattacks and weapon abilities while you burn them down.
*   Draconic Invocation banks 5 Embers instantly for your spenders and Echo of Nozdormu, which also slashes your remaining cooldowns. On-demand spike.

Weaknesses

*   The Draconic kit has no heal spell of its own. Your healing is Alexstrasza's Gift scaling off Ember spenders and a small Lifedrinker leech. Ember Touch from the class kit is a real single-target heal, but it costs a pool pick and it is a spot cast, not sustain.
*   No absorb and no mitigation cooldown in the spec. The defensives are CC-breaks and mobility: Flame Step off stuns and bonds, Grace of Alexstrasza's stun, slow and root immunity, Dragon Skin's raised resistances. Volcanic Shell and Inferno Barrier add absorbs at the cost of pool picks.
*   Flare Bolt, Firefall, Destroyer's Maw and Echo of Nozdormu are almost all hard casts. A silence or kick chain shuts the spec off.

Rotations

Solo / questing

Pre-pull: Draconic Aspect and Inner Flame up. Open at range with Flare Bolt, your main filler, which generates 20 Heat. Weave Lava Shard for its Blaze application; with Overwhelming Flames it spreads Blaze to nearby adds. On cooldown: Destroyer's Maw (big unresistable hit, generates 30 Heat) and Death From Above (root line, then a blaze-forward AoE, great vs clumped or fleeing mobs). Use Draconic Invocation to instantly bank 5 Embers, then dump them into Echo of Nozdormu on a single target (heavy chromatic hit that also reduces your cooldowns) or Flames of Neltharion on 2+ mobs, ideally under Aspect's Blessing for a guaranteed crit. Meteor on cooldown for a big AoE nuke. Firefall for cleave on 2-3 packs; on a single target, keep Destroyer's Maw and Firefall on cooldown to fish for Legacy of Deathwing resets. You have no dedicated panic heal, but Ashen Skin and Essence of Malygos do return mana (in-combat regen plus mana from your periodic damage). Keep those rolling, and only drink between pulls when a long fight outpaces them.

Dungeon

Pre-pull: Draconic Aspect and Inner Flame (party crit) up. Run your builder-spender DPS: Flare Bolt filler, weave Lava Shard/Overwhelming Flames to spread Blaze across the pack, and dump Embers into Ember spenders and Echo of Nozdormu on priority targets. Firefall (uncapped) and Death From Above give AoE cleave on clumped pulls. Flames of Neltharion is your AoE Ember spender, Echo of Nozdormu the priority-target one. Use Draconic Invocation to burst 5 Embers into a spender window, lined up with Aspect's Blessing, Eruption and Meteor when you need a spike. Your job is damage plus utility, not healing. Breath of Neltharion disarms a dangerous melee mob, and Death From Above's root peels a loose add off a squishy. There is no group cleanse or fear in the kit, so lean on positioning and the disarm/root tools you do have.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Mana is primary. It's a heavy-cost caster but not a dry turret, because it has real mana returns. Ashen Skin lets your regen keep ticking in combat and converts your periodic damage into mana, and Essence of Malygos restores a share of your max mana over time. You'll still drink between pulls on long fights, but the economy leans on those returns rather than downtime. The secondary is a Heat/Ember builder-spender. Damage spells build Heat that fuels Embers: Flare Bolt +20 Heat, Destroyer's Maw +30 Heat, Firefall +10 per target. Draconic Invocation instantly generates 5 Embers on a cooldown. Two spenders burn Embers, Flames of Neltharion for AoE and Echo of Nozdormu for single target, both boosted by Alexstrasza's Gift. Each Ember you spend has a chance via Legacy of Deathwing to reset Destroyer's Maw and Firefall, so keep those firing on cooldown to fish for free resets. Stop casting and you stop generating.**

Difficulty**Flare Bolt filler, dump Embers, Firefall the pack. That functions. Mastery is banking and spending Embers efficiently, so Draconic Invocation times into Echo of Nozdormu's cooldown reset. Thread Dragon Leap and Death From Above's root-and-soar into a fight instead of wasting them, and position for Dragon's Wrath. Breath of Neltharion's disarm is proactive; spending it as filler throws it away.**
