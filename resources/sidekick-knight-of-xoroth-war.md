Retail feel

Closest to a retail Fury/Blood-hybrid Death Knight in feel. The parallels are real but softer than a full tank. Gorged, where Gore heals you for 50% of its damage, is a Death Strike and lifesteal analog. Flesh Hook plus Chainwhip are Death Grip with a pull and a silence, and Decimation's guaranteed crit is a burst-window setup. Unlike Blood DK, it has no big damage-reduction cooldown and no Anti-Magic-Shell-style CC-immunity. Its win condition is out-sustaining you with lifesteal and out-tempoing you with cleave, not popping a wall and not needing a healer.

Playstyle

War is a self-sufficient Str/AP melee bruiser built on Rage plus two internal secondary resources, Demonfire and Demon's Blood stacks. You build Demonfire with Gore (2-charge, requires a 2H weapon: Polearm/Staff/2H Axe/Mace/Sword) and Meatsaw, then dump it into Skulltaker for scaling burst, more damage per Demonfire consumed, refunded partly by Blood Frenzy at high stacks. Meanwhile every melee hit quietly stacks Demon's Blood, which pumps the crit of Brimstone Bludgeon and Meatsaw via Chop Shop and can auto-unleash your Pestilence through Hellborn. Brimstone Bludgeon, which transforms Sever, is the AoE keystone: a hard-hitting cleave on nearby enemies that also unleashes your Pestilence of Death for a bonus Shadowflame burst. Survivability leans on Gorged. Gore heals you for 50% of the damage it deals over a few seconds and, at 3 stacks, empowers your next Brimstone Bludgeon. It's a lifesteal loop, but a thin one, and there's no dedicated damage-reduction cooldown. In group content this spec supports through party buffs and CC rather than by topping off teammates' health bars.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   With Pestilence of War emanating, every Demonfire you spend heals 1.5% of your maximum health and its Unleash leeches off nearby enemies. Your rotation is your healer, before Juggernaut's Demon's Blood damage reduction and tick-healing even come into it.
*   Flesh Hook pulls and Chainwhip silences, both on cooldowns cut by Hook, Line, and Sinker. Yank a flag carrier or a clothie into your team and shut the healer up, no coordination needed.
*   Meatsaw ignores armor through Absolutism and ramps with The Butcher, while Brimstone Bludgeon slams everything nearby and unleashes Pestilence of Death. Warbringer adds one more AoE slam. Enough cleave to flip an outnumbered fight alone.

Weaknesses

*   Burning Rage covers incapacitate, banish and polymorph and works while you are already under them, but nothing breaks stuns, fears, or roots. Juggernaut's damage reduction and the class kit's Black Shield absorb both eat banked Demon's Blood stacks, so they compete and may not be loaded when focus fire lands.
*   Flesh Hook is your only reach tool, it sits on a cooldown, and it does nothing to immune targets. Once it is down there is no ranged snare and no gap-closer. A competent kiter outlasts you.
*   Nothing you have heals anyone but you. Conqueror's Will buffs party crit and Pestilence of Death buffs your crit damage, so a focused duo partner gets no health back.

Stat priority

Hit (until your specials stop missing a +3-level target)›Expertise (until your attacks stop being dodged/parried)›Strength›Armor Penetration›Critical Strike›Haste

Rotations

Solo / questing

Open hard pulls with Brimstone Bludgeon to cleave and unleash Pestilence of Death. Then build Demonfire with Gore (2 charges, requires a 2H weapon) and Meatsaw (hits nearby enemies) while cleaving trash, using Sever to top up rage. Dump stacked Demonfire into Skulltaker for its scaling finisher: more damage per stack, partly refunded by Blood Frenzy at 4+ stacks. Weave Brimstone Bludgeon on cooldown for burst plus its Pestilence unleash. Keep Gore rolling for its Gorged lifesteal so you regen through fights instead of resting. There is no emergency defensive button, so on a bad pull break line of sight or peel with Flesh Hook/Chainwhip rather than reaching for a panic cooldown.

Dungeon

Keep Pestilence of Death active before every pull. It boosts your crit damage, its unleash deals a Shadowflame burst, and it refreshes on a short cooldown. Keep Conqueror's Will's party crit buff up too. Fire Brimstone Bludgeon on cooldown to unleash that Pestilence for its bonus burst while cleaving the pack. Peel loose adds off healers and casters with Flesh Hook (pull) or Chainwhip (silence), dragging or shutting them down. Sustain yourself with Gorged's Gore-lifesteal rather than drawing on the healer's mana. This spec supports through the Conqueror's Will crit buff and off-tank CC (pull + silence), not direct healing — and it has no damage-reduction cooldown to soak a big hit for the group.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Rage is primary. Melee auto-attacks generate it, Sever explicitly generates it, and Jagged Edge boosts rage from damage. You spend it on Skulltaker, Gore, Brimstone Bludgeon and Meatsaw. It never goes dry the way mana does, since auto-attacking alone refills it. Two secondary stacking resources ride on top. Demonfire is built by Gore, Meatsaw and Brimstone Bludgeon, consumed by Skulltaker for more damage per stack, and partly refunded by Blood Frenzy at 4+ stacks. Demon's Blood builds passively off melee damage, pumps Brimstone Bludgeon and Meatsaw crit via Chop Shop, and can auto-unleash your Pestilence via Hellborn.**

Difficulty**Auto-attack, smash your generators, dump Skulltaker, keep Gore rolling for its lifesteal. A simple rage-spender loop, and it functions. Underspending Skulltaker is the common failure — time it to spend Demonfire at the right stack count, and lean on Blood Frenzy's refund. Keep Demon's Blood high so Chop Shop's crit and Hellborn's free Pestilence unleashes pay out. Brimstone Bludgeon's Pestilence unleash and Decimation's guaranteed crit belong in the same burst window. And in a BG, throwing Flesh Hook or Chainwhip at the wrong target is the decision that loses the fight.**
