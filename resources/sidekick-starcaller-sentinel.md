Retail feel

Closest to a ranged Hunter/Elemental hybrid with a whisper of Restoration Shaman's damage-that-heals idea. The throughline is arcane ranged damage that occasionally spills healing onto marked allies via Eclipsing Arrows, rather than a real heal kit. It is a plant-and-cast spec like a mage or priest. Your shots are NOT castable on the move by default, and that only works while mounted on Huntress Saber, so you cast the class's control instead to make room. Aspect of the Cosmos plays the movement-control role a slowing shot or Earthbind fills. Sentinel's healing is small and entirely incidental to its damage rotation, so it reads as a damage spec whose output occasionally heals rather than a primary healer.

Playstyle

Sentinel is a ranged arcane spec that plants to cast. Spam Huntress Shot and Moon Arrow at range to build Scattered Stars and drip mana back, drop Starcall for an AoE tag that applies Scattered Stars to nearby targets, then cash the stacks through Starfire Shot, Lunar Lance or Trueshot, each consuming Scattered Stars for bonus arcane damage. Almost every action refunds mana (Moon Arrow via Ishnu-alah, Aspect of the Huntress off auto-attacks, and every Scattered-Star-consuming finisher), so you never sit down to drink mid-fight. Aspect of the Cosmos slows enemies you hit, with Moon Runner deepening the slow per stack, or converts that into your own movement speed with Cosmic Strides talented. Either way it keeps you controlling the gap. The support half of the kit is thin and passive. Eclipsing Arrows makes Huntress Shot heal any ally marked by a Moon Priest's Hand of Elune or Touch of Moonlight, so healing rides along with your normal damage rotation rather than being cast on its own. Casting on the move comes from Huntress Saber: while mounted on it you gain movement speed, immunity to slows, and the ability to cast offensive spells without dismounting or stopping. That's your repositioning tool — not free Moon Arrow casts on the move.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   Moon Arrow and Starfire Shot both cast while moving. You rarely trade positioning for output.
*   Aspect of the Cosmos slows everything you hit, deepened by Moon Runner, or feeds your own speed through Cosmic Strides. The unslowable Huntress Saber mount makes disengages clean.
*   Moon Arrow's Ishnu-alah refund, Aspect of the Huntress, and Scattered-Star consumption on Starfire Shot, Lunar Lance and Trueshot all give mana back. While you are actively fighting you do not go dry.

Weaknesses

*   Eclipsing Arrows makes Huntress Shot heal allies marked by Touch of Moonlight or a Moon Priest, but it fires off your damage and cannot save a partner through a burst window. Touch of Moonlight itself is one mana-costly button bought with a class pick. No direct heal in the spec.
*   Past Moonlit Slumber's sleep and the class kit's Starshatter stun line there is no hard CC, no ally cleanse and no interrupt. You cannot pull a debuff off a partner or lock out a cast. Vial of Moonwell Water purges crowd control off you while stunned; without that pick you have slows.
*   Nothing in the kit reduces damage taken. With Aspect of the Cosmos, Cosmic Strides and Huntress Saber on cooldown, positioning is all you have against a focused burst.

Stat priority

Intellect (the master stat — Explosive Moonlight converts it into ranged attack power AND hit rating, Huntress Training converts it into haste, and Huntress Shot / Starfire Shot / Trueshot all scale off your maximum mana, so Intellect drives nearly every part of your damage)›Spell Power (Starcall, Rain of Comets, Lunar Lance, and the SP term on every shot)›Critical Strike (Eye of Elune plus the bonus crit damage on Starfire Shot)›Haste›Hit only until your shots stop missing a +3-level target (Explosive Moonlight already supplies a chunk of hit from Intellect)›Agility (a minor ranged-AP/crit contributor once Intellect covers attack power)

Rotations

Solo / questing

Open with Starcall if up (AoE tag plus Scattered Stars, and Aspect of the Cosmos slowing the pack) → filler Huntress Shot to keep stacking Scattered Stars and regen mana between repositions → Moon Arrow whenever Huntress Shot is down, or when you want the extra mana tick → consume stacked Scattered Stars for burst via Starfire Shot, Lunar Lance, or Trueshot (2 charges) → on a pack, lead Starcall into Drawstring-of-Elune-boosted Huntress Shot for the AoE tick → finish the kite with Aspect of the Cosmos slows and Cosmic Strides speed, topping off with Moon Arrow. There's no melee weave and no stun. If a mob closes, keep firing and peel it with the Aspect of the Cosmos slow or a Starshatter finisher.

Dungeon

Pull: Starcall to AoE-tag and apply Scattered Stars, then keep Aspect of the Cosmos slows on stragglers. Sustain: keep your normal damage rotation running on the kill target (Huntress Shot/Moon Arrow builders into Starfire Shot/Lunar Lance/Trueshot spenders). If a Moon Priest in the group is marking allies with Hand of Elune/Touch of Moonlight, your Huntress Shots will heal those marked targets via Eclipsing Arrows, but you cannot place the marks yourself. Utility is thin: no interrupt, no direct heal, no cleanse, no combat-rez. You contribute ranged damage and slows rather than covering a healer's or an interrupter's job.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Dual-resourced. Energy fuels the spammable ranged abilities, Moon Arrow and Huntress Shot, while utility and burst spells cost a percentage of mana. Nearly every action refunds mana: Moon Arrow via Ishnu-alah, Aspect of the Huntress, and a chunk per Scattered Star consumed by Starfire Shot, Lunar Lance and Trueshot. Trueshot itself drains max mana to fire, but the consumption pays much of it back. Mana effectively sustains itself while you're fighting. It only runs dry when something cuts off the income side, so being kited, silenced or kept out of range stops you landing Moon Arrow and Huntress Shot and stops you spending stars.**

Difficulty**Huntress Shot and Moon Arrow filler with Starcall on cooldown performs reasonably with minimal thought. Chaining it under pressure is the hard part: Scattered Star stacking timed against the Starfire Shot, Lunar Lance and Trueshot consumption windows, Aspect of the Cosmos slows and Cosmic Strides and Huntress Saber mobility managed so an out always exists, and Eclipsing Arrows' heal landing on a marked ally at the moment it's worth something.**
