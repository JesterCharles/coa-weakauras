Retail feel

Closest to Survival Hunter, reflavored fully ranged instead of melee. The class fantasy is identical: you live and die by explosives. Sticky Bomb and Bomb Toss are your Wildfire Bomb, Oil-Spill Pylon is your Flare/Tar Trap control tool, and Napalm stacking into empowered drones and shots is a slow-burn DoT-then-amplify mechanic like Survival's bleed-and-bomb proc chains. The engine where damage against a Sticky-Bombed enemy detonates extra Shrapnel Mines and Sticky Bomb Explosions (Gazlowe's Wisdom, I Need More Explosions!) is mechanically the same idea as Survival's bomb proc chains. It diverges from Survival two ways. There's no melee component at all, since everything is gun- and gadget-based. And it bolts on a thin support layer Survival Hunter doesn't have: The BIG Guns! raid attack-power buff, plus an armor shred on Bomb Toss that softens targets for the group. Net: Survival's bomb identity crossed with a ranged deployable-summoner, with none of Survival's melee mobility (Harpoon/Wildfire) replaced by anything equivalent.

Playstyle

Demolition is a fully-ranged explosives gunner. Scrap Shot is your filler and stacks Napalm on the target, and you layer bomb, mine and drone combos on top. The core loop is tagging a target with Sticky Bomb, then feeding it damage so its explosion spreads: Gazlowe's Wisdom spawns Shrapnel Mines when Sticky Bomb detonates, I Need More Explosions! and Target Acquired scale the Sticky Bomb Explosions, and Aftermath makes your next 2 Scrap Shots faster and harder. It's a priority engine, not a flat 1-2-3. You weave Scrap Shot constantly since every 4th one empowers your next via the Boomshot passive, build Napalm stacks that amp Firepot Drone and Scrap Shot through Drone Striker and refresh a drone charge at 5 stacks through Grenadier, and manage the Build economy: Firepot Drone (3 charges, dive-bombing AoE), Spider Bomb and Spider Bomb Factory (auto-rushing bombs), and Oil-Spill Pylon (slow + fire-vuln zone). Rocket Launcher is your heavy nuke, guaranteed to crit against Sticky-Bombed targets via Surprise Launch!. Bomb Toss lobs an armor-shredding bomb that feeds the Bombastic and Bomber Man passives to reset itself and shave Sticky Bomb's cooldown. Your Sentry Turret adds free damage: Flamethrowing makes it breathe fire on every Bomb Toss, and Rocket Turrets makes it mirror your Rocket Launcher.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   Seed a stacked pack with Sticky Bomb, feed it Bomb Toss, Scrap Shot and Firepot Drones, and Gazlowe's Wisdom Shrapnel Mines and amplified Sticky Bomb Explosions go off across the whole cluster. Telegraphed, delayed, and fight-ending.
*   Bomb Toss stacks heavy armor shred on the target, softening it for your own Fire burst and your allies' physical damage. Bombastic and Bomber Man reset it and shorten Sticky Bomb's cooldown.
*   Firepot Drones, Spider Bomb Factory and the Oil-Spill Pylon's slow-and-fire-soften zone let you dictate the engagement range. You never have to melee anything.

Weaknesses

*   No anti-CC in the spec line. Every answer is a class-kit purchase: Kinetic Shield for stun immunity castable while stunned, Rocket Boots to dispel roots, Build: Alarm Beacon to strip fear, charm and sleep. None is a true trinket equivalent, and any floor costs Essence picks.
*   Combat Dash ends the moment you deal direct damage, so you cannot fight and kite through it. The stronger outs, Rocket Boots' root-dispelling launch and Kinetic Shield's damage reduction and stun immunity, are class-kit picks. Without them, whatever reaches you stays on you.
*   All your healing is borrowed from the shared Tinker kit and every cast is a GCD off the explosion engine. Repair Shot fires on yourself, Med Pack is a cleansing heal-over-time, Nanobot Reconstruction is a drip, Auto Resuscitation Device is the cheat-death. The strongest panic heals live in Invention.

Stat priority

Hit (ranged special-attack cap (until your specials stop missing a +3-level target))›Critical Strike›Haste›Agility›Attack Power›Spell Power›Armor Penetration

Rotations

Solo / questing

Open at range with Bomb Toss to shred the target's armor and start the Bombastic/Bomber Man reset chain. Drop Sticky Bomb on your target or pack immediately to arm the explosion engine (Gazlowe's Wisdom Shrapnel Mines + amplified Sticky Bomb Explosions). Weave Scrap Shot constantly so every 4th one empowers the next via Boomshot, and Aftermath's post-Sticky-Bomb window makes the next two faster and harder. Send Firepot Drones and Spider Bombs to build and spend Napalm (Drone Striker amps damage per stack, Grenadier refunds a drone charge at 5 stacks). Fire Rocket Launcher as your heavy nuke, guaranteed to crit into a Sticky-Bombed target, and burn Rockadier or Rocket Barrage on elites and tough pulls. Drop an Oil-Spill Pylon to slow and fire-soften anything that closes. You can top yourself with Repair Shot (or the shared Med Pack / Nanobot Reconstruction) between packs, but each heal is a GCD off your damage — pull within your survivability and let mana-on-crit (Explosives Expert) keep you casting.

Dungeon

Pull: drop an Oil-Spill Pylon at the chokepoint to slow and fire-soften the pack. Open with Bomb Toss on the priority target to plant the armor shred, then Sticky Bomb to arm the explosion engine. Sustain: keep your Sticky Bomb → Bomb Toss / Scrap Shot / Firepot Drone loop running so Gazlowe's Wisdom Shrapnel Mines and amplified Sticky Bomb Explosions keep firing. Feed Napalm into your drones (Drone Striker), and let your Sentry Turret pile on free damage via Flamethrowing and Rocket Turrets. AoE: Spider Bomb Factory and a well-placed Oil-Spill Pylon shine on packs, while Rocket Launcher (guaranteed crit on Sticky-Bombed targets) picks priority adds. Emergency: you can Repair Shot yourself or a struggling ally in a pinch, and Med Pack cleanses bleed, poison and disease. Healing means pausing your damage, though, and you still have no interrupt and no in-combat rez. You contribute an armor shred (Bomb Toss) and a raid attack-power buff (The BIG Guns!), so a focused ally is otherwise on their own defensives.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Mana-based, with most abilities costing a percentage of base mana. Regen matters less than Explosives Expert, which refunds a chunk of your maximum mana whenever you or your summons land a direct harmful-ability crit. A crit-heavy build casts almost indefinitely. A cold streak of non-crits dries you out. There's no ammo, Rounds or reload minigame here. The loop is mana in, crit refunds back, layered over cooldown and charge tracking: Firepot Drone charges, Rockadier windows, and Sticky Bomb's cooldown shortened by Bomber Man.**

Difficulty**You can't just spam Scrap Shot. Napalm stacks, Firepot Drone charges and your Sticky Bomb window all need tracking or you're leaving damage on the table from the first pull. At the top: Sticky Bomb tags timed so the amplified explosions and Gazlowe's Wisdom Shrapnel Mines land in the fullest cluster, Aftermath-empowered Scrap Shots spent, the Boomshot every-4th-Scrap-Shot buff kept rolling, Napalm driven to 5 stacks so Grenadier refunds drone charges, Rocket Launcher pointed at a Sticky-Bombed target for the Surprise Launch! guaranteed crit, and Rockadier and Rocket Barrage windows stacked back-to-back.**
