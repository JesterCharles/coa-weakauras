Playstyle

A pet-centric ranged spec. You summon and reinforce your Shadow Hounds (Unleash The Hounds, Kennel Master, Decimate, Coordinated Killing/Shadow Rage) while generating Rage from ranged auto-attacks. Fire Quickdraw, usable only after a critical strike, and Houndmaster's Call to send the hounds leaping. Anguish spreads shadow pressure and speeds your next Darkflock cone. Self-healing is light but real: Darkfeast is your on-demand heal, the class kit's Vampiric Tonic can add lifesteal, and Daredevil remains the emergency bail-out.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   A Shadow Hound core plus temporary lesser hounds from Unleash the Hounds, Kennel Master and Daredevil, reinforced by Decimate, Coordinated Killing and Shadow Rage. Nobody fights you 1v1.
*   At 35% health Daredevil summons three lesser hounds and resets Vault, which buys the disengage. Modest, but enough to grind through moderate pressure without a healer.
*   Repulse knocks back a frontal cone and your Trap spells reset on demand through Rearmament, which breaks up a push or peels for a teammate. Veiled in Darkness adds a party-wide Attack Power aura.

Weaknesses

*   Nothing answers a stun, fear, root, or polymorph aimed at you. A CC chain that ignores your hounds locks you out.
*   On-demand control is Repulse's knockback and Trap spells. No single-target hard stun and no silence, so locking down a kill target or peeling a caster is unreliable.
*   Houndmaster's Call and Quickdraw draw on both Rage and mana. Past Darkfeast's on-demand heal, and Vampiric Tonic's lifesteal if you spend a class pick, the only lifeline is Daredevil's once-per-cooldown near-death summon. A drawn-out fight strands you low with nothing to top off with.

Stat priority

Hit (ranged special-attack cap, until your specials stop missing a +3-level target)›Expertise (until your attacks stop being dodged/parried)›Agility (base RAP + crit that the whole ranged attack power kit scales on)›Intellect (Shadewalker converts Int into AP and Dark Mind converts Int into crit rating)›Spell Power (feeds the minor Shadow portions of Darkflock/Feast of Talons)›Critical Strike›Haste

Rotations

Solo / questing

Keep hounds out (Unleash the Hounds, empowered by Decimate) and let auto-attacks generate Rage. Fire Quickdraw only after a crit enables it, send Houndmaster's Call, and channel Darkflock for cone AoE (Anguish shortens its next cast). Use Repulse to knock a closing enemy back, and lean on the hounds to split damage while you kite.

Dungeon

Peels come from Repulse's knockback and your Trap spells rather than a silence or stun. Decimate empowers the hounds for burst on a priority target, and Veiled in Darkness passively buffs the party's Attack Power. With no self-heal, position to avoid damage and save Daredevil's near-death summon as your emergency button.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Dual Rage and Mana. Ranged auto-attacks generate Rage via Houndmaster, and Darkrider cuts the Rage cost of Shadowblast, Vault and Unleash the Hounds. Sharpshooter regenerates mana on Shadowblast, mana and not health. Deal With The Devil raises your costs in exchange for hound crit damage. There's no on-demand health refill in the model.**

Difficulty**Hounds summoned and empowered, Rage generated, Quickdraw's crit-gated window reacted to — a low bar to clear. Everything else is stacking Decimate and Coordinated Killing/Shadow Rage on the hounds, spacing Rearmament to reset Traps and Decimate, and treating Repulse and Daredevil as your only defensive beats, since there's no self-heal behind them.**
