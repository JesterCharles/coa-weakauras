Retail feel

Closest to retail Protection Paladin, with a dash of Discipline Priest bolted on. The core loop is Protection Paladin's mitigation converted into board presence: shield plus one-hand weapon, block and avoidance converted into board presence, spent on both offense (Justicar's Wrath, Dawnbreak's shield-radiate cone, Hammer of Kings) and defense (Seraphic Bulwark, Dawnguard block stacking). The Discipline-Priest flavor shows up in the shield-and-support toolkit. The Chosen turns your Illumination into a self-absorb that detonates for Holy damage via Hope. Scroll of Hope is a raid-wide magic-mitigation cooldown with a low-HP triage heal, in Power Word: Barrier / Pain Suppression flavor. Sun Disc pulses increased healing to nearby allies. Mechanically it's a Protection Paladin chassis wearing a Discipline Priest's shield flavor.

Playstyle

You fight in melee as a Strength/Stamina block tank with a one-hand weapon and shield, running mana plus a Solar Power sub-resource. Mana funds the Holy/Fire strikes and heals (Illumination, Justicar's Wrath, Dawnbreak, Hammer of Kings, Solar Nova). Solar Power spikes from your Gavel strikes, Dawnbreak, fulfilling a Vow through Dawnguard, and crits (Sun Warrior's Guidance, Bastion of Hope), unlocking recurring Dawn empowered effects via Angelic Presence. Your defense is a block-and-mitigate chassis: Seraphic Bulwark and Blessed One block stacking, Dawnguard's block chance off Vow fulfillment, The Chosen shield spun off your Illuminations (which explodes for Holy damage via Hope when depleted), and passive spell mitigation from Gleaming Armor and Stand Against Darkness. Moment to moment you're smashing Justicar's Wrath for high threat and self-healing off it via Adjudicator, radiating Dawnbreak off your shield for AoE, dropping Hammer of Kings' encircling damage aura, and layering Illumination and The Chosen shields to keep yourself topped. Survival is largely reactive and passive-driven rather than a button you babysit every GCD. That keeps the floor low, and the ceiling comes from timing the Dawn and Angelic Presence windows and holding Seraphic Bulwark against burst.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   Adjudicator heals you off Justicar's Wrath hits, Angelic Presence and Bastion of Hope proc off avoidance and crits, and The Chosen spins shields off your Illuminations. You stay up without spending every GCD healing.
*   Seraphic Bulwark stacks charged block, Blessed One and Dawnguard add block chance, and Clarity of the Heavens turns Intellect into block value, with Gleaming Armor and Stand Against Darkness covering spells. Killing you on a point is a project.
*   Dawnbreak, Hammer of Kings and Solar Nova spread damage across a whole scrum, while Scroll of Hope adds raid magic mitigation and a triage heal and Sun Disc puts out a healing aura.

Weaknesses

*   Justicar's Wrath, Dawnbreak, Hammer of Kings and all your AoE need weapon range, and Blessed One's slow resistance is the only movement tech you have. A ranged opponent disengages whenever they like.
*   No interrupt, silence, stun, or self-cleanse. Calm disarms, and hard-locks only Undead and Demons, which stops no cast and breaks no CC on you. A caster or a stun opener dictates the fight.
*   Output is low. You win on attrition and presence, never a fast kill, and a 1v1 against another tanky target runs very long. Once Seraphic Bulwark is spent you are on proc healing and block, which a second timed burst punches through.

Stat priority

Strength›Spell Hit (until your Holy/Fire attacks stop missing a +3-level target)›Spell Power›Attack Power›Critical Strike›Haste

Rotations

Solo / questing

Open with Justicar's Wrath or Dawnbreak to close and start Solar Power. Smash Justicar's Wrath on cooldown: it self-heals you via Adjudicator and generates high threat. Radiate Dawnbreak off your shield as your AoE cone. Drop Hammer of Kings' encircling damage aura on multi-mob pulls, and use Solar Nova for burst AoE. Weave Calm to disarm, or to hard-lock an Undead/Demon add, when a pack gets rough. Sustain passively off Justicar's Wrath's self-heal (Adjudicator), your Illumination-fed The Chosen shields, Angelic Presence avoidance procs, and Bastion of Hope crit refunds. You have no on-demand burst-heal button, so if a pull spikes you low, pop Seraphic Bulwark's block stacking or lean on Illumination.

Dungeon

Pre-pull: put up Sun Disc's healing aura and get a Vow rolling so Dawnguard's block stacks start. Sustain: Illumination as your single-target heal. Prioritize it while Angelic Presence is active for the empowered version and to feed The Chosen shield; Together In Conquest lets your Illumination splash to a nearby ally. Proactive: Scroll of Hope before known magic-burst windows for the raid-wide mitigation and its low-HP triage heal. Emergency: Seraphic Bulwark's block charges if you're eating cleave, and Calm to disarm or lock down a dangerous add. Filler: Justicar's Wrath / Dawnbreak / Hammer of Kings on cooldown to keep Solar Power and Dawn windows cycling and contribute real damage between heals. Note there is no interrupt and no dispel in the kit, so leave kicks and cleanses to the group.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Two resources. Mana funds the Holy/Fire strikes and heals, Illumination, Justicar's Wrath, Dawnbreak, Hammer of Kings and Solar Nova, and it can run tight in a long fight. Blessed Bulwark and Bastion of Hope replenish it off avoidance and crits. Solar Power spikes off your Gavel strikes, Dawnbreak and crits via Sun Warrior's Guidance, and off Vow fulfillment via Dawnguard, gating the empowered Dawn effects that Angelic Presence layers across the kit. Your defensive layer is mostly resource-free and automatic: block, Angelic Presence avoidance procs, Justicar's Wrath's self-heal via Adjudicator, and The Chosen shields off your Illuminations. Self-sustain holds up even when mana is tight.**

Difficulty**Block, swing, heal when low, and the passives keep you upright: block, avoidance and crit procs from Angelic Presence and Bastion of Hope, Justicar's Wrath's self-heal, The Chosen shields off your Illuminations. Playing it well is catching windows. Dawnbreak and Gavel strikes land right before Illumination to catch Angelic Presence. Solar Power gets tracked for Dawn procs. Hammer of Kings layers with Dawnbreak and Solar Eclipse's AoE timing. And Seraphic Bulwark's charges are held for the exact burst window rather than pressed reflexively.**
