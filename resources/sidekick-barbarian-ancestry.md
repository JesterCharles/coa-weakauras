Retail feel

Closest to retail Enhancement Shaman. The mechanical fingerprints line up directly. A weapon-proc mechanic adds bonus elemental (Frost) main-hand attacks and builds a secondary resource: Ancestral Combat and Fill Level generation ≈ Windfury Weapon procs feeding Maelstrom. A summoned pet copies your whirl attack, with Honored Ancestor via Ancestor's Call performing Ancestral Whirl ≈ Feral Spirit wolves mirroring Stormstrike. Stackable raid-wide totem-style auras cover haste, attack power and crit: Ancestral Fury, Splash Zone/War Cry, Frozen Blades and Clanlord's Totem ≈ Skyfury/Strength of Earth-style totems. Tavern Brawl is the party haste cooldown, Bloodlust/Heroism-adjacent. Self-sustain comes from combat procs rather than a dedicated heal spell, so Ramhorn Rage's group regen plus Liquid Courage's Fill Level damage reduction ≈ Enhancement's proc-flavored sustain, not Resto Shaman's actual healing kit. Like Enhancement, it's a melee DPS spec wearing support clothing via auras and a haste cooldown, not a healer.

Playstyle

A Keg Smash-spamming frost/physical melee brawler who buffs everyone near you just by existing. Keep Ancestral Combat's self-buff rolling: it gives a chance for bonus Frost main-hand swings that also build Fill Level, upgraded further by Improved Ancestral Combat. Then dump Energy into Keg Smash, a short-cooldown AoE frost+physical hit that splashes to nearby enemies. Your Tankard state is a second resource. Breath of the North only fires when the Tankard is empty, Ale of the God-King only when it's full, so you manage empty versus full the way a rogue manages combo points. Ramhorn Rage is emergency AoE sustain: it enrages you and allies within its radius for a stacking max-HP-per-few-seconds regen, extended by autoattacks. Your Fill Level stacks passively cut damage taken via Liquid Courage while you hold them. The Honored Ancestor pet performs Ancestral Whirl cleave off Keg Smash and Ancestral Combat through The Spins and Might of Utgarde. On top of that sits a wall of always-on party auras: Ancestral Wisdom hit, Frozen Blades crit, Ancestral Fury haste, Splash Zone and War Cry attack power. Your presence alone raises the group's damage before you've thrown a punch.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   Party auras with no cast behind them: Ancestral Fury haste, Splash Zone and War Cry attack and spell power, Frozen Blades crit, Clanlord's Totem. You swing group fights by showing up.
*   Ramhorn Rage regens group max HP and your autoattacks extend it, while Liquid Courage stacks damage reduction off held Fill Level. No healer required.
*   Frozen Tankard is a thrown Frost stun that flags Frozen, and Keg Smash's Blind Stupor slows casting. Enough lockdown to isolate someone and finish them.

Weaknesses

*   No defensive cooldown, and nothing breaks a CC chain. A coordinated focus kills you.
*   There is no ally-targeted heal. Support means passive auras, Ramhorn Rage's group max-HP regen, and Cheers!'s damage redirect, none of which react to a teammate taking burst.
*   No battle-rez, no reset. Sustain is a combat-uptime engine of Ramhorn Rage regen and Fill Level mitigation, so being locked out of swinging stalls the whole loop.

Stat priority

Hit (until your specials stop missing a +3-level target)›Agility›Haste›Armor Penetration›Critical Strike›Spirit

Rotations

Solo / questing

1) Keep Ancestral Combat's self-buff rolling for its Frost proc chance and Fill Level generation. 2) Keg Smash on cooldown (boosted by Trip to the Alehouse) as your core AoE/ST frost+physical hit. 3) Ale of the God-King the instant your Tankard is full, and don't let a fill go stale. 4) Breath of the North whenever the Tankard is empty. 5) Ramhorn Rage when you're taking damage. Its stacking max-HP regen, extended by your autoattacks, is your real self-heal. 6) Tavern Brawl! before a big pull for the party-haste enrage window. 7) Open dangerous pulls with Frozen Tankard to stun and set up the Frozen state for your Frost bursts.

Dungeon

1) Ramhorn Rage at the start of a hard pull, or whenever the group is taking sustained damage. Its max-HP regen (extended by your own autoattacks) is the closest thing you have to a group heal. 2) War Cry / Splash Zone at pull start for the party's attack-speed and attack/spell power burst. 3) Ancestral Fury and Frozen Blades stay up passively. Position so the whole group sits in your haste and crit auras. 4) Clanlord's Totem dropped at the fight to buff allies standing near it. 5) Cheers! on the squishiest party member, or the healer. It redirects part of their incoming damage onto yourself and cuts what they take. 6) Keg Smash on cooldown for AoE damage and threat across packs, feeding the Honored Ancestor's Ancestral Whirl via The Spins. 7) Frozen Tankard to stun a caster add or peel something off the healer. 8) Tavern Brawl! as the group haste cooldown for burn phases.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Dual resource. Energy has fast passive regen, like a rogue or druid pool, and it fuels every active ability: Keg Smash, Ancestral Combat, Cheers!/Clanlord's Totem, Frozen Tankard, Tavern Brawl!. Per-ability cost reducers ease the pinch (Breaking Morale on Break, Ancestral Spirit and Improved Ancestral Combat elsewhere). Heavy back-to-back AoE weaving can get tight, but you rarely run fully dry. On top sits the Tankard/Fill Level gauge, a combo-point-like secondary built by Ancestral Combat procs and Cold Brew off Frost crits. It gates Breath of the North, which needs empty, and Ale of the God-King, which needs full. Tracking that state is a real part of the rotation. Higher Fill Level also feeds Ice Cold Bubbles' Frost damage and Liquid Courage's damage reduction.**

Difficulty**Ancestral Combat uptime, Keg Smash on cooldown, Ramhorn Rage when you're hurt — that much runs itself. The depth is Tankard discipline: firing Ale of the God-King against a full Tankard instead of overwriting it, saving Breath of the North for an empty one, spending Frozen Tankard on a peel versus a Frozen-state Frost burst. Hold Ramhorn Rage, War Cry and Cheers! for the moment that wants them rather than pressing on cooldown, and you get far more out of the spec than the passive auras hand you for free.**
