Retail feel

Closest to Protection Warrior. The mechanical fingerprints line up almost 1:1. Ram is Shield Slam, Heavy Blow and Pulverize are Devastate, Retaliation is Revenge-flavored auto-retaliation where blocks strike back, Advance is Charge, and Brace and Raise Shield are Shield Block and Shield Wall. The one divergence from a pure damage-race warrior is the party-facing tools: Bastion and Vanguard's Might for ally damage-reduction, Tower Formation's personal tanking stance, and the taunts (Challenging Cry, Shield Challenge). Those read more like a Protection Paladin's aura layer grafted onto a Warrior chassis, hence support in the class label despite there being no direct heal for allies. The resource (Energy here, Rage there) is generated by tanking and attacking, so the whole kit is reactive and damage-fueled. Unlike stock Prot Warrior, Vanguard has no AoE stun. Hammer of Kings is an AoE slam that slows casting, and Hammer of the Law is a line hit that silences, not a Shockwave stun.

Playstyle

Vanguard runs on Energy, with cost relief and generation tied to fighting through hits. Seasoned Fighter cuts ability costs, Valiant Defender raises your max Energy, and Recuperation refunds Energy whenever you take or block a physical hit, so blocking and attacking drive the snowball rather than avoiding. Open with Advance, a forced 100%-speed gap-close, into Hammer of Kings for an AoE slam that also slows enemy casting. From there cycle Heavy Blow (line hit, high threat, grants Vanguard's Might's party damage-reduction aura) and Pulverize (cleaves extra targets via One Man Army, boosted by Footman's Call and Valiant Defender) while layering Raise Shield, Brace and Bastion to blunt incoming damage. Retaliation is largely passive: blocks deal damage back to the attacker, and King's Guard (Pulverize and Ram have a chance to reset Raise Shield's cooldown) plus Refuse To Die (Advance resets it outright) snowball your Raise Shield uptime. Survivability leans on mitigation and procs, not a healer's toolkit. Fine Plating stacks Physical damage reduction with every block, Honorable Demeanor turns blocks into stacking Strength and Stamina, and Iron Barrier gives your blocks a chance to critically block for double. The only direct heals are conditional ones — Final Verdict's execute-range self-heal, Lionheart's emergency heal when you're dropped low, and the class kit's Knight's Calling, a periodic health-and-Energy restore that costs a class pick.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   Raise Shield, Brace and Bastion overlap with block passives like Iron Barrier and Resilient. Uptime is high enough to eat focus fire without a healer.
*   You harden as you get hit. Resilient bumps max HP on blocks under Raise Shield, Iron Barrier turns blocks critical, and Paragon banks them for a payoff, so incoming damage raises your floor instead of needing a heal.
*   Hammer of Kings does AoE damage with a casting slow and Hammer of the Law hits a line with a silence. Bastion and Vanguard's Might hand nearby allies damage reduction for free.

Weaknesses

*   Every self-heal is conditional: Final Verdict inside execute range, Lionheart's rare low-health save, Knight's Calling only if you spend a pick. Past those you are on Honorable Demeanor's on-block Stamina stacks, block passives and Gallant's healing-received boost. You out-mitigate rather than out-heal, and you lose a straight damage race.
*   Advance is a committal forced-movement charge, not a repeatable gap-closer, and your only caster answer is Hammer of the Law's silence plus Hammer of Kings' casting slow, both in melee. Kiting classes outrange you.
*   Energy comes from staying in the brawl, with Seasoned Fighter and Valiant Knight relieving costs while you trade hits. Against someone who disengages, kites, or chain-CCs you out of combat, the resource stalls when you need it most.

Stat priority

Defense (to crit-immunity)›Expertise (until your attacks stop being dodged/parried, prevents dodge parry-haste)›Stamina (Bulwark converts Stamina into hit rating; Iron Guardian scales max HP)›Block Rating/Value›Strength (native Attack Power for threat)›Dodge/Parry›Armor

Rotations

Solo / questing

Advance in to close the gap -> Hammer of Kings on the pull for the AoE slam + casting slow if up -> Heavy Blow on cooldown (cheap, high threat, builds Vanguard's Shield and the Vanguard's Might aura) -> Pulverize as your Energy-dump filler between cooldowns (Footman's Call and Valiant Knight boost it, and it cleaves on multi-mob pulls) -> Ram whenever Energy allows for single-target burst (King's Guard can reset Raise Shield off it) -> Raise Shield/Brace reactively any time a pull is chunking your HP, both for the mitigation and to bank blocks toward Resilient's max-HP bump and Paragon's empowered Heavy Blow. Retaliation's block-reflect just wants you to stay in melee taking hits. Iron Barrier's critical blocks pad the mitigation the longer the fight runs.

Dungeon

Pull with Advance + Hammer of Kings for the AoE slam and initial threat/damage on the whole pack -> immediately follow with Heavy Blow (line hit, high threat) and Pulverize (Footman's Call boosts it) to spread threat and cleave damage across the pull -> drop Bastion or Tower Formation depending on need; Bastion is a group damage-reduction window, Tower Formation a personal block/armor stance via the Formations -> cycle your mitigation cooldowns (Raise Shield, Brace, Bastion) proactively before big hits rather than reactively, banking blocks for Resilient and Paragon -> use Challenging Cry / Shield Challenge to re-taunt anything that slips off you and onto squishier party members -> let Retaliation's block-reflect and King's Guard's Raise Shield resets keep building threat, mitigation, and damage the whole fight.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Energy, with cost relief and expansion tied to fighting through damage rather than a passive regen tick. Seasoned Fighter and Honorbound cut ability costs, Valiant Defender raises your maximum Energy, and Valiant Knight makes a Pulverize free after a melee crit. You're flush mid-brawl. You run dry in downtime, against a kiting opponent, or under CC that stops you from acting, which are the exact moments you need offense or defense most.**

Difficulty**Retaliation is largely passive, Heavy Blow and Pulverize are spammable fillers, and most of the defensive cooldowns are press-when-hurting. Playing it properly means Raise Shield, Brace and Bastion sequenced so mitigation overlaps actual incoming burst instead of soaking chip damage. Advance and Hammer of Kings do more as a peel than as an opener. Paragon and Vanguard's Shield stacks are worth banking for their payoff, and King's Guard and Wreck Formation proc windows need watching — otherwise you are a body standing in melee mashing buttons.**
