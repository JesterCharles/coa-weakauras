The control chain comes first: Spirit Shock to silence and interrupt a key caster, then Hex of Malice or Hex of Death via Malefic Arrow to cut a target's healing received while you pour ranged damage in, then a Spirit Eclipse dump once you've banked Spirits.

Retail feel

Closest to a Survival Hunter built on a shamanic support chassis. The ranged-weapon-and-pet-of-Serpent-Wards offense, with Jinx and curse control standing in for traps, reads Survival Hunter. The underlying class flavor reads shamanic: Serpent Wards pulsing damage like guardian totems, Spirit-stacking self-regen, and party auras like Darkspear Traditionalist and Strength of Da Loa. Mechanically it's a support-flavored chassis that was talented into being a real ranged-hybrid combatant, spending most of its buttons on Spirit-fueled ranged damage rather than on healing.

Playstyle

You fight from range with a Ranged Weapon equipped. The spec's damage identity (Reclamation, Malefic Arrow, Spirit Glaive, Spirit Eclipse, Godslayer) leans on ranged/RAP scaling, and Sen'jin's Apprentice turns a share of your Agility and Intellect into ranged attack power and spell power, so your shots scale off both. You bank a Spirit resource. Reclamation collects a Spirit on hit, Spirit Hunting and Loa Spirits generate extra Spirits from your casts, and you spend the stack with Spirit Eclipse (damage per active Spirit, each one exploding on impact) or convert it with Frenzied Spirits for crit and self-healing. Around that core you plant Serpent Wards: Call of Sseratus summons a pack of them for sustained damage, Voodoo Fire lets their attacks proc extra hits, and Hexfire Mass can drop one on top of an enemy. The True Spirit makes each Ward or Spirit you summon empower your next Spirit Glaive or Spirit Eclipse. Burst rides cooldowns. Mimic Ward summons a ward that echoes your Malefic Arrow, Reclamation and Loa's Brew onto your target, and talents add extra mimics to turn it into a stacked damage window. Veil of Darkness hangs a voodoo curse that increases all the damage you deal to that target. Control comes from Spirit Shock (silence + interrupt) and your Jinx layer (Hex of Malice spread by Shadowflare, plus a Malefic Arrow debuff line). Escape and defensives are dedicated cooldowns, Shadowstalker (stealth that clears harmful effects and boosts speed) and Shadow Avatar / Fool's Play, rather than a healer-style single defensive.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   Spirit Shock silences and interrupts, Hex of Malice spreads through Shadowflare, and Hex of Death cuts a target's healing received via Malefic Arrow. You shut a target off, not just damage it.
*   Shadowstalker clears all harmful effects and boosts speed for a clean disengage. Frenzied Spirits and Spiritual Devotee convert banked Spirits into a self-heal and a steady mana refund, so you recover between fights without a heal spell.
*   Reclamation, Malefic Arrow, Spirit Glaive and a Spirit-fueled Spirit Eclipse that explodes each Spirit on impact, with a pack of Serpent Wards from Call of Sseratus and The True Spirit behind them. This threatens real sustained damage.

Weaknesses

*   Shadowstalker wipes harmful effects on its own timer and that is the entire answer to CC. No on-demand stun or fear break, so a stunlock opener landed while it is down turns off every reset you have.
*   The engine needs hits landing to keep the Spirit stack turning. Deny you the time to bank Spirits and your Spirit Eclipse damage and your Spiritual Devotee mana return choke at the same moment.
*   Group healing exists and it is light. Brew Lobber auto-tosses brews across the party, Brew Splash spreads brew healing near your target, and the class pool adds Healing Ward. This is still a ranged-DPS tree and cannot stand in as a group healer.

Stat priority

Attack Power›Critical Strike›Agility›Intellect/Spirit›Spell Power›Ranged Hit (only until your specials stop missing a +3-level target)›Haste (lowest — it comes from Shadow Avatar/buffs, not gear)

Rotations

Solo / questing

1) Keep Serpent Wards up via Call of Sseratus. Resummon the pack when it expires so they're always adding damage, and let Voodoo Fire proc extra hits off them. 2) Fill your ranged pressure with Reclamation (usable while moving, and it collects a Spirit for your sustain) and Malefic Arrow, which also applies Hex of Death to cut the target's healing and, via Serpent's Guile, leaves a Nature damage-over-time. 3) Use Spirit Glaive to cut the target's Shadow Resistance and set up a hit, then spend banked Spirits with Spirit Eclipse (bigger with more Spirits stacked, each exploding on impact). 4) Weave The True Spirit: each Ward or Spirit you summon empowers your next Spirit Glaive or Spirit Eclipse. 5) Keep Hex of Malice rolling with Umbral Glaive. Its glaive refreshes Hex of Malice's duration and leeches health, so you re-up the dot without re-casting it. Fire Mimic Ward on a tough target so its ward echoes your Malefic Arrow/Reclamation onto it for a burst window. On 2+ pulls, spread Hex of Malice with Shadowflare and use Spirit Shock to shut off a dangerous caster. 6) Between pulls, let Spirit generation (Loa Spirits, Reclamation, Spirit Hunting) and Frenzied Spirits' self-heal top you off, using Spiritual Devotee's per-Spirit mana return to refill before pulling again.

Dungeon

1) Drop your Serpent Wards (Call of Sseratus) before or on the pull so they add sustained damage through the fight. 2) Apply Hex of Malice (spread by Shadowflare) and keep Hex of Death rolling via Malefic Arrow on the kill target to cut its healing and boost your throughput. 3) Build and spend Spirits: Reclamation and Spirit Hunting to bank them, Spirit Eclipse to dump, empowered by The True Spirit off every Ward or Spirit summon. 4) Use Spirit Shock to silence or interrupt a caster add, and spread Hex of Malice into trash for AoE. 5) If you get focused, Shadowstalker to clear harmful effects and reset, or Frenzied Spirits for the crit-and-self-heal window. 6) Line up burst on priority targets: Veil of Darkness first to amp all your damage to that target, Mimic Ward so its echoes stack your Malefic Arrow/Reclamation onto it, then Shadow Avatar (which also cuts Spirit Glaive's cooldown via Vol'jin's Blessing) and Fool's Play. The spec is built to contribute sustained ranged damage, not to stand and heal.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Mana-based, with per-ability costs softened two ways. Shadowhunter reduces the mana cost of all your spells and abilities by a flat percentage. Spirit actively refills you: Spiritual Devotee restores a share of maximum mana every time you collect a Spirit, on top of Frenzied Spirits' self-heal. Spirits come from Reclamation on hit, Spirit Hunting's chance on Malefic Arrow, and Loa Spirits, and they double as your Spirit Eclipse ammunition. Keeping the Spirit engine turning is both your damage and your sustain. It can still go dry if a target denies you time to land hits and rebuild Spirits, because that inflow is what feeds Spiritual Devotee's mana return and your Eclipse fuel alike.**

Difficulty**Weave ranged nukes — Reclamation, Malefic Arrow, Spirit Glaive — with Serpent Wards out, press Spirit Eclipse when you have Spirits, and you function. Full value is a Spirit economy problem first: banking with Reclamation and Spirit Hunting, spending Spirit Eclipse at the right stack count, reading Spiritual Devotee's mana return for sustain. Then empowering hits with The True Spirit off well-timed Ward and Spirit summons, holding Hex of Malice and Hex of Death uptime for the healing cut and damage bonuses, putting Spirit Shock on the right cast, and choosing between Shadowstalker's clear-and-reset and Shadow Avatar or Fool's Play as burst. A lot of moving parts for one character.**
