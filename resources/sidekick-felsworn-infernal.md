Retail feel

Closest to a Warlock flavor with Demon Hunter trim, though mechanically it's its own thing. The Inner Demon window transforms your spenders' riders, turning Fel Fireball into a Felstrike and adding Ruin's damage-over-time tail and Felwrath's pack-punishing blast, and it plays like a timed Metamorphosis power spike. The Infernal drop mirrors Warlock's summoned-add burst. The difference is that this is a ranged Felfury-builder-spender caster driven off Spell Power, and its self-healing is drain-flavored and thin. Manafeed converts a target's burned mana into health for you, closer to a Drain Life than a real defensive, backed only by the class kit's Hateforged Barrier absorb. Don't expect Warlock-grade sustain. No retail spec this maps to is a healer, so treat any support billing as flavor the kit doesn't back up. Its real group contribution is the passive Fel Infusion party/raid crit buff plus AoE burst and the fear-lock on Infernal.

Playstyle

You build and spend Felfury. Fel Fireball is your filler, generating 1 Felfury per cast and usable while moving, and your spenders (Sargeron Smite, Ruin, and the AoE Felwrath) consume it, with Ruin and Felwrath costing 2 Felfury each. Energy is a secondary resource: Sargeron Smite refunds some, Annihilation speeds your Energy regeneration while it runs, and Archimonde's Wrath makes your Felfury spenders crit more often, adding crit chance for every 10 Energy you're sitting on at the moment you cast. The floor loop is Fel Fireball into Sargeron Smite on cooldown, dropping Ruin as your hardest-hitting nuke (it pierces all absorbs) whenever you have the Felfury for it, and keeping a Bane of Chaos rolling on the target (only one Bane at a time). Layered on top is the Inner Demon window, the kit's periodic power state that upgrades several abilities' riders. Fel Fireball unleashes a Felstrike through Fel Apprentice, Ruin gains a damage-over-time tail, Felwrath hits dense packs even harder, and Sargeron Smite echoes a second cast free of cost. Infernal drops a pet to fight beside you, and Infernal Summoner gives your direct damage a chance to reset its cooldown. Control is soft. Cripple slows movement and casting, Sargeras Embrace is a channeled snare that also slows the target hard while you beam it, and your AoE lockdowns are Infernal, which horrifies everything in the drop radius while summoning its pet, plus the class kit's Whispers of the Pit, a ground-targeted AoE fear-and-slow that can break when its victims take damage. Self-healing is thin but not zero. Manafeed burns an enemy's mana and restores health equal to what it burns, dead weight against mana-less enemies, and the class kit's Hateforged Barrier adds a Stamina-scaled absorb that also regenerates a slice of your max health each tick (~2% per tick, ~20s cooldown when specced, though the Annihilation damage-return can eat into the absorb). Neither is a sustain engine — most damage you take stays taken. Endgame note: once your crit is high (a near-BiS raid build sits around ~44% self-buffed) the rotation shifts. You lean on procced Felstrikes and the crit-driven Energy return, so Ruin rarely needs a hard cast and you can skip most Sargeron Smite. At that crit level, drop Chaotic Intuition for Malice of Gul'dan, since Malice procs Felstrikes off your crits (removing the need to manually Felstrike to fuel the crit-damage benefit) and out-scales the longer a fight runs.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   Felwrath's AoE Shadowflame and Infernal's fear-on-drop flip a clumped teamfight, with Fel Infusion handing the party a passive crit buff on the side.
*   Infernal horrifies a whole pack on the drop, and Cripple plus Sargeras Embrace's channeled snare give you kiting and peel tools most nukers do not get. Whispers of the Pit adds a second breakable AoE fear. None of it is a true interrupt.
*   Ruin ignores shields and absorbs outright. Inner Demon stacks riders onto the window, Fel Fireball Felstrikes and Energy refunds, for a periodic spike.

Weaknesses

*   Manafeed is your only heal and it returns health equal to the mana it burns, which is nothing against a mana-less enemy. A shield or immunity costs Ability Essence: Hateforged Barrier, or Illidan's Guile, which stops you acting. Skip those and a stun chain kills you.
*   Every piece of control is soft: breakable fear, movement and cast slows, a channel-snare, no hard single-target CC. Anti-lockout is one talent deep in Nether Focus, which turns your shield cast into an interrupt and silence cleanse. Without that pick, one caster locking you out ends the builder-spender loop.
*   The rotation is casts and Felfury, all interruptible, and there is no escape or gap-opener. Melee that closes just wins.

Rotations

Solo / questing

Open with Bane of Chaos on a target that will live. Only one Bane can be active at a time. Felwrath cleaves any pack for Shadowflame, but its big bonus only triggers at 5+ targets while Inner Demon is up. So in 5+ packs, if you're specced into Illidari Magi (periodic damage returns extra Felfury), you can spam Felwrath instead of weaving Bane into the mix. On smaller packs, open with Felwrath, or drop Infernal to fear-lock the pack and add an infernal pet. Maintain Fel Fireball as filler between everything; it builds Felfury and stays castable while moving. Use Sargeron Smite at 3+ Felfury when your Energy is low so its Energy generation isn't wasted, not blindly on cooldown. Drop Ruin as your hardest single-target nuke when you have the Felfury — it pierces absorbs. Bank into the Inner Demon window on a tough pull for the upgraded riders (Fel Fireball's Felstrike, Ruin's absorb tail, Energy refunds via Dark Magician). Slow a runner with Cripple, or snare-and-nuke with Sargeras Embrace's channeled beam. There is no self-heal in the kit, so manage danger by killing fast and pre-emptively fearing packs with Infernal rather than expecting to heal through a bad pull. Felbreak shuts down a dangerous enemy cast.

Dungeon

No ally-targeted healing here, and no self-heal either. Your dungeon value is Fel Infusion's passive party/raid crit buff plus CC and burst AoE, not HPS, so treat the rotation as pull-control and burst. Pull: Cripple anything that runs, drop a Bane on the kill target. On bigger pulls, open with Infernal to fear the pack and buy the tank time, or Felwrath for AoE burst to help melt trash. Damage: Fel Fireball filler into Sargeron Smite and Ruin (absorb-piercing) on the priority target, banking Inner Demon windows for the upgraded riders. Utility: Felbreak is a real interrupt on caster mobs, and that's your main group-facing contribution alongside the passive crit buff. Sargeras Embrace's channeled snare can peel a runner. With no self-heal to lean on, avoid overpulling and rely on the tank and healer for your survival. Plan positioning around that gap.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**A Felfury builder-spender loop layered over Energy. Fel Fireball generates 1 Felfury per cast. Your spenders Ruin and Felwrath consume 2 Felfury each, and Sargeron Smite is your on-cooldown Felfury spender. Energy is the secondary pool. Sargeron Smite refunds some, Annihilation and Sunder feed it, Dark Magician refunds Energy on Ruin, Sunder and Felwrath while Inner Demon is active, and Archimonde's Wrath rewards spending at high Energy with extra crit on your Felfury spenders. Nothing in the kit is mana-gated, so Spirit is not a resource. It is the spec's top throughput stat anyway: Hidden Power turns Spirit into spell damage and Nether Spirit turns it into crit, and bisbeard weights it well above Intellect.**

Difficulty**Build Felfury with Fel Fireball, dump Sargeron Smite and Ruin, keep a Bane rolling, and the single-target damage is reasonable. Full value is harder. The builder-spender cadence has to stay tight, spenders want to land inside the Inner Demon window for the upgraded riders, and Archimonde's Wrath crits want high Energy behind them. All of that while threading Sargeras Embrace's channel-snare and Cripple to keep casting under pressure, with zero self-heal to bail you out of a positioning mistake.**
