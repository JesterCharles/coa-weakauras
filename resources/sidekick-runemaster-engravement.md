Retail feel

Closest to Enhancement Shaman. It's an Agility melee that weaves autoattacks and weapon-rune procs (Elemental Carvings, Runeblade, Power Engraving) with personal burst (Fist of the Ancients, Primordial Strength, Zenith). The imbue-and-strike loop and hybrid physical/elemental damage read like Enhancement's Windfury/Flametongue melee. Like Enhancement it also brings group utility without being a healer. Runes of Quickness is a party/raid spell-haste aura, Runic Brand stacks a magic-damage-taken debuff on the target, and Guarding Rune drops a magic-damage-reduction barrier for the group. Survival is personal mitigation (Granite Resolve, Leystone Springs), not a healing kit.

Playstyle

You weave melee autoattacks with instant and short nukes. They proc Elemental Carvings (Fire/Earth/Air/Water) and, via Elemental Carvings on Fist of the Ancients, a random bonus: Fire gives armor penetration, Water mana over time, Earth a heal-over-time, Air melee haste. Primordial Blast goes on cooldown, Fist of the Ancients is your triggering strike, and Runic Brand stacks a magic-damage-taken mark on the target (Leyborn/Marked) that its detonation cashes in. Runic Tempest resets Fist of the Ancients and opens your Runeshroud abilities, Fists of Power engraves your fists for a melee-haste window, and Power Overwhelming resets Runic Brand off Runeblade and Primordial Blast procs. Zenith, with two charges via Echoes of Eternity, and its Runelord follow-up are your ramp burst. AoE comes from Runic Explosion feeding Wild Steam and Ley Power drawing extra damage from every nearby enemy. On the support side, Leyline Disturbance projects a party melee/ranged haste aura and shaves incoming stun duration, and Guarding Rune drops a barrier that reduces Magic damage taken for allies inside. Self-preservation is proc- and rune-based rather than a spammable heal loop: Granite Shield from Runic Tattoos: Earth, the Earth Elemental Carving heal-over-time proc, and Protective Warding cooling your Rune of Guarding.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   Leyline Disturbance hands the party a haste aura and shaves stun duration, and Guarding Rune barriers them against magic damage. You do all that while bursting with Primordial Blast, Fist of the Ancients, Runic Brand and Zenith.
*   Ley Power ramps damage per nearby enemy and Runic Explosion into Wild Steam pays you for being surrounded. Being outnumbered is the shape this spec wants.
*   The Earth Elemental Carving heal-over-time, Granite Shield under Runic Tattoos: Earth, and Water-Carving's mana return keep you in the fight without a dedicated healer, with Protective Warding cooling Rune of Guarding on top.

Weaknesses

*   No repeatable on-demand heal for yourself or an ally. Sustain is RNG procs, the Earth Elemental Carving heal-over-time and Granite Shield, and RNG does not out-heal sustained pressure.
*   Damage and utility come in windows rather than a stream. Primordial Blast is on cooldown, Runic Brand needs its mark to stack and detonate, and Zenith, Runic Tempest and Fists of Power are windowed. With those down mid-fight there is little left to press.
*   Defense is Guarding Rune plus RNG mitigation procs. Granite Resolve adds an on-demand physical damage reduction usable while stunned, but there is no true absorb and nothing against magic, so once Guarding Rune drops spell pressure finds a soft target. Escapes are class-kit picks.

Stat priority

Hit (until your specials stop missing a +3-level target)›Expertise (until your attacks stop being dodged/parried)›Agility›Critical Strike›Haste

Rotations

Solo / questing

Open with Primordial Blast (nuke) while closing distance, then melee to proc Weapon Engravings and trigger Fist of the Ancients. Take whatever Elemental Carving procs (Water = mana, Earth = self-heal-over-time, Fire = armor pen, Air = haste). Keep Runic Brand on the target so its mark stacks and detonates for a chunk, using Power Overwhelming resets to re-apply it. Fire Zenith (with its Runelord follow-up) as your burst on a tough target. Use Runic Tempest to reset Fist of the Ancients and open your Runeshroud abilities, and Fists of Power for a haste window. On packs, use Ley Power to draw damage from every nearby enemy, and Runic Explosion into Wild Steam for AoE. Lean on the Earth Elemental Carving heal-over-time and Granite Shield if you pull extra adds and need to ride out the damage.

Dungeon

Pull: run Leyline Disturbance so the party gets its haste aura, and set your Runic Tattoo to whatever the group needs (Earth for Granite Shield uptime, Water if your own mana margin is thin). Sustain: keep meleeing for Weapon Engraving/Elemental Carving procs. Water Carving keeps your mana up, Earth Carving keeps your HP up without drawing from the healer. Stack and detonate Runic Brand on the priority kill target. Weave Primordial Blast and Fist of the Ancients on cooldown, using Runic Tempest to reset Fist of the Ancients on packs, and Ley Power/Runic Explosion into Wild Steam for AoE. Emergency: drop Guarding Rune so the group, and you, take reduced Magic damage during a heavy magic phase. Lean on Granite Shield and the Earth-Carving heal if you're eating physical cleave, and let Protective Warding accelerate Rune of Guarding after a big hit. On bosses, drop Power Engraving as a group DPS cooldown and save it for the kill. It's a stand-in-it zone that raises the party's magic damage and critical strike.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Mana-based. Nearly every ability costs a percentage of base mana, Primordial Blast, Fist of the Ancients, Runic Brand and the cooldowns included, and there's no secondary point currency. Your other resource is the Weapon Engraving and Elemental Carving proc economy off autoattacks, which fuels Fist of the Ancients, Wild Steam and the mitigation and mana procs. In-combat mana relief comes from procs too: Leystone Springs restoring mana on auto attacks, boosted under Runic Tattoos: Water, Flowing Rivers improving Replenishment, and the Water Elemental Carving. Without those landing, a long fight runs you dry. Burst is paced by cooldowns rather than a stored spender pool: Zenith, Runic Tempest, and Power Overwhelming resetting Runic Brand.**

Difficulty**Autoattack for engraving procs, keep Runic Brand up, press Primordial Blast and Fist of the Ancients on cooldown — it functions. Aligning it is another matter: Runic Brand re-applications sequenced around Power Overwhelming resets, Zenith, Runic Tempest and Fists of Power timed against the group's burst, the right Runic Tattoo picked per fight. Elemental Carvings are RNG you manage rather than control — bank Water for mana and Earth for the self-heal, time Fire and Air for burst.**
