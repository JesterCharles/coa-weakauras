Retail feel

Closest to Vengeance Demon Hunter, re-tuned as a pure damage spec rather than a tank. The pieces line up. Infernal Strike is a literally-named engage. Pestilence of Apocalypse plus Hellfire Form's tick gives you an Immolation-Aura-style pulsing AoE burn. Demonfire is a Soul-Fragment-style secondary resource, generated by a filler (Seeking Flame, Infernal Strike) and spent on finishers (Hellmaw, Skulltaker, Flames of Xoroth, Hellfire Form). Hellfire Form is also the Metamorphosis-style transformation cooldown. Defense is where it diverges hard. Vengeance's actual survival kit, Demon Spikes and Fiery Brand's self-heal and Soul Cleave lifesteal, isn't present here, and there isn't even a self-shield to stand in for it. That makes it far squishier than the retail spec it otherwise resembles.

Playstyle

A melee/caster hybrid running on Rage plus a secondary Demonfire stack resource. Core loop: weave a cheap filler strike in Infernal Strike, which generates 2 Demonfire and has its cost reduced by Combusting Blade, while firing Seeking Flame for Fire damage that generates 1 Demonfire and slows the target. Periodically dump stacked Demonfire into Hellmaw for a big Shadowflame hit-and-burn or into Flames of Xoroth for AoE, both scaling up per Demonfire consumed. Unleash Hellfire turns every Demonfire dump into a bonus Fire blast on nearby enemies. Layered on top is Hellfire Form, a demon transformation fueled by your Demonfire stacks that increases your magic damage and pulses Fire damage to everything around you while it lasts. Keep Pestilence of Apocalypse up almost constantly so nearby enemies take increased Fire damage from you, and Calamity strengthens and lengthens its Unleash burst. Your AoE comes from Flames of Xoroth, Hellfire Form's pulse, Unleash Hellfire's detonations, and Rain of Chaos crashing down when you cast Flames of Xoroth with a full 6 Demonfire. This spec has essentially no defensive cooldown of its own beyond Flamewrath's incidental on-hit absorb. The class kit can plug that hole with Suffuse (a self-heal that also reduces damage taken, which Boiling Blood amplifies further) or Black Shield's absorb, both picks that compete with damage. Skip them and survival is about killing fast and keeping targets slowed — not mitigating.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   Hellmaw, Skulltaker and Flames of Xoroth all hit harder per Demonfire consumed. Dump a banked pool inside Hellfire Form's Fire damage and crit window and it lands.
*   Pestilence of Apocalypse raises Fire damage taken in a field, and Hellstorm plus Hellfire Form's pulsing burn work inside it. Into the Maw's Hellfire Explosion and Unbound Inferno clear packs and punish BG clumps.
*   Infernal Strike engages and generates Demonfire on the way in, and Seeking Flame's slow keeps pressure on at close-to-mid range when a target tries to peel off. There is no speed proc behind it.

Weaknesses

*   No healing or lifesteal in the spec at all, just Flamewrath's incidental on-hit shield. Any real defensive is a class-kit pick: Suffuse, which heals and trims damage taken, or Black Shield's Demon's Blood absorb. Skip those and you are a melee DPS with nothing.
*   No cleanse, no trinket equivalent, no CC-immunity cooldown anywhere in the list, and no hard CC or silence past Seeking Flame's slow. A chain-CC opener goes unanswered.
*   Hellfire Form and the Demonfire dumps swing fights, but Rage only builds while you stay engaged. Kited or CC'd out of Seeking Flame range, the whole engine stalls.

Stat priority

Spell Hit (until your spells stop missing a +3-level target)›Spell Power›Strength›Critical Strike›Haste›Intellect

Rotations

Solo / questing

1) Open with Seeking Flame (ranged instant, builds Demonfire, slows) or Infernal Strike to engage (also builds 2 Demonfire). 2) Apply and refresh Pestilence of Apocalypse on cooldown for the solo damage boost; nearby enemies take more Fire damage from you. 3) Weave Infernal Strike/Flames of Xoroth as filler between cooldowns. 4) Fire Seeking Flame whenever it's up for Demonfire and the slow. 5) Once Demonfire stacks are high, dump via Hellmaw for a big Shadowflame hit, Skulltaker for a crit finisher, or Flames of Xoroth for AoE, letting Hellmaw's Into the Maw explosion and Unbound Inferno add splash. 6) Pop Hellfire Form against tougher fights, banking Demonfire first for max duration, for the Fire-damage/crit window and cooldown-free Seeking Flame. 7) On packs, lean on Hellstorm and Hellfire Form for sustained AoE burn. 8) There is no defensive button — kill fast and use Seeking Flame's slow to keep dangerous mobs off you.

Dungeon

This kit has no healing and no defensive cooldown, so there is no real "sustain" phase to give. The honest dungeon rotation is pull/burst/slow, not heal/support: 1) Engage with Seeking Flame or Infernal Strike. 2) Keep Pestilence of Apocalypse up so the pack takes more Fire damage from you, and use Seeking Flame's slow to peel loose adds. 3) Weave Infernal Strike/Flames of Xoroth filler, dumping Demonfire via Hellmaw, Skulltaker, or Flames of Xoroth for burst. 4) On trash packs, pop Hellfire Form for pulsing AoE burn on bigger pulls, and let Hellstorm and Hellmaw's Hellfire Explosion detonate off your Demonfire dumps. 5) There is no party heal, cleanse, shield, or self-defensive to lean on for anyone. Group safety comes from killing threats fast and slowing them, not from mitigating damage after the fact, so don't take pulls that assume you can eat damage.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Rage is primary, generated through combat like a Warrior and spent on Infernal Strike and your other Rage-cost abilities. Demonfire stacks are the secondary. Seeking Flame and Infernal Strike generate them, with a proc chance from Brimstone Splinters, and Hellmaw, Skulltaker, Flames of Xoroth and Hellfire Form consume them for scaling bonuses. It can go dry. Rage generation requires staying actively engaged and Demonfire trickles in only from your generators, so kiting, roots or being peeled off stalls both resources at once and shuts down your burst.**

Difficulty**The core loop is simple to execute passably: Seeking Flame filler, melee filler, Demonfire dumped into Hellmaw, Skulltaker or Flames of Xoroth, Pestilence of Apocalypse kept up. Then the detail work — dumping Demonfire at high stack counts, banking stacks before Hellfire Form for max duration, refreshing Pestilence of Apocalypse and timing its Calamity-boosted Unleash without clipping your Rage-spenders. You also have no defensive cooldown at all, so Seeking Flame's slow and Infernal Strike positioning are how you avoid damage you can't heal.**
