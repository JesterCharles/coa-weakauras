Retail feel

Closest to Restoration Shaman, with a Discipline Priest wrinkle. The AoE group heals read like Chain Heal and Healing Rain: Moonflow spreads moonwell waters to everyone nearby, and Prayer of Elune heals a whole party while dispelling magic. Aspect of the Goddess is a proactive healing-amp cooldown in the Water Shield/spirit-link family, and Moonlit Slumber, a mana-draining sleep, plays like a Hex/Bind Elemental control tool on a party-support kit. All of it wears the same lunar and moonwell theme. Layered on top is a Discipline-style Atonement loop, since Aspect of the Goddess turns your ranged autoattacks into ally healing and consuming Scattered Stars into extra heals. Mechanically it's Resto Shaman's group-heal toolkit fused with Disc's your-damage-is-your-healing philosophy.

Playstyle

Moon Priest runs two resources at once. Energy fuels its attacks, Moon Arrow at range and Huntress Shot, and those same attacks pay back mana, while its heals are priced as a percentage of max mana. So the loop is: weave Moon Arrow and Huntress Shot between casts to keep the mana engine running, drop direct heals (Moonwell, Hand of Elune, Touch of Moonlight) and AoE heals (Moonflow, Prayer of Elune) to keep the group topped, and use Aspect of the Goddess to boost your healing and make consuming Scattered Stars heal allies. Several stacking mechanics run underneath. Lunar Phase is generated by crits and by Celestial Mind/Moon Gazing, boosting ranged haste via Rapid Cycle. Scattered Star is built by Blessed By The Stars and Huntress Shot's Lunar Eclipse, then dumped into Moonflow, which then triggers with no cooldown. Moonwater is applied by your heals via Rippling Moonwater. And the Full Moon / New Moon cycle from Cycle of the Moon swings Hand of Elune between more healing and cheaper casts through Full Cycle. Most of the play is knowing when to spend a stack and when to bank it. Because Aspect of the Goddess turns your ranged damage into ally healing and your Scattered Stars feed Rampaging Waters' healing and crit buff, this spec can fight and heal at the same time rather than sitting in the backline.

Strengths & weaknesses

Strengths

*   Aspect of the Goddess turns ranged autoattacks and Scattered-Star consumption into ally healing, and Rippling Moonwater and Rampaging Waters feed off your damage as well. You fight and sustain at once instead of sitting in the backline.
*   Moonlit Slumber is a long single-target sleep that also drains the target's mana, and Cosmic Shift phases an ally into damage-proof improved stealth. Lock a threat down or pull a partner out.
*   Prayer of Elune, Moonflow and Moonwell cover a group fight, amplified by Aspect of the Goddess. A stacked skirmish swings hard on that.

Weaknesses

*   Heals are priced as a percentage of max mana and the regen engine depends on Moon Arrow and Huntress Shot landing. Kited, silenced, or kept out of weapon range, the pool dries fast.
*   Your biggest AoE value is Moonflow chaining during Lunar Eclipse with Aspect of the Goddess up, and both assume you are free to act. An interrupt and silence train that shuts off your ranged attacks starves your mana and your incidental healing together.
*   No bubble, no ice-block equivalent, no hard immunity. Vial of Moonwell Water is a real CC-break, purging all crowd control with brief immunity while stunned or feared. Cleansing reaches harmful magic through Prayer of Elune and poison and disease through Elune's Purification. Curses stay on.

Stat priority

Spell Power›Haste (extra Torrent/Slipstream/Pond ticks, faster Deluge/Water Bolt casts)›Critical Strike (Scepter of the Moon, Moon's Embrace, Enchanted Water)›Intellect (mana pool + spell crit)›Spirit/MP5 (sustain through long tide-healing channels)

Rotations

Solo / questing

Open at range with Moon Arrow, the default filler: cheap Energy, usable while moving, regens mana. Weave in Huntress Shot to build Scattered Stars; its Lunar Eclipse applies extra stars via the Moon Priest passive. Once stacks are up, dump Moonflow. During Lunar Eclipse it triggers with no cooldown and consumes Scattered Stars for extra value. Keep crits flowing to build Lunar Phase (Moon Gazing/Celestial Mind), and pop Celestial Form when you want a crit-and-cooldown burst window. Between pulls, cast a self-target Moonwell/Hand of Elune. Your heals apply Moonwater via Rippling Moonwater, and Aspect of the Goddess keeps output up as you keep attacking, which cuts downtime versus sitting to drink.

Dungeon

Pre-pull: keep Aspect of the Goddess active and pre-heal or top the tank with Hand of Elune. On the pull, open with Prayer of Elune or Moonflow to top the group before damage lands. Sustain phase: Moonflow on cooldown (or free during Lunar Eclipse), Prayer of Elune for stacked group damage plus a magic dispel, Hand of Elune / Touch of Moonlight as tank filler and single-target spike coverage. Keep Huntress Shot/Moon Arrow flowing to fund mana and feed Aspect of the Goddess' incidental group healing. Emergency: Aspect of the Goddess as a healing-amp cooldown ahead of telegraphed burst, Celestial Form to accelerate your heal cooldowns during a crunch, and Stellar Convergence to blanket the raid with Eclipsing Arrows so your Huntress Shots heal marked allies. Weave Moonlit Slumber to lock down a loose add. For cleanses, use Prayer of Elune (magic) plus Final Prayer's extra dispel reactively. Note there is no poison, disease, or curse cleanse in the kit, only harmful-magic removal.

Resource & difficulty

Resource**Dual resource. Energy powers the ranged attacks, Moon Arrow and Huntress Shot, and those same abilities pay mana back, so they function as a mana battery. Heals are priced as a percentage of max mana rather than flat costs. Hand of Elune, Moonflow, Prayer of Elune, Moonwell and the rest scale their cost with your pool, so a bigger pool via Intellect funds more heals and scales their output. It can go dry. Anything that stops you landing Moon Arrow or Huntress Shot, whether kited, silenced or out of range, removes the income side of the loop while the percentage costs keep draining.**

Difficulty**The direct heals — Moonwell, Hand of Elune, Touch of Moonlight — and the AoE ones, Moonflow and Prayer of Elune, are simple to cast and productive on their own.
