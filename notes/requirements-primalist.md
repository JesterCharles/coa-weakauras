## 3. Pets

- [x] Does this class summon a pet? **Yes — Wildwalker only.** A tamed beast
      ("animal spirit", Spirit Beast Master L10 passive: "You may now tame
      beasts in Azeroth to aid you as a companion"). Permanent (dies/dismissed
      → Recall Animal Spirit revives). The other three specs have none — the
      27 pet-ish skillbook mentions are all `specs=['wildwalker']` except
      Primal Rush ("you and your pet", class-wide, works petless).
- [x] Player-pressed pet abilities? **Yes:** Throat Clamp (pet dash interrupt
      + school lock — "your only interrupt", Sidekick dungeon rotation),
      Primal Shred (pet AoE bleed, in the main rotation), Rylak's Bite (you
      AND pet rush, 2 charges, main rotation), Bring Me Their Bones (pet-stack
      mark).
- [x] Pet buffs/debuffs to track? **Yes:** Misha's Rage / Leokk's Fury
      (Legacy of Rexxar, on you and pet — tracked on player), Rylak's
      Blessing (pet damage/crit stacks — pet unit), Savage Frenzy (enrage,
      you and pet — tracked on player), Bring Me Their Bones (5 stacks on the
      TARGET — target band).
- [x] Does pet absence gate the rotation? **Yes.** Primal Shred, Rylak's
      Bite, Throat Clamp, the entire Legacy of Rexxar bleed engine and the
      Focus economy all need the pet alive. **NO PET alert** (the
      Stormbringer/Tinker shape): ask the PET unit for the `Primalist Pet
      Scaling Aura` family (524057, 524060–524063, `Primalist Pet Scaling
      (All)` 805948 — db.exil.es names, not tooltip-verified → §6), fire on
      absence, gate on a wildwalker signature spell.
- [x] Pet resource bar? **No.** The pet runs on Focus but the player never
      spends it; it is fed passively (Bestial Wrath crits, Rips and Tears).
      A bar for a resource with no player decision attached wastes envelope
      space. Deliberately scoped out; revisit on feedback.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Animal spirit (tamed beast) | Tame/Harness Animal Spirit; Call/Recall Animal Spirit | permanent | presence (scaling aura, pet unit); Rylak's Blessing (pet unit) | NO PET alert; on-me row |

## 4. Primary damage source and the main row

Spec roles (Sidekick-cited, pending in-game — recorded in
`resources/spec-roles.md`):

- **geomancy — damage** ("it stays a self-reliant DPS caster rather than any
  kind of support"; "Not a heal")
- **grovekeeper — healing** ("Grovekeeper is a dual-resource melee-healer";
  "Grovekeeper is a Support spec"; dungeon text is a full healer loop). Kit
  corroboration: 21/60 spec abilities mention heal/absorb/shield (35%) vs
  1–9% on the other three — the ~5x healer signal.
- **mountain-king — tank** ("Solid main-tank for raid bosses — brings combat
  rez"; "Solid dungeon tank — AoE pulls and threat")
- **wildwalker — damage** ("This is a DPS spec, not a healer"; melee bruiser)

| Spec | Primary damage/healing | Main row, in press order (cited from the Rotations text) | Icons | Resource |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Geomancy | Earthshaping-ramped nukes (Stoneshard/Terrasurge spenders) | Geode Barrage → Seismic Tremor → Stoneshard → Terrasurge → Eruption | 5 | Rage (power) + mana |
| Grovekeeper | melee-woven heals (Hammer of Life splash; Seismic + Wildclaw off-role) | Hand of the Earthmother → Spirit Charge → Seismic Wave → Sacred Grove → Wildclaw | 5 | Rage (power) + mana |
| Mountain King | Quake/Mountain Fury cleave + threat | Quake → Seismic Crash → Geode Barrage → Mountain Fury → Mountain Hammer → Rock Barrier | 6 | Rage (power) + mana (minor) |
| Wildwalker | Wildclaw/Totemic Smash weapon strikes + crit-bleed engine | Wildclaw → Rylak's Bite → Totemic Smash → Primal Shred | 4 | Rage (power); pet Focus scoped out (§3) |

Two names the first draft of this table carried are NOT buttons and left the
main rows during the inventory review: **Lithic Lance** (L20 passive — the
conversion proc glows Geode Barrage instead, the Stoke lesson) and **Tears of
the Earthmother** (574326 is rank=Passive dur=-1 — it is the target-band HoT
matched by name). `CD_PER_ROW` derives from the narrowest row: wildwalker's 4
icons, row_w(4)=182px, 28w−2 ≤ 1.2×182 → **7**.

Main-row derivations, one line each, from the spec pages' Rotations sections:
- *Geomancy solo:* "Open with Geode Barrage or Seismic Tremor… Build
  Earthshaping with Geode Barrage, your main spammable filler, watching for
  the Lithic Lance conversion… Spend Earthshaping into Stoneshard, your main
  spender… Fire Terrasurge as a big armor-shredding hit once stacks are
  high… Weave in Eruption once Earthshaping is stacked."
- *Grovekeeper dungeon:* "start Tears of the Earthmother on the tank as your
  primary rejuvenate… Spirit Charge closes to and tops a spike target… Drop
  Seismic Wave when the group is stacked… Emergency: Sacred Grove…"; solo:
  "Weave Hand of the Earthmother… the moment you or a party member drops
  low"; "Wildclaw as filler". Healer main row leads with the heal kit,
  off-role damage tail matches the solo priority.
- *Mountain King solo:* "Open with Mountain Hammer… On 2+ mobs, open with
  Mountain Fury… Spend rage on Quake on cooldown (2 charges/8 sec)… Weave
  Seismic Smash and the Seismic family… Rock Barrier reactively." Quake
  leads as the most-pressed (8s recharge ×2 charges); Seismic Crash is the
  baseline Seismic family member every Mountain King has (Seismic Smash is a
  23-TE choose-one, tracked in the offense row).
- *Wildwalker solo:* "spend rage into Totemic Smash on cooldown… Primal
  Shred instead when 2+ mobs are up… Rylak's Bite on its charges… keep
  Wildclaw as your main filler strike… Frenzied Roar as an
  attack-speed-and-rage cooldown → Savage Frenzy to… burst." Wildclaw first
  as THE filler (most pressed).

**Resource envelope:** every spec spends Rage as primary (`power`, rage) and
carries a mana pool for the utility half ("A secondary layer of cooldowns and
utility costs a % of base mana" — wildwalker; "Mana pays for nearly every
hard-hitting nuke" — geomancy; "Mana fuels the heal and utility kit" —
grovekeeper; "Mana is a small secondary pool" — mountain-king). Envelope:
rage bar (primary, nearest main row) + mana bar. Mountain-king's mana is
`minor` (half height) per its own text; the other three keep full-height mana.
Earthshaping/Geomolding stacks stay in the on-me row, NOT the envelope —
they are aura ramps read as "how big is my next spender", not a spent
resource; and 15/20-cell segment bars would not fit the width lock.
