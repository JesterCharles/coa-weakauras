Server changes

Ascension changelog — 15 changes not yet folded into a release

Newest published change for this class: 2026/08/07. Changelog read 2026-08-07. Most changelog entries are damage numbers that no WeakAura reacts to. This counts the ones nobody has ruled on yet, which is a different and more honest thing than saying the pack is wrong.

  • 2026/08/07outstandingNote: Previous update of Corpse Explosion spell incorrectly noted an increase to its cooldown from 10 seconds to 30 seconds, The actual intended and applied value is 60 seconds.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingYour Summoned Minions will now be instantly summoned when you are resurrected inside a battleground.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingStudy of Death now summons 5 total Zombies, up from 3.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingScourge Disciple has been fixed, your Skeletal Archers now properly provide you haste while active.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingImproved the Necromancer AI, making them less likely to attack targets that are of no-danger to you or nearby party members.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingGraveyard is a brand new spell taught to Necromancers at level 60. You mark a graveyard, and you begin turning corpses below your feet, resurfacing them. Nearby enemies suffer plague damage when a resurfaced corpse is brought back from its grave.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingFoul Invocation now additionally causes you, and your minions to emit a Disease Cloud dealing area of effect damage while active. This disease cloud is affected by modifiers that affect diseases, such as plague scientist, which increases all disease damage dealt by 100% for targets under 20% health.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingFixed a bug where Unholy Runes was not increasing the damage of Crypt Swarm. Additionally, it now increases the damage of Blight and Blight has a 5% chance when it deals damage to reset the cooldown of Corpse Explosion.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingFixed a bug where Sepulchral Might was not increasing your minion’s damage from the spell power gained.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingFixed a bug where Frozen Barbs from Crypt Fiend couldn’t miss.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingFixed a bug where Anti-Magic Shell was applying to the Necromancer and not the minions that were summoned.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingFiend talent value increased to 30% from 20%
  • 2026/07/31outstandingFetid Ward, Glacial Ward, and Bone Ward now persist through death.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingEntomb now only makes the target immune to Physical damage instead of all damage.

Import

Three steps

  1. Copy a string below.
  2. In game, type /wa.
  3. Click Import, paste, confirm.

Re-importing an updated version replaces the old one. If the pack looks unchanged after an update, delete the old group first — WeakAuras keeps what it already has when nothing tells it the pack moved.
