Server changes

Ascension changelog — 13 changes not yet folded into a release

Newest published change for this class: 2026/07/31. Changelog read 2026-08-07. Most changelog entries are damage numbers that no WeakAura reacts to. This counts the ones nobody has ruled on yet, which is a different and more honest thing than saying the pack is wrong.

  • 2026/07/31outstandingWhirling Assault’s damage increase against enemies above 80% HP increased from 300% to 500%.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingThick Skull can now break Horrify effects as well as Stuns.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingRamhorn Rage now doubles your passive health regeneration.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingIron Gut now gives you a 30% reduction to the duration of poison effects, down from 40%, but it now additionally gives a 30% chance to resist the application of poisons entirely.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingIncredibly Strong no longer reduces the maximum range of Maiming Spear, instead increases its duration. Deadzone removal remains untouched.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingHail of Hammers now additionally increases the critical damage of Crush by the same amount.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingGiant Tosser now additionally increases the damage of Headhunter’s Spears from further than 20 yards by 100%, but the additional energy is reduced from 50% down to 15%.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingDrunken Brawler stacks now last 5 seconds, up from 3, and grant 2% dodge chance per stack, up from 1%.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingCrusher added as a switch node with Hail of Hammers, completely removing the cast time of Crush but reducing its damage by 25% and removing its stun.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingCrush now pauses your swing timer when cast instead of restarting it.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingBorn in Blood now grants 10% movement speed per stack, up from 5%, but the max stack count has been decreased to 10, down from 20. Armor Penetration values per stack remain the same.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingBorn in Blood base duration increased to 4 seconds, up from 3.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingAxe Twirling is now toggleable, and has no cooldown and no fixed duration, but it reduces your damage dealt by 15%. Talents affecting Axe Twirling have been adjusted with this new duration and no cooldown in mind.

Import

Three steps

  1. Copy a string below.
  2. In game, type /wa.
  3. Click Import, paste, confirm.

Re-importing an updated version replaces the old one. If the pack looks unchanged after an update, delete the old group first — WeakAuras keeps what it already has when nothing tells it the pack moved.
