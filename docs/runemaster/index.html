Ascension changelog — 11 changes not yet folded into a release

Newest published change for this class: 2026/08/03. Changelog read 2026-08-07. Most changelog entries are damage numbers that no WeakAura reacts to. This counts the ones nobody has ruled on yet, which is a different and more honest thing than saying the pack is wrong.

  • 2026/08/03outstandingSwapped Mind Over Matter in the Class Tree with Battle Engravings in the Talent Tree, meaning all Runemasters can now take Battle Engravings.
  • 2026/08/03outstandingMind Wrath - has been buffed as a switch node with Battle Engravings. It now affects 2 targets up from 1.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingUnleashed Flame Glyph duration has increased from 6 seconds to 10 seconds (2 extra ticks).
  • 2026/07/31outstandingThere is work being done to improve the Weapon Enhancements.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingTempo now additionally increases the damage of Runic Brand.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingRunic Explosion base damage has been increased by approximately 25%.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingRunic Brand Attack Power scaling has been reduced from 30% -> 28%, but now has an additional 15% Spell Power scaling.(This spell had zero spell power scaling before)
  • 2026/07/31outstandingPrimordial Strength Attack Power to Spell Power scaling conversion increased from 25% -> 30%
  • 2026/07/31outstandingOverloaded Flame Glyph duration has increased from 5 seconds to 12 seconds, but the tick rate has been reduced from 1 second to 1.5 seconds (3 extra ticks).
  • 2026/07/31outstandingHoarfrost AoE Range increased from 5 yds to 8 yds.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingFist of the Ancients Attack Power scaling has been reduced from 65% -> 58%, but now has an additional 20% Spell Power Scaling (This spell had zero spell power scaling before)

Import

Three steps

  1. Copy a string below.
  2. In game, type /wa.
  3. Click Import, paste, confirm.

Re-importing an updated version replaces the old one. If the pack looks unchanged after an update, delete the old group first — WeakAuras keeps what it already has when nothing tells it the pack moved.
