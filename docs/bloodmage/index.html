Import

Packs

Take the all-specs pack unless you only ever play the one spec. They are built from the same source and lay out identically.

How to import — three steps
  1. Copy a string below.
  2. In game, type /wa.
  3. Click Import, paste, confirm.

Re-importing an updated version replaces the old one. If the pack looks unchanged after an update, delete the old group first — WeakAuras keeps what it already has when nothing tells it the pack moved.

Ascension changelog — 12 changes not yet folded into a release

Newest published change for this class: 2026/07/31. Changelog read 2026-08-07. Most changelog entries are damage numbers that no WeakAura reacts to. This counts the ones nobody has ruled on yet, which is a different and more honest thing than saying the pack is wrong.

12 entries
  • 2026/07/31outstandingWicked Howl now has a 2 minute cooldown down from 3 minutes.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingTransgression no longer requires Insatiable, but its duration has been reduced from 30s to 15s, and it no longer casts a free Vampiric Fang.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingRage (talent) now provides 10% more Spell power from AP.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingForbidden Power now increases your spell penetration by an amount equal to 100% of your armor penetration.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingEternal Curse is now instant cast.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingClass tree connections have been repathed, creating more build diversity in the first tier of the talent tree. Alacrity can now connect to Dark Curse and Bloody Sacrifice.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingBloodbolt rage cost has been reduced from 40 to 30.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingBlood Shards now deal Spellshadow (Shadow+Arcane) and now correctly benefits from Dark Hunt. This change will also make it deal more damage to those who are shadow or arcane vulnerable.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingBlood Hunt also provides 10 Expertise in addition to its previous effects.
  • 2026/07/31outstanding- Hemal Excision curses now last for a maximum of 30 seconds.
  • 2026/07/31outstanding- Hemal Excision can no longer be preserved through map changes.
  • 2026/07/31outstanding- Hemal Excision can no longer be cast on pets, guardians and non player targets to remove their curses.
