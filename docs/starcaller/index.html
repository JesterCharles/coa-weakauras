Import

Packs

Take the all-specs pack unless you only ever play the one spec. They are built from the same source and lay out identically.

How to import — three steps
  1. Copy a string below.
  2. In game, type /wa.
  3. Click Import, paste, confirm.

Re-importing an updated version replaces the old one. If the pack looks unchanged after an update, delete the old group first — WeakAuras keeps what it already has when nothing tells it the pack moved.

Server changes

Ascension changelog — 13 changes not yet folded into a release

Newest published change for this class: 2026/07/31. Changelog read 2026-08-07. Most changelog entries are damage numbers that no WeakAura reacts to. This counts the ones nobody has ruled on yet, which is a different and more honest thing than saying the pack is wrong.

13 entries
  • 2026/07/31outstandingWarden’s Blade now has a new effect for Aspect of the Goddess, Elune’s Cradle, which spawns a small radius healing over time circle around the target of the Blade, healing up to 8 allies within every 3 seconds.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingTyrande’s Training moved to level 50 spec passive. Tyrande’s Training also affects Trueshot, and it now provides 25% crit chance and crit damage against burning targets, up from 10%.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingTouch of Moonlight now properly scales with healing power.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingThe level 30/40/50 Warden vertical line talents have been shifted in position.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingSentinel level 20 passive now gives 10% INT -> Ranged Crit Rating conversion.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingNew talent in the old place of Tyrande’s Training, gives your Starfire Shot a Lunar Eclipse effect, giving it a damage over time Burn effect that also causes the target to be unable to stealth or invis.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingMana scaling of Starfire Shot reduced slightly.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingLunar Lance (and its accompanying DoT if you take the Moonlit Lancer talent), are now Spellfire damage instead of Arcane. This allows the DoT to trigger Tyrande’s Training. It also means that this DoT deals more damage to both Arcane Vulnerable OR Fire Vulnerable targets.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingLunar Combatant now gives you increased intellect based on your strength and increased weapon damage based on your spell power
  • 2026/07/31outstandingCelestial Glaives and Lunar Combatants effects have been combined into the Celestial Glaives node and Lunar Combatant has been reworked
  • 2026/07/31outstandingCelestial Cleave can now be cast without a target, has 1 yd increased range and a wider cone of hit. Additionally, fixed issues with its targeting cone so that it could now correctly hit.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingCan now wear daggers.
  • 2026/07/31outstanding- The level 50 passive for Moon Guard has been merged into the level 30 passive. The new level 50 passive reads: “You are protected by the Light of Elune, you absorb 10% of damage you take into mana from attacks that deal more than 20% of your maximum health.”
