Ascension changelog — 12 changes not yet folded into a release

Newest published change for this class: 2026/07/31. Changelog read 2026-08-07. Most changelog entries are damage numbers that no WeakAura reacts to. This counts the ones nobody has ruled on yet, which is a different and more honest thing than saying the pack is wrong.

  • 2026/07/31outstandingVows now have a reduced global cooldown.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingVows are now on the stance bar.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingSun Stride description now reflects its effect more accurately. Sun Stride now also frees you from slows and roots.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingRadiant Halberds effect has been merged with Champion’s Arrival
  • 2026/07/31outstandingLuminary Mendicant now additionally causes your Horusath Blast to silence the target.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingJustice now deals 50% Weapon Damage up from 40%.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingFixed an issue where Adjudication would trigger a Rank 1 Dawnsear instead of the highest known rank.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingAncient Rites now grants you 150/300/450% of your intellect as mana, in addition to its previous effects.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingAll Gavel Spells now deal 100% weapon damage up from 80%.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingAdjudicator now additionally causes your Justicar’s Wrath to deal 50% more damage if it strikes only a single target.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingAdjudication was also renamed to Herald of Dawn to not be confused with the Adjudicator talent in Seraphim.
  • 2026/07/31outstandingA new capstone has been added to Valkyrie: Sunstorm.

Import

Three steps

  1. Copy a string below.
  2. In game, type /wa.
  3. Click Import, paste, confirm.

Re-importing an updated version replaces the old one. If the pack looks unchanged after an update, delete the old group first — WeakAuras keeps what it already has when nothing tells it the pack moved.
